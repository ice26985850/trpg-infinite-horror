II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

The Reincarnators (輪迴者), as provisional teachers / intern heroes at U.A. High School, must protect students during the League of Villains' assault on the USJ and help the pro heroes repel Shigaraki Tomura and his bio-weapon, the Nomu.

Full Plot Background

In a world where over 80% of the population possesses superpowers — "Quirks (個性)" — U.A. High is the top academy training future pro heroes. One day, Class 1-A arrives at the USJ (Unforeseen Simulation Joint) for disaster-rescue training.

But within ten minutes, disaster truly strikes. An organization calling itself the "League of Villains" invades the USJ through a portal — Kurogiri's Quirk. Their goal is clear: kill All Might (歐爾麥特), the Symbol of Peace. For this they brought a bio-weapon built to counter All Might — the Nomu, a monster with "Shock Absorption" and "Super-Regeneration."

The Reincarnators enter this world as "U.A. provisional teachers" or "intern heroes." After Kurogiri scatters them across disaster zones, they must protect separated students, repel League commanders, and ultimately return to the Central Plaza to support All Might against the Nomu.

The unique challenge of this script: protection takes priority over combat. The Reincarnators' main task is not to eliminate every enemy, but to ensure student safety — meaning they must sometimes abandon favorable combat positions to rescue trapped students.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: early USJ assembly is a guidance scene; mid-script rescue across disaster zones is free exploration (choose the flood / fire / ruins zone first); the late Central Plaza showdown is scene-by-scene progression.

World Special Rules

  • Quirk (個性) System: In this world over 80% are born with superpowers — "Quirks". Reincarnators may use a Quirk via:
  • The Reincarnator gains a temporary Quirk (random or GM-assigned, see Quirk table). Using a Quirk requires an Intellect (INT) check, DC2. Success activates normally; failure means the Quirk cannot be used and enters a 1-round cooldown.
  • Quirk fatigue: after continuous use (over 2 times per battle), each use's Intellect check DC +1.
  • The Reincarnator's Quirk is world-limited; it vanishes after return (unless redeemed at the Main God).
  • Temporary Quirk Table (1d6):
  • Shockwave: release a shockwave from the palm, 1d8 blunt damage, range 20 ft. On the 3rd consecutive use, a Stamina (STA) check, DC2, failure causes hand pain (attack DP −1 for 2 rounds).
  • Hardening: local body hardening, Defense +2, unarmed attack becomes 1d6 blunt. Lasts 3 rounds, then 2-round cooldown.
  • Extend: limbs stretch to 3× length, melee range +2, Agility-related checks DP +1. Muscle fatigue after over 3 rounds.
  • Electrify: release current from fingertips, 1d6 electric damage, target's next-round action DP −1 (paralysis). Usable 3× per battle.
  • Speed: double movement speed, Initiative +3, lasts 2 rounds. 4-round cooldown.
  • Float: hover in air (max 15 ft), lasts 2 rounds. 3-round cooldown.
  • All Might Time Limit: The Symbol of Peace can only hold muscle form ~30 min (counted from his USJ arrival) due to old injury. After 30 min, muscle form vanishes; All Might becomes weakened (all attributes −3, cannot fight).
  • Teacher-Student Morale: Each student successfully rescued grants all Reincarnators and NPCs +1 action DP (cap +3). Each student gravely wounded or dead: DP −1 (floor −3). Rescued students actively assist once safe (can join as light NPCs — see NPC Roster).

X. Appendix

  • Maps: See maps/ for MHA_WM-01.svg, MHA_EM-01.svg, MHA_BM-01.svg, MHA_BM-02.svg
  • Special Rules Supplement: Quirk system, fatigue rules, All Might time limit, teacher-student morale rules (see "V. World Setting → World Special Rules")
  • GM Memo:
  • Common Blockage 1: Players don't know how to hurt the Nomu. Solution — have Midoriya (high analysis) observe and suggest "Shock Absorption only absorbs impact — try piercing or ice"; or have Aizawa erase the Nomu's Quirk after surgery (key turning point).
  • Common Blockage 2: Players want to solo Shigaraki, ignoring student protection. Solution — make the Main God watch vibrate a reminder "Students protected: X/5" (quantify the goal); or distant student screams remind them.
  • Common Blockage 3: Party split across zones, hard for GM to manage. Solution — use montage narration (switch scenes every 2–3 rounds per zone); or keep Kurogiri's portal open (allow cross-zone support).
  • Difficulty Up: Nomu gains "Impact Reflection" (reflects half damage on physical hit); add 1–2 League commanders per zone; Ruins explosive countdown shortened from 5 to 3 rounds.
  • Difficulty Down: Nomu regeneration drops to 2 HP/round; Flood-zone commanders −1; All Might time limit extended to 45 min.
