II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

A remote mountain village, The Forsaken Village (外場村). Summer — a strange European-style mansion moves in, bringing new neighbors: the Kirishiki family. After that, villagers die one after another — then "revive." The Reincarnators (輪迴者), as outside visitors / investigators, must uncover the truth before the village is fully overrun.

V. World Setting

Timeline

Modern Japan, midsummer. The 10th day since the Kirishiki family moved in. The village has 15 deaths, 8 of whom have already "revived" as Shiki.

Key Locations

  • Village Office: Information center; household registry tracks the death pattern
  • Ozaki Hospital: The only medical facility; Dr. Toshio Ozaki conducts secret autopsies
  • Kirishiki Mansion: A European-style manor on the hill; the Shiki's nest
  • Temple: Priest Seishin Muroi's residence; where villagers gather
  • Out-of-Village Bus Stop: The only external transport; the evacuation point

World Special Rules

  • Shiki Transformation: A villager drained of blood by a Shiki revives as a Shiki after 3 days. Shiki retain past memories but obey superiors absolutely.
  • Shiki Weakness: Sunlight (direct 1 round = instant death), wooden stake through the heart (Agility + Melee DC3).
  • Werewolf (人狼): The Kirishiki family's high-ranking servant; can move by day, must be beheaded to kill.
  • Villager Trust: Villager attitude shifts from suspicion → trust → dependence, affecting available supplies and assistance.

Main God Restrictions

  • Do not expose the existence of the Main God Space to villagers
  • No futuristic-tech weapons in this world
