II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

The Reincarnators (輪迴者), serving as temporary professors or prefects at Hogwarts, must protect the school and stop a thief from breaching the forbidden area amid the temptations and dangers surrounding the Philosopher's Stone.

Full Plot Background

In 1991, at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, a legendary artifact known as the Philosopher's Stone is hidden deep beneath the school — shielded layer upon layer by magical defenses jointly devised by several professors. However, the remnant soul of the dark wizard Voldemort clings to the seemingly timid Professor Quirrell, attempting to pass through these defenses and steal the Stone.

The Lord God arranges for the Reincarnators to act as "exchange scholars" or "security advisors dispatched by the Ministry of Magic" at Hogwarts. Their task: while the school runs normally, investigate suspicious movements around the forbidden area and ensure the Philosopher's Stone is not stolen. Dumbledore leaves a cryptic clue — "When the three-headed dog falls asleep, the true enemy is close at hand."

The challenge of this scenario lies in the fact that the wizarding world may be entirely foreign to the Reincarnators. Wand use, the spell system, and dealing with magical creatures — everything is new. At the same time, the players must not overly interfere with Harry's trio's adventure, because "the course of destiny" requires them to grow on their own.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: Opening in the Hogwarts Great Hall → free exploration of the castle's floors → finale: the guardians' battle in the underground chamber

World Special Rules

  • Wand Use: Non-wizard characters must make an Intelligence (INT) + Magic check to cast spells. Basic spells Difficulty (DC) 1–2, advanced spells Difficulty (DC) 3–4. Failure may trigger an accidental magical effect.

  • Spell Learning: Basic spells (Levitation, Light, Disarming) can be learned in the library — each attempt costs 1 hour and an Intelligence (INT) check, Difficulty (DC) 2.

  • Hogwarts Rules: Movement around campus is forbidden after 9 PM (violation deducts House points or incurs detention). Each time caught, professor favorability toward the players -1.

  • Portrait Intelligence Network: Portraits in the castle can be spoken with for information (Presence (PRE) + Expression check, Difficulty (DC) 1), but they also report suspicious behavior to the professors.

  • The Mirror of Erised: The final defense of the underground chamber. To obtain the Stone, one must be "one who wishes to find it but not use it" — pure intent to guard it.

Lord God Restrictions

  • "Do not actively harm or kidnap Hogwarts students (self-defense excepted)."

  • "If obvious supernatural abilities (outside the magic system) are used before students, school-wide favorability -2."

VII. In-Depth NPC Design Guide

Albus Dumbledore — Headmaster of Hogwarts

Dimension Design Guide
Core Trait Wise and kind, fond of hinting through riddles; calm on the surface yet secretly in control of everything.
Interaction Tier Surface: opening-feast speech. Mid: if players show magical talent or the will to protect students, he will speak privately in his office and offer key hints.
Key Trigger "Curious — I seem to have forgotten to close the door to the forbidden area. I do hope no one goes in." — this is a hint.
Combat Role The strongest wizard of this world — will not fight directly, but can provide magical support at key moments (remotely dispel one threat).

Rubeus Hagrid — Gamekeeper

Dimension Design Guide
Core Trait Kind-hearted but loose-tongued, with an abnormal fondness for dangerous magical creatures.
Interaction Tier Mid — a source of leaked information. Drinking and chatting with Hagrid may accidentally yield important intel (e.g., the three-headed dog's weakness is music).
Key Trigger If players mention the "Philosopher's Stone," Hagrid panics: "Yer shouldn't know about that!" (then accidentally reveals more).

Professor Quirinus Quirrell — The Timid-Seeming Mastermind

Dimension Design Guide
Core Trait Stutters, neurotic — a timid professor by day, Voldemort's servant by night. Voldemort's face is hidden beneath his turban.
Interaction Tier Surface: timid professor. Deep (after reveal): cold servant of the dark wizard.
Clues Smell of garlic (claims it wards off vampires), loitering near the forbidden area, a suspicious alibi during the Halloween Troll rampage.
