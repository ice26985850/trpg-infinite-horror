II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

The Reincarnators (輪迴者), as temporary investigators for the Phantomhive house, hunt the truth behind the Jack the Ripper serial killings in Victorian London.

Full Plot Background

London, 1888, the Victorian era. In the back alleys of Whitechapel, several prostitutes are brutally murdered — the public calls the killer "Jack the Ripper." Scotland Yard is helpless; the Queen commissions the Phantomhive house to investigate. But Ciel Phantomhive — the 13-year-old Earl — and his flawless butler Sebastian already suspect this is no ordinary serial case: the victims' uteruses were precisely removed, the work of no human hand. The truth is worse than Jack — reapers, fallen angels, and a mad noble's conspiracy entwine in London's fog.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: opens at Phantomhive Manor → free investigation on London's streets → final confrontation.

World Special Rules

  • Victorian etiquette: misbehavior at noble events (wrong attire, rude words) gets you ejected or suspected. Presence (PRE) + Etiquette checks decide social success.
  • Eye of the Reaper: some NPCs (Sebastian, Grell) see humans' "death list" — a person's remaining lifespan. If asked, Sebastian answers: "Yours is strange — I cannot see the end" (hinting at the Lord God's intervention).
  • Demon's contract: the Phantomhive–Sebastian contract is the world's core — Sebastian is a demon who may devour Ciel's soul after revenge is done. Sebastian cannot disobey Ciel's orders.
  • Scotland Yard interference: too-flashy investigation brings Scotland Yard onto the players themselves — more difficulty and time pressure.

Lord God's Restrictions

  • Do not reveal the Lord God Space to in-world characters.
  • Victorian-era weapons (pistols, blades) are allowed, but modern automatic weapons are forbidden.
  • Using supernatural abilities in public causes panic and Scotland Yard pursuit.
