II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

The Reincarnators (輪迴者) are sent to the legendary Shinsen Island — a paradise said to hold an elixir of eternal life. But the island's true rulers are the half-human, half-divine Tensen (天仙), and the truth behind the "elixir of immortality" is far darker than anyone imagined.

Full Plot Background

In the late Edo period, Shogun Tokugawa Ienari became obsessed with the art of immortality. Legend spoke of a "Shinsen Island" in the southern seas, where a divine medicine granting eternal life grew. The Shogunate dispatched countless expeditions — none ever returned. The latest plan is more extreme still: send condemned criminals as "offerings," supervised by executioners. If a criminal brings back the elixir of immortality, they earn a full pardon.

The Reincarnators arrive in this world as either condemned criminals or executioners. Alongside the source characters — Gabimaru (a ninja on death row) and Yamada Asaemon Sagiri (a female executioner) — they land on Shinsen Island. Once ashore, they quickly discover: this is no paradise, but a living hell.

The island teems with genetically mutated Youma (妖物). Its rulers are seven demigods known as the Tensen (天仙) — humans who reached the island in an earlier age and, through prolonged contact with the island's mysterious plants, became undead monsters. The Tensen leader, Rien (蓮), has lived for centuries and is searching for the perfect vessel to transfer her soul. The truth behind the so-called "elixir of immortality" is the mutant plant called the Dan (丹) that transforms humans into Tensen. Once consumed, one becomes either a Youma or a Tensen — and in either case, can no longer be called "human."

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: free exploration by day (finding water, food, dojos, and meeting criminals and executioners), shifting to scene-by-scene progression at night or in specific zones (encountering Youma, exploring dojos, the Tensen Palace).

World Special Rules

  • Tao (道) System: Shinsen Island harbors a life-energy called the "Tao." After learning to sense it (Perception check, DC 3, 4 hours of meditation), the Reincarnator may use attribute enhancement — choose Strength (STR), Agility (DEX), or Endurance (STA); that attribute's check dice pool (DP) +2, lasting until combat ends or the scene changes. After each Tao enhancement, rest 1 hour before reuse.
  • Advanced Tao (after exploring multiple dojos): enhance two attributes at once (DP +1 each); or boost a single attribute DP +3 but then make an Endurance (STA) check, DC 2, failure grants 1 level of Fatigue.
  • Medicinal Plants: Island plants have strong medicinal or toxic properties. Intellect (INT) + Medicine check, DC 2 identifies plant uses. Findable: Bloodwort (restores 1d6 HP), Antidote Mushroom (clears poisoned state), Hallucinogen Mushroom (on ingestion, Intellect (INT) check, DC 3, failure → 2 hours of hallucinations), Dan Flower (extremely dangerous — contact requires Endurance (STA) check, DC 3, failure begins Youma transformation).
  • Wave (Nami, 波浪): Every ~36 hours Shinsen Island undergoes a "Wave" — the surrounding seawater mysteriously recedes, exposing submerged areas. During a Wave: new dojos and paths appear; some Youma wash ashore (+50% encounter chance); the sky shows violet aurora. The Wave lasts 2 hours, then water returns (those who fail to retreat must make a Swim check — Endurance (STA) + Athletics, DC 3).
  • Youma Transformation: Prolonged exposure to Dan spores turns humans into Youma. Each 4+ hours spent in spore-dense zones (e.g., deep Youma Forest) requires one Endurance (STA) check (DC escalating 1→4). Failure costs 1d4 Sanity; losing over half your Sanity triggers Youma-ification symptoms (body mutates, action DP −2).

X. Appendix

  • Maps: See maps/ for JKR_WM-01.svg, JKR_EM-01.svg, JKR_BM-01.svg, JKR_BM-02.svg
  • Special Rules Supplement: Tao system, medicinal plant rules, Wave mechanic, Youma transformation rules (see "V. World Setting → World Special Rules")
  • GM Memo:
  • Common Blockage 1: Players cannot pass the Perception check to awaken Tao. Solution — allow Gene Lock DP bonus; or have the Dojo Master offer "enlightenment tea" (pun — temporarily +1 Perception but −1 Endurance), lowering DC to 2.
  • Common Blockage 2: Players want to consume Dan for power. Solution — have Gabimaru or Azadou show what becomes of Dan consumers (utter loss of self, becoming Youma or Tensen puppets); or show petrified warriors in the palace.
  • Common Blockage 3: Rien too strong to defeat. Solution — emphasize the dialogue route (Rien has in fact been lonely 400 years, harbors a twisted longing for "true humans"); if S-03 defeated other Tensen, Rien weakens from lost companions (all check DCs −1).
  • Difficulty Up: increase Wave frequency (every 24h instead of 36h); double Tensen patrol count; dojo hidden trials (Master challenges player on training failure).
  • Difficulty Down: reduce Youma count; dojo Perception DC lowered to 2; Waves bring no extra Youma; Gabimaru and Sagiri assist more frequently.
  • Unconventional Player Actions: If players try to "sail away from Shinsen Island" — warn that giant Youma lurk in the waves (implying the island's barrier prevents leaving), and the Shogunate executes escaped criminals at sea. If players "dig up the Dan's roots" — discover a giant Core Entity (母體) plant root system piercing the whole island underground (a high-risk, high-reward route — destroying the Core weakens all Tensen but triggers island-wide tremors).
  • Inspiration: Jigokuraku (Hell's Paradise) manga vols. 1–13, anime season 1
