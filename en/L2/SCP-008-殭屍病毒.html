II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

An SCP-008 viral aerosol leaks; Site-02 bio-site outbreaks with zombie plague. The Reincarnators (輪迴者) must reach the central lab and initiate the sterilization protocol within 12 hours.

Full Plot Background

The present day. SCP Foundation bio-research Site-02 studies SCP-008 — an extremely dangerous prion-like pathogen, transmissible via fluid contact or aerosol. The infected transform within 3 hours into mindless, hyper-aggressive "zombies," spreading the virus through bodily fluids.

A dismissed researcher (a Chaos Insurgency mole) deliberately sabotaged SCP-008's freezer unit before evacuating. The virus spread as aerosol through the ventilation system to the entire east and central wings. The site triggered emergency lockdown — but ~60% of personnel were already exposed.

The Reincarnators are urgently conscripted by the Foundation as "external biochemical-response consultants" (in truth, forcibly teleported by the Main God). They awaken in the west-wing quarantine zone; their watches read: "Reach the central lab, initiate the sterilization protocol. Survive."

Challenges: 1. Zombie horde — infected count grows exponentially with time (initial 20 → over 150 after 12 hours) 2. Time pressure — the sterilization protocol needs manual initiation, and key systems (ventilation lockdown, lab power) must be functional before then 3. Evolved infected — some develop special abilities (speed / armored / spitter types)

Plot Structure Type

Act-by-Act: early free exploration, mid-to-late scene-by-scene progression.

World Special Rules

  • SCP-008 Infection Mechanism: Contact with infected bodily fluid → Stamina check (DC3), failure infects. Incubation 1d6 hours (asymptomatic but contagious), then transforms into an infected (during which an extra Stamina check per hour, DC +1 each).
  • Sterilization Protocol: After start, 5 min releases 1200°C high-temp sterilization gas site-wide. All creatures (including Reincarnators) must enter designated safe pods before sterilization begins — the central lab has safe pods (capacity 6).
  • Infected Classification: ① Normal Infected (★) slow but numerous; ② Evolved Infected (★★) randomly generated — Speed type (Agility 6, move ×3), Armored type (base Defense +4 physical resist), Spitter type (ranged acid attack).
  • Resource Scarcity: Weapons and ammo limited. Encourage using environmental items (fire extinguishers, fire axes, chemicals) and stealth tactics (infected perceive within 30 ft, deaf but sensitive to motion and smell).

Main God Restrictions

  • Do not reveal the existence of the Main God Space to plot characters
  • This world is modern realistic tech (SCP Foundation tech = modern +10 years — energy weapons, biochemical protection available but rare)
  • No outside contact during site lockdown — all comms electromagnetically shielded
  • Attempting to flee the site without initiating sterilization → external auto-defense opens fire (treated as escaped infection threat)
