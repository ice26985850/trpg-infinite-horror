II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

Victorian London — the Reincarnators (輪迴者), as demon hunters, investigate the demon behind a string of serial killings.

Full Plot Background

London, 1889, the Victorian era. A serial-killer case — bodies carved with symbols, drained of blood, hearts arranged in a pentagram. The secret order "the Demon Hunters' Society" knows the truth: a demon has awakened in London's shadows. From low sewer demons, to a possessed noble MP, up to a Demon Duke.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid.
