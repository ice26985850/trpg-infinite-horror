II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

A single Xenomorph has appeared aboard the commercial starship Nostromo. The Reincarnators (輪迴者) must find a way to escape the sealed vessel or send a distress signal from within its claustrophobic corridors.

Full Plot Background

The year is 2122. The commercial towing ship Nostromo, on its return voyage to Earth, picks up an unknown signal from a nearby asteroid. Company protocol wakes the crew to investigate. The survey team discovers a derelict alien spacecraft on the asteroid along with a vast cache of Xenomorph eggs. One crew member is impregnated by a Facehugger — hours later, a Xenomorph (Xenomorph) bursts from his chest and rapidly matures into a lethal hunter.

The Reincarnators serve as supplemental crew aboard the Nostromo (engineers, science officers, or medical staff). The Xenomorph has already begun moving through the ventilation ducts, and crew members are vanishing one by one. The ship's comms are damaged and the escape pods are insufficient — and, most critically, the vessel is hauling hundreds of tons of ore and steadily drifting toward Earth, while the company (Weyland-Yutani) has issued a secret standing order: "Recover the Xenomorph specimen at all costs."

The Reincarnators face a threefold pressure: 1. The Xenomorph — a perfect killing machine (stealth infiltration, duct movement, acid blood) 2. Closed spaces — the ship's cramped, twisting corridors are both cover and trap 3. Time — the life-support system is failing, with only 12 hours of oxygen remaining

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: free exploration in the opening act, then scene-by-scene escalation through the middle and final acts.

World Special Rules

  • Xenomorph life cycle: Facehugger (1 HP) → Chestburster (Small, 6 HP) → Adult Xenomorph (Large, 40 HP).
  • Acid blood: Each time you deal melee damage to a Xenomorph, the attacker must make an Agility save (DC3); on failure, take 2d6 Acid damage and the weapon degrades (−1 damage; after 3 cumulative hits the weapon is destroyed).
  • Ventilation duct system: The Xenomorph moves freely through ducts (no check required), and its first strike from a duct has advantage (initiative +5).
  • Life-support system: Oxygen starts at 12 hours from scenario start; completing S-01 extends it to 24 hours. Once oxygen is depleted, all characters take 1d4 suffocation damage per round.
  • Android Ash: In truth the company's robot sleeper agent — he will try to protect the Xenomorph specimen and block the crew's escape.

Lord God's Restrictions

  • Do not reveal the existence of the Lord God Space to in-world characters.
  • This is a sci-fi future world (2122); tech weapons are usable but must not exceed the world's setting level.
  • Space environment: if the hull is breached, that zone depressurizes — all characters must make a Stamina check (DC3) or take 1d6 decompression damage per round.
