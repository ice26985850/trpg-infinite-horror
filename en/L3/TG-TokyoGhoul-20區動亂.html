二、劇情概要（GM 限定）

One-Line Summary

The Reincarnators (輪迴者) are dropped into Ward 20 as temporary CCG investigators. Aogiri Tree is about to launch an extermination campaign; all-out Ghoul-vs-human conflict is imminent. Amid clashes of kagune and quinque, you must choose—stand with humanity to destroy the Ghouls, or betray the CCG once you learn the truth?

Full Story Background

The world is near-future Tokyo. Human society appears peaceful on the surface, but beneath it lurks a subhuman species that feeds on human flesh—the Ghoul (喰種). Ghouls possess physical prowess far beyond humans and a biological weapon called the "kagune" (赫子), and can survive only on human flesh and coffee. The CCG (Commission of Counter Ghoul / Ghoul Countermeasures Bureau) is the government agency dedicated to fighting Ghouls, wielding weapons called "quinque" (庫因克) forged from Ghoul kagune.

Ward 20 is among Tokyo's most Ghoul-active districts—calm on the surface, but with undercurrents surging below. The Ghoul militant organization "Aogiri Tree" (青桐樹) plans to launch an extermination campaign here, intending to destroy the CCG's Ward 20 branch.

The Reincarnators (輪迴者) enter 48 hours before Aogiri Tree's campaign. The CCG headquarters temporarily assigns them as "Special Investigators" to the Ward 20 branch, to help suppress the coming Ghoul offensive. But as the investigation deepens, players will discover the CCG is not monolithic—some investigators are more terrifying than the Ghouls.

Core Conflict: The Reincarnators (輪迴者) must navigate among three factions—the CCG (human order), Aogiri Tree (Ghoul radicals), and Anteiku (Ghoul moderates / antique coffee shop).

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: Opens with free exploration (Ward 20 street patrol, clue investigation) → mid-game act-by-act progression (Aogiri attack, sewer chase) → finale stronghold battle.

World Special Rules

  • Four Kagune Types: Ghouls divide by kagune (赫子) into four types—Ukaku (羽赫): speed type, ranged kagune shots, Agility +2 but Endurance check DC+1; Koukaku (甲赫): defense type, heavy armored kagune, base defense +3 but initiative −2; Rinkaku (鱗赫): strength type, tentacle kagune, damage +2 but own defense −2; Bikaku (尾赫): balanced type, tail kagune, no bonus or penalty
  • Quinque Weapon System: Anti-Ghoul weapons forged from Ghoul kagune. Deal ×2 damage to Ghoul-type enemies. Quinque tiers: C-tier (base attack DP+1, initial issue), B-tier (damage +1d6, needs CCG trust 30), A-tier (attack DP+3 and damage ×1.5, needs CCG trust 60 or promotion after killing 10 Ghouls)
  • RC-Cell Detection Gate: At all CCG facility entrances. Triggers alarm when a Ghoul passes (unless Composure + Conceal check DC 4 to suppress RC-cell activity). Reincarnators (輪迴者) can be flagged "non-human" by the gate (if carrying live Ghoul tissue or kagune samples, need DC 3 conceal check)
  • Ghoul Regeneration: All Ghouls auto-recover 1d6 HP at the start of each round, unless hit by quinque or RC-cell inhibitor (the round they are hit by quinque does not trigger regeneration)
  • Cannibal Instinct: When a Ghoul's HP drops below 30%, it enters a berserk state (kagune attack DP+2, base defense −2, cannot rationally negotiate). If a berserk Ghoul kills its target, it feeds on the spot and recovers 2d8 HP
