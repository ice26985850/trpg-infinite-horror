二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-line Summary

Reincarnators (RC) No-Clip into Backrooms Level 94 — a kingdom made of animation, with a 12-hour day/night cycle. Friendly cartoon villagers help by day, but the kingdom's guards turn hostile at night. Finally they must face the realm's ruler — the Cartoon King.

Full Story Background

Level 94 is a unique Backrooms level — it looks like 1930s Rubber Hose Animation. Buildings, trees, even the sky are drawn in ink. This level follows a strict day/night cycle: alternating every 12 hours. By day, cartoon villagers are friendly and helpful, offering intel and aid; by night, the kingdom's guards turn hostile, hunting any non-cartoon-style existence.

After No-Clipping in, the Reincarnators find themselves in a small safe cabin — a shelter left by some Backrooms traveler. A diary inside reveals that Level 94 is ruled by the "Cartoon King" — a powerful entity able to manipulate cartoon style and dimensional power. He treats Level 94 as his kingdom and tolerates no "non-cartoon" presence threatening his rule.

There is only one way to leave Level 94: defeat the Cartoon King and pass through the portal he guards. But the Cartoon King's power shifts with the day/night cycle — by day he is dormant (weaker but more guards), by night he is fully awake (strongest but fewer guards).

Core conflict: strategically choose the attack timing (day assault means more guards but weak Boss; night means strong Boss but fewer guards), collect cartoon items to weaken the Boss, and survive the day/night cycle.

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: freely explore and collect items in the cartoon kingdom by day → defend or raid by night → finally face the Cartoon King in the throne room.

World Special Rules

  • Day/Night Cycle: Level 94 alternates day and night every 12 hours. Day: cartoon villagers friendly, Kingdom Guards (day) patrol only around the castle, Cartoon King dormant deep in the castle (can be attacked but won't strike first). Night: cartoon villagers hide indoors, Kingdom Guards (night) fully hostile and patrol the whole kingdom, Cartoon King awakens and actively patrols within the castle.
  • Cartoon Physics: Level 94 follows cartoon-world physics — characters can briefly float (jump height ×2), take no blood damage but show comic "stars/birds" effects, environmental objects (pianos, safes) may randomly drop (roll per scene — triggers on 1d6=6).
  • Ink Contamination: After being hit by the Cartoon King's ink magic, make END DC3; failure grants the "Ink Contamination" status — appearance turns black-and-white cartoon style, all social checks DC+2 (non-cartoon villagers fear you), lasts until using the Magic Eraser or natural dissipation by day.
