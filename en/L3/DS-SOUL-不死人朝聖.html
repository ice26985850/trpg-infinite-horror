二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-line Summary

Reincarnators (RC) awaken as Undead in the Northern Undead Asylum and set foot on the ancient prophesied pilgrimage — crossing the kingdom of Lordran to ring the Awakening Bell hidden deep in the Undead Church.

Full Story Background

In the beginning, the world was shrouded in grey fog — neither light nor dark. Until the first flame appeared, bringing disparity — light and dark, life and death. Four great beings — the first Flame inheritors — took the Lord Souls from the fire and established the age of the gods.

But the flame will eventually fade. As the flame wanes, the Undead curse spreads — the cursed do not truly die, but revive endlessly by the bonfire, losing a part of their Humanity with each death, until they become mindless Hollows.

The prophecy says the chosen Undead will ring two Awakening Bells — one high in the church, one deep underground — to awaken the ancient Flame inheritors, reconnecting them to the First Flame.

The Reincarnators are freed from the Northern Undead Asylum cell by Oscar. Oscar gives the Reincarnators a key, then collapses — he was grievously wounded by the Asylum Demon. The Reincarnators must fight out of the asylum and set on the pilgrimage to Lordran: cross the Undead Burg, defeat the Gargoyles guarding the church, and ring the first Awakening Bell — then learn the second bell lies deeper.

Story Structure Type

Act-by-act: Northern Undead Asylum escape → arrive at Firelink Shrine → cross Undead Burg → Gargoyle battle at Undead Church → ring the Awakening Bell.

World Special Rules

  • Undead Curse: After death, Reincarnators revive at the nearest bonfire — but each revival consumes 1 Humanity. At 0 Humanity, become Hollow: rotting appearance, NPCs refuse to talk, trade prices doubled. Humanity restored via "Humanity items" (gain 1 per Boss defeated). 5 cumulative deaths = complete Hollowification — quest failure.
  • Bonfire: A sacred rest point. At a bonfire you can: restore HP and Estus, use Souls to upgrade Attributes, refill Estus charges. Discovering a new bonfire auto-becomes the new revival point.
  • Estus Flask: Refilled each bonfire rest. Start with 3 flasks — each restores 2d6+2 HP. More flasks via strengthening the bonfire by spending Humanity (each Humanity +1 flask cap — max 10).
  • Souls: Experience/currency gained from defeating enemies. Use Souls at bonfires to upgrade (same system as ER). On death lose all unspent Souls — must return to the death location to retrieve; dying again loses them permanently.
