二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Sentence Summary

The Reincarnators (RC) are dispatched to Central City as temporary members of the State Military to investigate a series of disappearances among high-ranking officers. At the end of their investigation, they uncover the conspiracy of the Homunculi — the Nationwide Transmutation Circle. Stop it, or all of Amestris will be turned into a Philosopher's Stone.

Full Plot Background

The story takes place in Amestris, a militaristic nation where alchemy flourishes. This country is built on alchemy as its technological foundation, and the military maintains the institution of "State Alchemists" — alchemists who pass the examination are granted a silver pocket watch and enjoy military rank and privileges.

Yet the foundation of this nation rests upon a gigantic lie: the Nationwide Transmutation Circle — a colossal transmutation array that costs the souls of the entire population. The mastermind behind it is "Father" and the seven Homunculi he created — immortal beings named after the Seven Deadly Sins.

The Reincarnators (RC) enter at a point 10 days before the Nationwide Transmutation Circle activates. Edward and Alphonse Elric are investigating the Homunculi and the truth of the Philosopher's Stone in Central City. Colonel Roy Mustang (the Flame Alchemist) is secretly mobilizing loyal troops within the military. The Homunculus army pushes the plan forward at each node.

Core Conflict: Within 10 days, the Reincarnators must investigate the location of the Homunculi's lair (beneath Laboratory 5), stop at least one Homunculus from completing a node of the Nationwide Transmutation Circle, and destroy the corresponding Philosopher's Stone.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: Opening free investigation (Central Military Command, Central City streets, the periphery of Laboratory 5) → Mid-game faction assembly (linking up with Mustang or the Elric brothers) → Final act preventing the Nationwide Transmutation Circle.

World Special Rules

  • Alchemy System: Using alchemy requires first "Comprehend → Decompose → Reconstruct." Check format: INT+Science (Alchemy), Difficulty (DC) 3. On success can alter the shape and nature of matter. The Principle of Equivalent Exchange — you must provide materials equal in quantity to the target substance (e.g., to build a stone wall you need an equal amount of stone present on site). The Philosopher's Stone can perform transmutations directly, ignoring Equivalent Exchange.
  • Philosopher's Stone Usage Rules: Consume 1 Small Philosopher's Stone Shard → single alchemy check DP+3 and no materials needed. Consume 1 Medium Philosopher's Stone Shard → perform one healing transmutation (restore 3d8+5 HP) or destructive transmutation (deal 3d6 irreducible damage to a Homunculus). Large Philosopher's Stone Shard → obtained only at the final node, redeemable for special enhancements after returning to the Main God (MG) Space.
  • Homunculus Physiological Traits: All Homunculi share traits — Regeneration (1d6 HP per round), weakness is the Philosopher's Stone core (destroying the core means permanent death, precise attack DP-4), immune to poison and disease. Each Homunculus has unique skills (see enemy roster).
  • Alchemy Backlash: Triggered when an alchemy check is a critical failure (no successes in the DP and at least one 1) — the backlash effect depends on the transmutation target (usually 1d6 damage or material collapse causing environmental hazard).

Main God (MG) Restrictions

  • "Prohibited from revealing the existence of the Main God Space to plot characters."
  • "Supernatural abilities that conflict with the alchemy system (such as Eastern Taoist arts, Western magic) are forbidden in this world. Enhancement-type abilities (Gene Lock) may be used normally."
  • "If you actively assist the Homunculi in completing any step of the Nationwide Transmutation Circle → immediate mission failure and annihilation."
  • "The State Alchemist qualification exam is an independent system; Reincarnators cannot obtain formal qualification in the short term (the silver pocket watch can only be obtained as a keepsake version via S-03)."

十、Appendix

  • Maps: Please refer to FMAB_WM-01.svg, FMAB_EM-01.svg, FMAB_BM-01.svg, FMAB_BM-02.svg in the maps/ directory
  • Special Rules Supplement: Alchemy System, Philosopher's Stone Usage Rules, Homunculus Physiological Traits, Alchemy Backlash (see "五、World Setting → World Special Rules")
  • GM Memo:
  • Common Stuck Point 1: Players may fail to grasp the scale of the Nationwide Transmutation Circle. Solution — have Mustang unfold the national map during the briefing, mark all node locations in red, creating visual impact
  • Common Stuck Point 2: Players get lost in Laboratory 5. Solution — provide a findable "facility map" document, or let the alchemy communication array capture underground energy flow as guidance
  • Difficulty Up: Have President King Bradley appear in the final battle under the pretext of "inspection" — the addition of Wrath (★★★★★) greatly raises difficulty. Recommended only for teams seeking the ultimate challenge. Alternatively, set Alphonse's seizure as inevitably successful → the brothers cannot join the final battle
  • Difficulty Down: Lower the Philosopher's Stone core's base defense (16→12) and HP (30→20), have Gluttony slip on its own vomit at round 3 (humorous relief to lower tension), increase the number of surviving prisoners (provides more combat support)
  • Player Unconventional Actions: If players try to "make their own Philosopher's Stone" → seriously warn that the process requires a large number of living human souls (essentially mass murder), not recommended and may cause other NPCs to become permanently hostile. If players try to "assassinate President King Bradley" → he is the Homunculus Wrath (★★★★★) — say humorously: "Please make sure your will is written first."
  • Mustang's Rainy-Day Limit: The Flame Alchemist cannot use flame alchemy in the rain (the lighter won't light). During the final battle the GM may optionally make the weather rainy — limiting Mustang's combat power as a difficulty adjustment
  • Reference Material: Fullmetal Alchemist anime Season 1, manga volumes 1–27 (complete edition)
