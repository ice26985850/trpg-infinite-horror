二、Story Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

The legendary Hunter-only game "Greed Island" — equal parts paradise and hell. Reincarnators (轮回者) are dropped into an arena where Nen (念能力) users slaughter one another; they must collect the required cards, evade the Bomber's hunt, and find the only way out of the game.

Full Story Background

The time period is unspecified (the Hunter world). Greed Island is a materialized game world created by the Nen user "Ging Freecss" together with his companions, located on a remote island in the real world. Entrants are teleported in via a dedicated console (JoyStation) using Nen. Everything inside the game — items, monsters, NPCs — is constructed from Nen.

Game rules: 1. Collect 100 specified spell cards to clear the game; a clear winner may take 3 cards back to the real world. 2. Players may steal cards from one another (using the "Peep" or "Rob" spell cards). 3. Death ends the game (and means real-world death).

The Reincarnators enter roughly two weeks after Gon and Killua enter the game. By now the Bomber Genthru has formed an alliance and begun systematically hunting other players. Biscuit has already started training Gon and Killua at Antokiba City.

The core challenges of this script: - Card Collection: Understand the game rules and spell system. - Nen Confrontation: Against enemies of different Nen categories, you must grasp the six-school counter relationships. - Resource Management: Spell cards have limited uses and cannot be spent endlessly. - Genthru Threat: The Bomber gang actively hunts players holding rare cards.

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: The first 48 hours after entering the game are free exploration (you may choose to team up with Gon or act alone), after which the story advances scene by scene toward the Bomber's final battle.

五、World Settings

Nen System (World Special Rule I: Nen)

When Reincarnators enter this world, the Lord God forcibly opens their aura nodes (similar to the Hunter world's "Nen initiation"), granting basic Nen usage eligibility. All Reincarnators gain:

The Four Basic Nen Techniques

Nen Technique Effect
Ten (纏) Constant. Grants base Nen defense bonus successes equal to the Resolve (決心) attribute (stacks with Ken). No check required
Zetsu (絕) Technique to suppress aura. Agility + Stealth checks gain DP+3. Requires 1 standard action to activate, lasts the scene
Ren (練) Release large amounts of aura. Attack checks DP+2, but own base defense -2 (defense weakens as aura is externalized). Lasts 1 scene
Hatsu (發) Personal finisher. Each Reincarnator may develop one Hatsu in a Nen category. See Nen-category rules below

Nen Defense — Ken (堅)

Field Content
Mechanism As a reaction, spend 1 Nen pool point to gain defense bonus successes equal to Melee skill level, lasting 1 round
Notes When stacked with Ten, advanced Nen users' total defense bonus successes can reach 5–7, greatly reducing the risk of instant death

Nen Pool

Field Content
Maximum Resolve × 2 + 10
Recovery Recover 3 points at end of each scene; using Zetsu meditation (Resolve check, DC 2, takes 10 minutes) recovers Resolve-attribute points
Cost Ken: 1 point/use; Hatsu: by skill; Nen attack enhancement: 1 point per +2 DP

Six Nen Categories

Upon entering the game, the GM must perform a Water Divination check (Power + Perception check, DC 2) to determine each Reincarnator's Nen category. Different categories grant DP bonuses in their domain:

Nen Category Trait Skill DP Bonus
Enhancement (強化系) Enhance self or objects Power-related checks DP+2, melee damage +3
Transmutation (變化系) Change aura's nature Agility-related checks DP+2, may convert aura to elemental attributes
Conjuration (具現化系) Materialize aura into objects Craft-related checks DP+2, may create items with special effects
Emission (放出系) Shoot aura outward Firearm/ranged attacks DP+2, Nen ball range ×2
Manipulation (操作系) Control objects or creatures Control-related checks DP+2, may control enemies (Intelligence contest)
Specialization (特質系) Unique, unclassifiable abilities Custom special ability (GM approval), all-attribute checks DP+1

Nen Category Counters

Enhancement ──beats──> Transmutation ──beats──> Conjuration ──beats──> Emission ──beats──> Manipulation ──beats──> Specialization ──beats──> Enhancement

The countered side's Nen attack checks DP-2, Nen defense bonus successes -1.

Hatsu (發) — Personal Finisher

Each Reincarnator may design one Hatsu matching their Nen category. Examples: - Enhancement: "Hard" (Ko) — concentrate all aura into the fist, single melee hit damage +5, costs 3 Nen - Transmutation: "Thunder Palm" — convert aura to lightning, attack deals 3d6 lightning damage, costs 2 Nen - Emission: "Nen Ball Barrage" — fire 1d3 Nen balls, each dealing 2d4+2 damage, costs 3 Nen

Spell Card System (World Special Rule II)

Inside Greed Island there are two types of cards: Specified Cards (100 total, numbered 000–099) and Spell Cards (re-obtainable, with per-use limits).

Common Spell Cards

Spell Effect Acquisition Difficulty
Accompany (同行) Teleport instantly to a designated player or NPC DC2, 3 uses per card
Magnetic Force (磁力) Teleport to the last place visited via Accompany DC1, 5 uses per card
Listening (竊聽) Peek at all cards held by a designated player DC2, 2 uses per card
Peep (偷窺) See one card held by a designated player DC0, 10 uses per card
Rob (搶奪) Randomly seize 1 card held by a designated player DC3, 1 use per card
City Flight (城內飛行) Fly within the current town DC1, 5 uses per card
Leave (離開) Teleport to the starting plains DC0, 10 uses per card
Holy Water (聖水) Restore all HP, but usable only once DC2, 1 use per card

Spell cards can be bought from magic shops in each town or earned by completing NPC quests.

How to Obtain Specified Cards

  • Complete NPC quests
  • Defeat specific monsters (refresh once per month)
  • Acquire from other players (trade or rob)

Cardification

Physical items in-game can be turned into collectible cards via the "Book" spell (placed in the card book), and materialized for use via the "Gain" spell.

GM Memo

  • Common Stuck Point 1: Players may not understand the Nen system — fix: Biscuit as training mentor explains the six categories and counters in detail. Also provide a simplified "six-category chart" item in the tutorial scene.
  • Common Stuck Point 2: Genthru's "A Handful of Gunpowder" damage may instakill players — fix: provide intel beforehand (Biscuit can explain Genthru's weakness — Gunpowder needs prep time; break his gesture to interrupt). In the mine battle, provide disarmable detonation points to spread his damage output.
  • Difficulty Up: Genthru final battle HP to 120, Gunpowder damage to 6d6, raise his Nen-pool cap (more Gunpowder uses). Or double mine detonation-point count.
  • Difficulty Down: Genthru HP to 60, Gunpowder to 4d6, Ken defense fully blocks fire damage (default half penetration). Or let Gon-team fight throughout.
  • Player Unconventional Actions: If players try "Accompany Genthru to an unreachable place," remind them Genthru also has spell cards and can counter-teleport. But creative spell tactics (e.g. teleport Genthru to Razor Gym for Razor to handle) should be encouraged with a reasonable success rate.
  • Card Collection Pace: GM must provide card opportunities timely, ensuring Reincarnators collect at least 30 within 84 hr. Suggest ~1 card opportunity per 2 hr game time.
