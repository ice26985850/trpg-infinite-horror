二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Sentence Summary

The Reincarnators (RC), commissioned by the Astral Express crew, travel to the frozen city of Belobog to seal the Fragmentum erosion core, and complete the sealing ritual alongside the Express crew in an apocalyptic environment overrun by Fragmentum creatures.

Full Plot Background

In a distant galaxy lies a miraculous railway spanning the star sea — the Astral Express. Pioneers ride this wondrous train left behind by the Aeon Akivili,穿梭 between star regions to seal the Stellaron (星核) that threatens civilization.

This world is set on Belobog — a planet covered by eternal blizzards. Eight hundred years ago, the invasion of the Antimatter Legion caused the Fragmentum (裂界) to spread here — an anomalous phenomenon that erodes reality and distorts physical laws. The root of the Fragmentum is the Stellaron buried deep beneath the city.

The Reincarnators are recruited by the Astral Express navigator Himeko to assist the crew (the Trailblazer, March 7th, Dan Heng) in descending into Belobog's undercity, locating and sealing the Fragmentum erosion core. But the Fragmentum is inhabited by all kinds of distorted creatures — from low-tier Fragmentum constructs to the cataclysmic Doomsday Beast. The greatest danger is the Stellaron itself — it has begun to develop self-awareness and is no longer a passive energy source.

Core Conflict: Survive in an environment of ever-encroaching Fragmentum, defeat the Fragmentum creatures and Doomsday Beast along the way, and finally complete the sealing ritual before the Stellaron awakens.

Plot Structure Type

Scene-by-scene: Open aboard the Astral Express receiving the mission → gradually descend into the Fragmentum after arriving at Belobog → finally face the awakening Stellaron at the Fragmentum core and complete the seal.

World Special Rules

  • Fragmentum Erosion Mechanic: In Fragmentum zones, make an Endurance check DC2 each hour against erosion; failure takes 1d4 Fragmentum damage (ignores AC). Wearing a Fragmentum protective suit grants DP+2.
  • Spatial Distortion: Every 3 rounds in the deep Fragmentum, the battlefield terrain randomly changes once — GM rolls 1d4: 1=Gravity Up (movement speed halved), 2=Gravity Down (movement speed doubled but attack DP-1), 3=Space Fold (random teleport to adjacent grid), 4=Time Slow (all Initiative -2).
  • Sealing Ritual: Core rule. Sealing requires 6 rounds of continuous guidance (Dan Heng as lead guide). Each round you may roll INT+Occult to assist (DP+1/success). Each Fragmentum shard provides an extra +1 DP. After sealing completes, the Stellaron loses all special abilities.
  • Astral Express Combat Style: Crew members use abilities of different elemental Attributes (physical, flame, ice, lightning, etc.). Elemental interactions: fire→ice, lightning→water, etc. (attribute counter deals +2 DP damage).
