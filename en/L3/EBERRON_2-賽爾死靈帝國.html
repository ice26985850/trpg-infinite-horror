二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-line Summary

Thrane's frontline city faces a fierce assault by Karrnath's undead legion. Reincarnators (RC) are thrown into this desperate defense, holding a strategic stronghold against necromancers and a lich general.

Full Story Background

Eberron is a magically industrialized fantasy world. Karrnath's ruler cooperates with the Blood of Vol church, using necromancy to create a vast undead legion. Now this legion launches an invasion against neighboring nations — The Last War enters its most intense phase.

The Reincarnators are dropped into the besieged frontline city "Fort Dawn," the last barrier against the undead legion's advance. The garrison has fought continuously for 9 days; ammunition and mana are nearly spent. The undead legion, commanded by a lich general, charges ceaselessly.

The Reincarnators' task is to help defend the city for 72 hours until reinforcements arrive — or find a way to break the undead legion's command chain and collapse the offensive at its root.

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: free exploration at the opening, shifting to act-by-act progression in the mid-to-late game.

World Special Rules

  • Undead Legion Tide: Every 4 hours an enemy attack wave auto-triggers. Wave size: 1d6+4 zombies + 1d4+2 skeletons. If summoning towers exist, numbers double.
  • Wall Durability: Wall total durability is 100 points. Each attack wave deals 1d10 damage to the wall. At 0 durability the city falls. Repair actions (STR DC3) restore 1d6 points.
  • Morale System: Garrison morale is 0–10. Morale affects defender combat: morale 7+ → DP+1, morale 4- → DP-1. Completing side quests and defeating enemy units raises morale.

Main God Restrictions

  • Forbidden to open the city gate without authorization (undead legion would immediately rush in)
  • Forbidden to use large-area fire spells (city has flammables and sheltering civilians)
  • Forbidden to reveal the Main God space to the garrison
