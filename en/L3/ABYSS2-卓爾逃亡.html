二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-line Summary

Reincarnators (RC) are thrown into a Drow prison camp in the Underdark. Within the dark domain of Lolth the Spider Queen, they must orchestrate a daring escape from this subterranean world of betrayal and dark magic.

Full Story Background

The Underdark is a vast cavern network beneath the surface world, ruled by the Drow (dark elves). Drow society teems with betrayal, intrigue, and fanatical worship of Lolth (the Spider Queen).

The Reincarnators are cast into the Underdark by the Main God (MG) and awaken inside a Drow prison camp. Treated as slaves, they are forced to toil in dark mines. The Drow guards are merciless, and the camp's slave overseer is a cruel priest of the Spider Queen.

The camp sits on the edge of Menzoberranzan. Sealed deep below is an exiled Demon Lord — it watches, waiting for its chance at vengeance. The Reincarnators must organize a revolt among the slaves and find a path out of the Underdark.

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: free exploration at the opening, shifting to act-by-act progression in the mid-to-late game.

World Special Rules

  • Total Darkness: The Underdark has almost no natural light. Units without Darkvision or a light source are considered blinded (all actions DP-4). Phosphorescent plants provide dim light (radius 3m, DP-2).
  • Drow Toxin: Drow warriors coat their weapons with a sleep toxin. A hit target must make an END + Resolve contest check (DC = number of successful attacks); failure causes sleep for 1d4 hours.
  • Subterranean Navigation: The Underdark is extremely easy to get lost in. Long-distance movement requires PER + Survival check (DC 4). Failure veers off course, costing an extra 2 hours.
  • Eye of Lolth: Lolth can watch the camp through the spider statue. Performing illegal acts within the statue's sight has a 30% chance of being discovered. Destroying the statue interrupts the surveillance.

Main God Restrictions

  • Using bright-light spells is forbidden (attracts many underground creatures)
  • Drow are extremely sensitive to betrayal; a single betrayal makes all Drow permanently hostile
  • Revealing the Main God space to slaves is forbidden
