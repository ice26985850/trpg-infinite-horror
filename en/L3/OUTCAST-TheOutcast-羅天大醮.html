二、Story Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

Reincarnators (轮回者) enter the Luo Tian Grand Ceremony — a martial tournament on Mount Longhu — as Outsider competitors. While advancing, they uncover the Quanxing cult's plot to steal the Heavenly Master's secret technique, Tong Tian Lu.

Full Story Background

In the shadows of modern China exists a world unknown to ordinary people — the Outsider (異人) world. Outsiders are humans with special abilities who wield "Qi" (炁, innate energy) to perform supernatural feats. The Heavenly Master Mansion of Mount Longhu is among the most authoritative Outsider organizations; its Luo Tian Grand Ceremony (a martial tournament) is held every few decades, drawing Outsiders from across the nation.

But this ceremony hides a conspiracy — Quanxing (全性), a cult devoted to absolute freedom, has planted multiple sleeper agents among the competitors. Their goal: while the Mansion's guard is weakened during the event, infiltrate the forbidden back mountain and steal the secret "Tong Tian Lu" (通天籙). If it falls into Quanxing's hands, the Outsider world's balance is shattered.

Reincarnators enter as newly-risen Outsider competitors — the Mansion secretly tasks them with investigating Quanxing. While advancing in the ring, they must gather evidence of the sleeper agents and stop the plot at the crucial moment.

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: register and freely explore the venue → tournament bracket (advance scene by scene) → notice Quanxing activity → stop the theft during the finals.

World Special Rules

  • Qi (炁) System: Outsiders use Qi as energy. All Outsider skills cost Qi (like a Willpower pool). Qi recovers via "Regulate Breath" (1 round recovers 1d4 + Perception Qi points).
  • Luo Tian Tournament Rules: 1v1 elimination. No attacking outside the ring. Down 10 sec or forfeit = loss. Killing opponents forbidden (disqualification + Mansion punishment). A protective ward by Mansion elders surrounds the ring.
  • Outsider Ability Types: Innate (born — e.g. lightning, telekinesis), Cultivated (technique inheritance — e.g. Golden Light Spell, Five-Thunder Law), Sorcery (curses and gu), Body Arts (body-as-weapon styles).
  • Quanxing Trait: Belief in "complete nature" — no moral restraint. Their Qi carries chaotic traits — Perception DC 3 to identify.

Lord God Restrictions
