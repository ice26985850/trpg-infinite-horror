II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

The 74th Hunger Games — 24 Tributes, one arena, only the last 6 alive return to the Lord God's space. The Reincarnators are thrown into this lethal duel as Tributes from a "new district," facing both Career Tribute hunts and alliance betrayal amid forests, rivers, caves, and artificial disasters.

Full Plot Background

The setting is the near-future dystopia of Panem. The Capitol rules twelve administrative districts and, every year, holds the "Hunger Games" — drawing one boy and one girl from each district, 24 Tributes total, thrown into a man-made arena to slaughter one another — to cement its control. The entire process is broadcast live nationwide by cameras.

The 74th Games' arena is mixed forest/grassland terrain, with a "Cornucopia" at the center — a giant metal structure piled with weapons and supplies. Rivers, caves, dense woods, and open fields surround it, but every zone has artificial traps placed by the Game Control Center (GCC).

The Reincarnators enter 1 hour before the Games begin — they wake in the underground prep room of the Tribute Tower, just finished with styling and the opening parade. Their wristwatch shows the mission. The next hour is a strategy meeting with mentor Haymitch, then they are sent up glass tubes to be launched into the arena.

GM note: threats here come from three layers — ① the other 23 Tributes (especially the Career Tributes from Districts 1–2), ② the arena's artificial traps (disasters, fire walls, floods), and ③ resource scarcity and alliance-trust crises. Social maneuvering outweighs pure combat.

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: Phase 1 is Tribute Tower prep (pre-battle strategy and socializing), Phase 2 is the arena opening (Cornucopia supply scramble), Phase 3 advances scene by scene (forest stronghold → manhunt → final showdown).

World Special Rules

  • Sponsor System:
  • Performance is rated "Spectacle Score" (GM judges, 1–10): successful combat (+2), healing ally (+1), tactical trick (+3), showing humanity (sympathy +2), killing a Career (+5).
  • Accumulate 10 → mentor may request 1 airdrop. Item decided by GM 1d4 (see S-01 table).

  • Silver parachute descends, 2 rounds to land (Reincarnators and enemies see simultaneously). The drop is marked in the survivors' sky projection (visible arena-wide).

  • Arena Ecology:

  • Forest: Hide DP+2 (canopy cover); every 8h random encounter 20% (normal/Career/Tracker Jacker).
  • River: unlimited water; shallow move -1; open banks (no cover).
  • Cave: Defense Base Defense+2 (single entrance); GCC monitoring weakest (stealth DP+2, unseen by Capitol).

  • Field: open ground — ranged DP+2 (no block), Hide DP-4 (almost no cover), highest trap density (fire wall / mines).

  • GCC Disasters:

  • Fire Wall: a 50m-wide fire wall creeps (5m/round); crossing deals 3d6 fire (Agility + Athletics DC3 to pass through gaps).
  • Flood Valve (Day 4): raises river zone water 3m; shallows become deep (Swim DC2); Capitol water snakes added.
  • Poison Mist: covers radius 50m, 1d4 toxin/round (Endurance DC2 to hold breath but not stop skin absorption — still 1 damage/round).

  • Mutant Hounds (Day 5): gene-modified wolf creatures — track a specific Tribute (GCC locks via sky projection) until target dead. HP20, Agility 8, bite 2d6+2 pierce.

  • Nightly Dead Projection:

  • After each sunset, GCC projects the day's dead photos and districts on the sky dome. National anthem plays. All Tributes must stop — customary mourning time.
  • A Reincarnator moving during projection is marked "disrespectful" by GCC; next day trap-focus chance ×2.

  • During projection you can see who died (watch also updates the count).

  • Fatigue System:

  • Every 24h without rest needs Endurance check (DC2); failure loses 2 DP. Resting in forest stronghold or cave (cover / ally watch) recovers normally; in open field or near Cornucopia — resting needs a night watch or 50% encounter chance.

Lord God Restrictions

  • "Do not reveal the Lord God's space to plot characters."
  • "No weapons beyond this world's tech level" (this world ~ year 2100 — above modern but no supernatural) — Genetic Lock is exempt (human potential, not tech).
  • "When killing an enemy Tribute, confirm they are truly hostile" (no indiscriminate slaughter — Capitol audience resents it, lowering sponsor favor).

VIII. Multi-Team Rules

This script's special nature: "all Tributes are competitors" — Reincarnators not on the same team are technically also rivals (but the Lord God transfers by team as the survival unit).

If multiple Reincarnator teams compete: - Each team judged independently for survival — Team A with 3 alive (among the 6 remaining) clears all 3; Team B wiped fails. - Optional ② "ally 2 Tributes" — if the ally is another team's Reincarnator, it counts. - Harming fellow-team Reincarnators — judged "betrayal" by the Lord God, deducts 2000 reward points (if insufficient, erased after return).
