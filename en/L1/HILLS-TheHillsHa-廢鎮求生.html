II. Story Overview (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

In an abandoned mining town inside a nuclear test zone, mutated cannibals roam and hunt. The Reincarnators must cross the wasteland town and reach the safe zone within 72 hours.

Full Story Background

The world is set in the modern-day New Mexico desert of the United States. During the 1950s, the U.S. government conducted numerous nuclear tests in this area; the radiation mutated the local residents into bloodthirsty cannibals. They dwell in underground mines and only emerge at night to hunt.

In the source film, the Carter family is attacked at this location. The Reincarnators arrive one day after the Carter family was attacked. Cannibals are still active in the wasteland town, and emboldened by a recent "successful hunt," they now roam even during the day.

The Reincarnators awaken at an abandoned gas station on the edge of the town. They must cross the entire town and reach the military checkpoint (safe zone) 15 km to the north. The challenge comes not only from the mutant cannibals' attacks, but also from the desert environment itself — extreme heat, lack of water, and ever-present radiation.

Story Structure Type

Act-based (linear crossing route): free exploration in the early phase, with act-by-act progression through the middle and later phases.

World Special Rules

  • Radiation Damage: For every 12 hours exposed in the wild, make an END check (DC 2). Failure deals 1d4 radiation damage (cannot naturally recover; requires medical treatment or reaching the safe zone).
  • Cannibals Fear Light: Night activity bonus DP+2; under daylight or strong light DP-2 (use flares, fire, etc. to drive them off).
  • Desert Environment: Daytime heat — for every 4 hours without drinking, make an END check (DC 2); on failure all checks lose DP-1 (stacking).
  • Sound Attraction: Gunshots attract all cannibals within a 500-ft radius.

Lord God Restrictions

  • Forbidden to reveal the existence of the Lord God Space to story characters.
  • This world has no external communications infrastructure (no cell signal).
  • Attempting to detour around the town (e.g., crossing the desert) doubles radiation and dehydration risk.
