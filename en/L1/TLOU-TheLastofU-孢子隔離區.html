II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

Twenty years after the Cordyceps fungus pandemic destroyed civilization, in the Boston Quarantine Zone the Reincarnators (輪迴者) receive a task: escort a girl immune to the fungus across the fallen city to the Fireflies' lab — her blood may be humanity's only hope.

Full Story Background

The world has been overrun by Cordyceps-infected humans. Infection has four stages: Runner (newly infected, intact vision and fast), Stalker (hides and ambushes), Clicker (blind but with acute hearing, instant-kill attack), Bloater (massive armored, throws spore bombs). Every inch of the city may hide spores — a gas mask is basic equipment.

Story Structure Type

Mixed: Phase 1: gather gear and intel inside the Quarantine Zone. Phase 2: escort Ellie across the city ruins, responding scene-by-scene to various infected and hunter groups.

World Special Rules

  • Infected Types: Runner (intact vision, fast), Stalker (ambush from hiding), Clicker (blind but acute hearing, instant-kill attack), Bloater (heavy armor, spore bombs).
  • Gas Mask: In spore zones, without a gas mask, Endurance (END) DC3 each round; failure = infection — transform into a Runner after 24 hrs.
  • Sound Management: Clickers are extremely sensitive to sound. While stealthing, all actions require Agility (AGI) + Stealth DC3. Failure draws Clickers.
  • Ellie's Immunity: Ellie won't be infected if bitten. But a direct Clicker attack can still kill her (physical damage, not infection).
