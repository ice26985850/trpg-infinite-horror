II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

The afterlife is neither heaven nor hell — it is an ordinary Tokyo apartment containing a giant black sphere called GANTZ. Those chosen by GANTZ (all freshly dead) are forced into a killing game: equipped with powered combat suits and sci-fi weapons, they must exterminate designated alien targets within a time limit. Each kill earns points; 100 cumulative points lets you choose: revive a dead person, gain freedom, or acquire a stronger weapon.

Full Plot Background

GANTZ's rules are simple: chosen → teleported to a mission zone → eliminate all designated aliens within the time limit → failure or death means permanent death (GANTZ will not revive you). Aliens range from the seemingly harmless Onion Aliens to giant Buddha Aliens — appearance never reflects strength. The Reincarnators are dropped into GANTZ's loop: as newcomers they join the hunt, needing to quickly adapt to GANTZ's weapon system and combat suits while managing the other GANTZ players on their team (also forced dead — some panic, some are selfish, a few are reliable fighters).

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: scene-by-scene mission structure — each side quest corresponds to one GANTZ mission (clear a specific alien). Completing 3 missions accumulates 100 points for freedom. Between missions there is a short prep phase (in the GANTZ room).

World Special Rules

  • GANTZ Equipment System:
  • X-Gun Rifle (basic): 1d10 energy, precise, 30-round charge.
  • Z-Gun Cannon (advanced — unlocked at 25 points): 3d12 energy, range 15ft, 5-round charge.
  • GANTZ Knife (basic): 2d6 slash, high-frequency vibration (ignores 5 points of armor).
  • Combat Suit (basic / issued free during missions): Base Defense +2, STR+2, AGI+2, END+2, with built-in radar. Forced-issued by GANTZ before entering a mission zone; does not occupy an equipment slot, costs no points, auto-retrieved at mission end.
  • Point System: Onion Alien = 2 pts each, Buddha Alien = 10 pts each, Dinosaur Alien = 20 pts each. 100 cumulative points lets you choose freedom, revival, or a powerful weapon.
  • Time Limit: each mission has a strict time limit. Overtime forces retrieval (those who didn't finish = death). The GANTZ sphere projects a countdown on your wrist during the mission.
  • Free Combat-Suit Issuance Rule: all Reincarnators, before entering a GANTZ mission zone, are auto-issued and wear the black skintight combat suit (bonuses above); it occupies no slot, costs no points, and is auto-retrieved at mission end, cannot be taken out of this script.

Lord God Restrictions
