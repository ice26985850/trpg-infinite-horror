II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

In the far future, the megastructure (The City) built by humanity has expanded unchecked beyond Jupiter's orbit. In this inorganic labyrinth, the Safeguard exterminates without distinction any human who does not carry the Net Terminal Gene. The Reincarnators are trapped in the deep layers of the megastructure and must find the legendary water purifier — a device that filters the megastructure's toxins — and reach the Safe Level past the pursuit of the Silicon Life and the Safeguard.

Full Plot Background

The megastructure is an infinitely stretching mechanical maze. Millions of layers of steel, conduits, abandoned factories, and perpetually dark shafts. Humanity has degenerated into small settlements, barely clinging to survival. The Safeguard — the network's immune system — mercilessly "disinfects" any human. The Silicon Life is a non-carbon-based life-form evolved by the megastructure, possessing higher physical resistance and destructive power. The Reincarnators' only advantage: the Silicon Life occasionally fight each other over territory, and these gaps can be exploited.

Story Structure Type

Hybrid: segmented maze exploration — each level is an independent scene. Descend gradually from the starting level; each level has a different ecosystem and enemies. Final objective: find the Purifier Level and the transit elevator.

World Special Rules

  • Mechanical Environment: Most of the megastructure's metal floors are electrified (1d4/round unless insulated boots equipped). Coolant in the pipes is lethal poison (contact → Endurance DC4 or 2d8 toxin/round).
  • Silicon Life: Non-carbon life, resistant to physical attacks (damage -2), but energy weapons deal +3 damage to them. High-tier Silicon Life uses gravity manipulation (all within range DP-2).
  • Safeguard System: Safeguard spawn infinitely — each destroyed unit is replaced by reinforcements within 3 rounds. The only safe methods are stealth or temporary Net Gene masking. Safeguard are also hostile to non-human targets — exploit their fights with Silicon Life.

Lord God Restrictions

  • "Do not destroy the megastructure's structural support pillars — or the whole level collapses (instant death)."
  • "Net Terminal Gene fragments cannot be transferred — only the implanted person is protected."
  • "Killing innocent settlement residents deducts 1000."
