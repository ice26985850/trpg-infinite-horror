II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

The northernmost town in Alaska enters a 30-day polar night. An ancient vampire clan treats this isolated town as a hunting ground. Survive until sunrise.

Full Plot Background

The world is modern. Barrow, Alaska, the northernmost town, has a 30-day polar night each year — the sun does not rise. A vampire clan led by the ancient Marlow plans to turn the town into their hunting ground during this period. They cut communications, destroy vehicles, burn the airport — then begin house-to-house slaughter.

The Reincarnator enters the scene as a "short-term visitor" to the town — perhaps a scientist on expedition, a passing traveler, or a member of the winter supply transport team. The polar night has just begun (Day 1); the vampires' first attack will start 48 hours later (Day 3).

This is not a script won by combat — individual vampires far surpass humans (STR 5, DEX 4, with rapid regeneration). The Reincarnator's challenge is: organize survivors, find the vampires' weakness (UV lamps — sunlight is unusable here, but UV lamps can substitute), fortify defenses, and survive 30 days.

GM Note: Time management is the key of this script. Each day's daytime (actually also night) is preparation time, while the night (vampire activity peak) is the survival trial. The Reincarnator must weigh "gathering supplies" against "fortifying defenses" in time allocation.

Plot Structure Type

Scene-by-Scene (advanced by day): free exploration early, scene-by-scene progression in mid-late phase.

World Special Rules

  • Polar Night Environment: Full darkness throughout (no sunlight). Humans lack night vision and need light — but light exposes position and attracts vampires
  • Vampire Weaknesses: ① Ultraviolet (2d6 radiation damage/round); ② Silver (damage reduction negated);
    ③ Flowing water (cannot cross, psychological barrier); ④ Must be invited to enter private residences (ancient rule)
  • Vampire Advantages: ① Darkvision (60ft); ② Rapid regeneration (2 HP/round, unless damaged by silver or UV);
    ③ Animal communication (can control wolf packs, bats); ④ Immune to normal weapons (non-silver/non-UV attacks get −5 reduction)
  • Daily Progression: GM rolls 1d6 each day for vampire activity intensity: 1-2=Low (0-1 encounters), 3-4=Medium (1-2 encounters), 5-6=High (2-3 encounters, may include elite vampire)
  • Civilian Morale System: Each dead civilian gives remaining civilians 20% panic chance (Resolve Check DC3, failure causes dangerous behavior like attempting to flee)

Main God Restrictions

  • Prohibited from revealing the existence of Main God Space to plot characters
  • This world is a modern realistic world (with supernatural elements — vampires). Reincarnators' supernatural abilities are seen by civilians as "miracles" or "witchcraft"
  • Cannot leave Barrow early — roads snow-blocked, airport burned, all vehicles destroyed
  • Any attempt to walk out of town on foot during polar night = suicide (−40°C extreme cold, no light, wolf-pack threat)
