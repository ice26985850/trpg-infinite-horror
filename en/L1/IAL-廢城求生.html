II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

Manhattan, New York has become a dead city; human civilization survives only as broken ruins after a viral outbreak. As one of the last survivors, the Reincarnator must survive seven days under the hunting threat of nocturnal Variants (Darkseekers), find clues to a cure, and reach the extraction point.

Full Plot Background

The year is 2012, three years after the Krippin Virus (KV) fully broke out. A gene-modified virus designed as a cancer cure mutated, converting 90% of the global population into nocturnal, bloodthirsty, light-fearing Variant creatures — the Darkseekers. Fewer than 1% of humans are naturally immune.

The Reincarnator enters at the third year after the outbreak. Manhattan is now overgrown with vegetation and ruins; abandoned vehicles and collapsed buildings litter the streets. Daylight is the safe window for exploration, but after sunset the Darkseekers pour out of darkened buildings and dominate the dead city.

GM Note: The true threat of this script lies not in the single-unit strength of enemies, but in the time pressure of the day-night cycle and the scarcity of resources (gasoline, ammunition, medical supplies, light sources). Darkseekers fear light but are extremely aggressive and swift in darkness.

If differing from the original timeline, you may set that Dr. Neville already sacrificed himself before the Reincarnators arrive, leaving only recordings / notes / experimental data as clues to the cure.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: Phase One (Days 1–2) is awakening at Washington Square and free exploration (searching supply depots); Phase Two (Days 3–7) shifts to scene-by-scene progression (lab infiltration → safe-house night watch → extraction).

World Special Rules

  • Day-Night Cycle System:
  • Daytime (06:00–18:00): Variants retreat deep into buildings; streets and open areas safe. Free movement, search, construction. But any indoor windowless space (basement, sealed room) may still hide lurking Variants.
  • Night (18:00–06:00): Variants active in all unlit areas. Moving outdoors requires a Perception (WIS)+Investigation Check (Difficulty (DC) 2) every 30 minutes; failure triggers 1d4 Variant patrol.

  • Dusk (17:30–18:00) and Dawn (05:30–06:00): transition periods, Variant activity −50%, but a few larger individuals may still move in shadows.

  • Light Source Rules:

  • Variants fear light: direct sunlight = instant death (immediate carbonization). Bright light (military flashlight, flare, searchlight, etc.) = within range (5m bright / 10m dim) reduces Variant DP by 4 and disables special abilities.
  • A safe house must maintain a continuous light source — otherwise a raid roll every 30 min (1d10, 8+ Variants breach the line).

  • Light fuel: flashlight battery (depletes every 2 hours), generator+gasoline (1 unit gasoline per 12 hours), campfire (needs fuel added every 4 hours).

  • Variant Perception System:

  • No vision (treat as permanently blinded), but hearing and smell extremely acute.
  • Sound attraction radius: gunshot 300m, shout 80m, normal conversation 15m, movement (unhidden) 30m, generator running 100m.
  • Throwables can create noise to lure Variants away (DEX+Athletics Check, Difficulty (DC) 2).

  • Strong reaction to blood scent — any bleeding target (HP below 50%) within 200m radius is prioritized and locked by Variants.

  • Fatigue System:

  • Every 24 hours without rest requires an Endurance (CON) Check (Difficulty (DC) 2); failure loses 2 DP, stacking (max −6 DP). Rest recovery in a safe house is 2× normal.

  • Infection Mechanism:

  • After being bitten by a Darkseeker, an Endurance (CON) Check is required (Difficulty (DC) 4, as the mutated KV virus is stronger after three years). Failure: body temperature keeps rising, converting to a Variant after 1d3×10 minutes. Success: only bite damage (1d8 piercing) and retains human identity.
  • If cure data obtained (S-01), infection Endurance Check DC drops to 2.

  • "If you deliberately break building windows in daytime to introduce sunlight and kill lurking Variants, kill points are halved" (Main God views this as exploiting world rules)
