二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

Protect Earth-616 from the collapsing multiverse and stand against the rogue Scarlet Witch.

Full Plot Background

The multiverse is collapsing. The corpse of a Doctor Strange from a parallel universe appears on Earth-616 — the first warning sign. Soon, different universes begin invading one another — buildings flicker between alternate versions of reality, and the laws of physics start to fail.

The culprit is Wanda Maximoff (the Scarlet Witch) — wielding the power of the Darkhold, she seeks a universe where she can be a mother, but in doing so she tears apart the structure of the multiverse.

The Reincarnators must assist Doctor Strange and Wong in stabilizing the reality structure at three-dimensional convergence points, while fighting the chaotic constructs from every universe. In the end they must face the Scarlet Witch — but defeating her is not the only option. Saving her — freeing her from the corruption of the Darkhold — is the harder but better ending.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: Defend three convergence points simultaneously → each point is an independent battle.

Key Locations

Location A: New York Sanctum (Base)

Doctor Strange's New York stronghold. Filled with magical artifacts and portals. This is where the first convergence point is located.

Location B: Kamar-Taj

A mystic sanctuary in the Himalayas. The library holds vast knowledge about dimensional stability. Wong provides support here.

Location C: Wundagore Mountain (Final)

Wanda's retreat. Reality warped by chaotic magic and Darkhold runes. The site of the final confrontation.

World Special Rules

  • Reality Collapse: Every 12 hours, 1 new convergence point automatically appears — must be defended
  • Chaos Magic: Wanda's magic can "rewrite" reality — the GM may change 1 field rule on her turn (e.g. "gravity direction flips" or "fire immunity becomes fire weakness")
  • Star Portal: Miss America can open cross-dimensional teleportation — but requires a 2-hour cooldown after each use

Lord God Restrictions

  • "Using the Darkhold is forbidden (if a Reincarnator attempts to read it — immediately make Resolve (RES) DC 6 / failure = corruption)"
  • "If Miss America dies — lose the cross-dimensional escape route"
