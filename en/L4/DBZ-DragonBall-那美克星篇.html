二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

The cosmic emperor Frieza descends upon the peaceful planet Namek with his whole army to seize its Dragon Balls. The Reincarnators must gather at least 3 Dragon Balls while fleeing Frieza's legions, and survive until Goku arrives.

Full Plot Background

Age 762, Planet Namek. After the Saiyans attacked Earth, Piccolo and Kami died in battle, and Earth's Dragon Balls vanished with them. To revive their comrades, Krillin, Son Gohan, and Bulma travel to Namek by spaceship — a planet that has its own Dragon Balls.

But they are not the only ones who want the Dragon Balls. The cosmic emperor Frieza has long since arrived at Namek with his army, slaughtering Namekians to seize the Dragon Balls. Frieza's power is overwhelming — even in his first form he can easily destroy a planet.

The Reincarnators enter shortly after Krillin and Gohan arrive at Namek. Frieza's army already occupies several villages, systematically collecting Dragon Balls. Piccolo is en route to Namek with Princess Snake's help — while Son Goku, lagging behind, trains himself with gravity aboard his spaceship.

The core challenges of this script: - Dragon Ball Contest: Namek has only 7 Dragon Balls — Frieza already holds several; the rest must be raced for against his army - Power Gap: Frieza's first form alone can crush the whole party. Before facing him head-on, you must gather Dragon Balls, gain Namekian aid, and wait for Goku - Ki System: The core combat style of the Dragon Ball world — Perception+Athletics checks to control Ki attacks; advanced techniques like Kaio-ken are the key to turning the tide - Countdown: Namek will explode under Frieza's final attack — a desperate 5-minute countdown

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: After landing (S01) is free exploration — choose to assist Krillin or act alone to find Dragon Balls. Then progress by plot to the Frieza encounter (S02–S04), finally the forced Frieza showdown + planet-explosion countdown (S05–S06).

五、World Setting

Ki System (World Special Rules)

Ki — Basics

Item Rule
Ki Amount Endurance × 3 + Resolve × 2
Ki Recovery Recover 5 points at end of each scene; meditation (WIS+COMP check, DC2, requires 5 min) recovers Endurance-value points
Ki Cost Declared per Ki-related check — every 3 Ki consumed, damage +1d6 (cap +3d6 = 9 Ki)

Ki Attacks

Ki attacks use Perception+Athletics (Ki) checks; base damage is 1d6 energy. You may spend extra Ki to increase damage or range: - Increase damage: every 3 Ki = +1d6 damage (cap +3d6) - Expand range: spend 3 Ki → area attack (AOE 3m), all targets take base damage (no bonus)

Advanced Ki Techniques

Technique Rule Cost
Kaio-ken AGI+Melee check, DC3. On success all attacks this round deal ×2 damage, but at round end requires Endurance DC3 check — fail = 2d6 recoil damage this round 5 Ki
Spirit Bomb Requires 3 rounds charging (cannot act during — SP status). Round 3: Resolve DC5 check — success deals instakill to target (any enemy with HP<25%). Fail = all Ki spent charging in first 2 rounds wasted (3d6 recoil) 12 Ki (4 Ki per charge round)
Bukūjutsu (Flight) Passive flight — AGI+Athletics check, DC1 (passive maintenance 1 Ki/round) 1 Ki/round (while flying)
Solar Flare WIS+Athletics check, DC3 — success blinds all enemies 1 round (Defense DP-4) 4 Ki

Saiyan Near-Death Revival (once per script only)

When a Reincarnator's HP drops below 10% (near-death state) and survives, all attributes permanent +1 (once per script). This is the Saiyan racial trait — Reincarnators gain a temporary "Simulated Saiyan Bloodline" ability in this script.

Battle Power Scouter (Enemy Equipment)

Technology equipped by Frieza's army — detects the target's total attributes (sum of 9 attributes + current HP). This means the enemy knows your strength — hiding true power (using "Ki Suppression") requires an INT or WIS DC3 "Ki Suppression" check.

Namek Explosion Countdown (5 minutes)

When Frieza's final attack ignites the planet's core, the 5-minute countdown begins. Each game round ≈ 30 seconds — about a 10-round countdown. During this period: - All non-evacuation actions suffer DP-2 (time pressure) - Must find evacuation means: spaceship (Goku's/Frieza's ship), Instant Transmission (Goku), or Lord God teleport (costs B) - If not evacuated within 10 rounds: Namek explodes, all life on the planet dies (cannot be revived by any means)

  • Common Stuck Point 1: Frieza's oppressive presence makes players afraid to act — Solution: through Krillin and Gohan's intel, clearly convey "Frieza has transformations, not just first form" "you can't match him but can outwit him." His greatest weakness is arrogance — can be stalled until Goku arrives.
  • Common Stuck Point 2: 5-minute countdown causes panic — Solution: use "real-world countdown vs in-game rounds" to ease pressure. Emphasize 10 rounds is feasible — each round has clear progress. If Reincarnators panic too much — have Goku proactively offer "Instant Transmission for 1 person" as a safety net.
  • Difficulty Up: Frieza skips second/third form directly to final form, Ginyu Force attacks simultaneously + Frieza remote death-beam interference, 5-min countdown drops from 10 to 7 rounds, Namek core explosion delayed to 3 rounds after Frieza battle (instead of counting down only after — i.e. planet begins crumbling the moment of Frieza's final attack).
  • Difficulty Down: Frieza enters only second form then decides not to waste time on Reincarnators (interrupted by Goku arriving sooner), Ginyu Force defeated by Goku first (no Reincarnator fight needed), 5-min countdown raised from 10 to 15 rounds, passive pressure of planet-explosion countdown canceled from DP-2.
  • Player Unconventional Actions: If players try to "teleport directly to a safe planet using Dragon Balls" — ruling: Namek's Dragon Balls use Namekian language, INT DC4 to decipher — after deciphering must speak the Namekian wish. If success → plot jumps greatly — directly avoids Frieza final battle, but loses S03 evacuation tension and Goku vs Frieza climax. GM decides per situation whether to allow — and whether to grant full reward.
