二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

Help the wise King Gilgamesh repel Tiamat's horde of monsters and protect the final defense line of human history.

Full Plot Background

Human history — is about to be incinerated. King Solomon's Incineration of Humanity has already begun, and seven Singularities have distorted history. The Seventh Singularity — Mesopotamia, 2600 BCE — is the final defense line of human history.

The Alliance of the Three Goddesses is destroying this era. And an even more terrible threat — the primordial mother goddess Tiamat — is awakening from the depths of the Persian Gulf. Her tide of chaos will drown the walls of Uruk within seven days, transforming all life into her beasts.

Gilgamesh — no longer the arrogant tyrant, but ruling Uruk as the wise King — is organizing the final defense. He has summoned Ritsuka Fujimaru and Mash from Chaldea, and… the Reincarnators. This is the last battle for the survival of humanity.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: Defense-line battle — hold the walls of Uruk against three waves of attack → final thrust into the Persian Gulf.

Key Locations

Location A: Walls of Uruk (Main Defense Line)

The last fortress of Mesopotamia. Walls reinforced by Gilgamesh with the King's Treasury — HP 300 / Base Defense 18. Magic turrets and Gilgamesh's ranged Noble Phantasms are deployed on the walls.

Location B: Shore of the Persian Gulf

Tiamat's awakening point. The black tide of chaos keeps welling up from the sea, covering the land along the shore. The air here is toxic — non-divine creatures take 1d6 chaos damage each round.

Location C: Absolute Demonic Front (Final Battlefield)

The vast plain between the walls and the Persian Gulf. Largely submerged by Tiamat's tide of chaos — only a narrow land bridge leads to Tiamat's true body.

World Special Rules

  • Tide of Chaos: Tiamat's black mud. Those who touch it must make Fortitude DC 4 each round — failure accumulates 1 stack of "Chaos Corruption". At 5 stacks, transform into a monster
  • Divine Weapon: Only weapons with Divinity deal full damage to Tiamat. Non-divine weapon damage ×0.5
  • King's Treasury: Gilgamesh can provide 1 Noble Phantasm support every 3 rounds (roll 1d6 for type: 1-2 attack / 3-4 defense / 5-6 healing)

Lord God Restrictions

  • "Forbidden to reveal future history to ancient humans"
  • "If a Reincarnator uses modern weapons — Gilgamesh will see it as an insult and lose trust"
  • "If the walls of Uruk are breached before the final battle — instant mission failure"
