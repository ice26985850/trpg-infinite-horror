二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

In Fuyuki City, defeat at least 2 Servants, survive the Holy Grail War, and help break the curse of the Grail.

Full Plot Background

Fuyuki City. A deep-winter night. The Fifth Holy Grail War is underway — seven magi (Masters) have each summoned a legendary Heroic Spirit (Servant), fighting secret life-and-death battles across the city. Only the sole victor may obtain the omnipotent wish-granting machine — the Holy Grail.

But the Grail has been corrupted. Any wish is fulfilled in the most twisted way. The Reincarnators' mission is not only to defeat 2 Servants — they must also assist Rin Tohsaka or Shirou Emiya in discovering the truth of the Grail and breaking the cycle of corruption.

There are seven Servant classes: Saber, Archer, Lancer, Rider, Caster, Assassin, Berserker. Each possesses different legendary-class abilities. Who you ally with — and who you fight — will determine the entire course of the war.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: Open-ended free exploration of Fuyuki City; you may freely choose alliances and the order of engagements.

Key Locations

Location A: Emiya Residence (Safe Base)

Shirou Emiya's home — a Japanese mansion in a residential district. This is Saber's base, and one of the few safe zones. Rin Tohsaka frequently appears here.

Location B: Ryudou Temple (Caster Base)

An ancient mountain temple. Caster has laid a powerful barrier here. From here you can overlook the entire Fuyuki City.

Location C: Fuyuki Civic Hall (Final Location)

The site where the Grail descends. A giant magic circle is hidden underground.

World Special Rules

  • Servant Battles: Servants have resistance against ordinary weapons — Reincarnators need Magecraft reinforcement or Servant allies to deal effective damage
  • Grail Corruption: As time progresses, corruption increases. On day 7 the Grail descends — if S-01 is not unlocked, the Grail descends destructively
  • Command Spells: Can be obtained from defeated Masters — each Command Spell can order a Servant once (valid in this world)

Lord God's Restrictions

  • "If all Servants die but the Grail is not broken — the corrupted Grail descends immediately"
