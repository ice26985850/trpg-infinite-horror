二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

One-Line Summary

Defeat an Abyssal Devourer — a terrifying creation of an ultimate Awakened Being.

Full Plot Background

In a medieval world where Yoma lurk in hiding, humanity's only hope is the "Claymores" — half-human, half-Yoma female warriors implanted with Yoma flesh. When a Claymore overuses Yoki, they "awaken" — becoming Awakened Beings far more terrifying than Yoma.

The most powerful Awakened Beings are called "Abyssal Devourers" — their very existence is a realm of death. The Organization (the secret institution that breeds Claymores) has dispatched Clare and several Claymores to subjugate the newly emerged Abyssal Devourer. The Reincarnators join this subjugation campaign as the Organization's "outside collaborators" — but the Organization bears them no goodwill either.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid: Track the Abyssal Devourer → cooperate with or act independently of the Claymore squad.

Key Locations

Location A: Organization Forward Base

A simple camp. Tents store supplies and silver weapons. Clare and Miria are formulating battle plans.

Location B: Awakened Being Lair

A valley polluted by Yoki. The sky is dark red — a physical manifestation of concentrated Yoki. Ordinary humans must make an Endurance DC3 check hourly in this environment.

Location C: Abyssal Canyon (Final Battlefield)

A gigantic fissure cuts into the earth. The Abyssal Devourer lurks in the canyon depths — its Yoki is so strong it distorts the surrounding physical space.

World Special Rules

  • Yoki: Both Claymores and Awakened Beings use Yoki — a power that constantly erodes the user's will. A Claymore's Yoki usage cap is 80% — exceeding it causes awakening
  • Silver Weapons: All Claymore weapons are custom silver swords — DP+2 vs Yoma and Awakened Beings
  • Yoki Sense: Perception DC3 can sense Yoki sources within 100ft

Lord God's Restrictions

  • "Do not reveal the Reincarnators' true identity to the Organization"
  • "If a Reincarnator gains Yoki — after this world ends it must be returned or converted to point rewards"
