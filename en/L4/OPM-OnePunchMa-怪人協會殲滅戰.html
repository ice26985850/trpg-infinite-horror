II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

One-Line Summary

The Monster Association has built a vast underground base beneath Z-City. The Reincarnators (輪迴者), as temporary S/A-Class Heroes of the Hero Association, must descend into the base and eliminate the monster threat before Saitama arrives.

Full Plot Background

Z-City — the metropolis with the highest monster appearance rate. The Monster Association has built a multi-level base deep underground (B1–B3), gathering a large number of Tiger- to Dragon-level monsters, led by the Monster King, Orochi. The Hero Association assembles S-Class and A-Class heroes for a raid.

The Reincarnators are dropped into this world by the Main God and join the raid as temporary A/S-Class Heroes. However, the biggest variable is not the monsters — but the bald hero, Saitama. Saitama can solve any problem with a single punch, but he is currently lost in the underground maze. The time pressure is this: Saitama may arrive at any moment; once he strikes, all monsters are one-shot, and the Reincarnators lose their chance to prove themselves.

Plot Structure Type

Hybrid (Timed Battle): Each underground level advances linearly, but within each level there are multiple optional rooms and routes. Saitama's arrival probability increases every Round — providing constant time pressure.

World Special Rules

  • Saitama Time Pressure (core rule):
  • Initial arrival rate: 0%
  • Each combat Round elapsed: Saitama's arrival rate +10%
  • At 100% arrival rate: Saitama appears at the start of the current Scene
  • Saitama's arrival = all enemies in that Scene are one-shot (no reward) + Scene ends immediately

  • Players may influence Saitama's arrival time via S-01

  • Hero Rank Bonuses:

  • As temporary A/S-Class Heroes, the Reincarnators gain these passive bonuses:
  • S-Class: base Armor Class (AC) +2

  • A-Class: Hit Points (HP) +10

  • Disaster Level Equivalents:

  • Tiger-level (★★★): threatens a city block — roughly equal to a powerful enhanced human
  • Ghost-level (★★★★): threatens a city — roughly equal to a small army
  • Dragon-level (★★★★★): threatens several cities — a single entity can destroy a city
  • Above Dragon / Monster King (★★★★★+): threatens human civilization

  • Players' Unconventional Actions: If players try to "find Saitama directly and follow him" — GM ruling: Saitama moves randomly through the underground maze, tracking difficulty DC 5 (Perception + Investigation). Even if found, Saitama asks confusedly, "Where is this? Got a supermarket sale flyer?" then keeps wandering.
