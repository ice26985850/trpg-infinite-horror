## V. World Setting

### Timeline
> Modern day. Day 1 after the geomagnetic storm. All of northern Canada is without power or communication.

### Key Locations
- **Crash Site (墜機點)**: The broken fuselage provides initial supplies and shelter from the wind
- **Lake Mython (密斯頓湖)**: A frozen lake; a cabin sits at its center and fishing spots line the shore
- **Silent Valley (寂靜河谷)**: A transition zone where wolves roam but resources are abundant
- **Abandoned Mine (廢棄礦坑)**: A deep mine that blocks the wind and holds coal for heating
- **Signal Tower (信號塔)**: A battered radio tower on the mountain peak; activating it triggers rescue

### World Special Rules
- **Body Temperature System**: Every 4 hours, make an Endurance (END) check (Difficulty Class (DC) 1). Failure = body temperature drops one step (Normal → Cold → Hypothermia → Frostbite). Cold-weather gear grants DP+2.
- **Hunger System**: Every 12 hours you must consume 1 unit of food, or all checks suffer DP-2.
- **Wolf Packs**: Wolves are sensitive to firelight and noise. You can distract them by throwing meat (Agility (AGI) + Athletics (ATH) DC2).
- **Time Pressure**: After Day 20, blizzard frequency doubles.
