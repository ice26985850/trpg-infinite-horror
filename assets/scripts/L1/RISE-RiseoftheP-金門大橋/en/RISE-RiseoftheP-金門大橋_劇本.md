## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

> The genetically modified ape Caesar, granted superhuman intelligence by the ALZ-113 virus, leads all the apes from the San Francisco Zoo and the genetics laboratory in a mass breakout. The virus boosts ape intelligence but is a lethal plague to humans. The Reincarnators (輪迴者) are trapped in an outbreak-stricken San Francisco and must cross the city to reach the evacuation point on the Golden Gate Bridge.

### One-Line Summary

Under the twin threats of the ape uprising and the spreading virus, cross downtown San Francisco and escape to the far side of the Golden Gate Bridge.

## V. World Setting

### Timeline
> Modern day, San Francisco. Day 3 of the ALZ-113 outbreak. Caesar's ape horde controls the north side of downtown; the south end of the Golden Gate Bridge is the last human defensive line.

### Key Locations
- **Financial District (金融區)**: Skyscraper-lined; home to the Gen-Sys genetics lab and the birthplace of the apes
- **Zoo Area (動物園區域)**: Scattered cages and escaped animals; Caesar gathered a large ape force here
- **Fisherman's Wharf (漁人碼頭)**: A waterfront warehouse district where boats can be found
- **South End of Golden Gate Bridge (金門大橋南端)**: The last human line, manned by police and National Guard
- **North Shore of Golden Gate Bridge (金門大橋北岸)**: Evacuation point where the military receives refugees

### World Special Rules
- **ALZ-113 Virus**: After human infection, make an Endurance (END) check (Difficulty (DC) 2). Failure = lose 1 Endurance per 24 hours until death. The vaccine must be obtained from the lab (S-01).
- **Ape Intelligence**: Apes can organize tactics, use tools, and set traps. Apes are neutral toward humans — they do not attack unprovoked but will defend their territory.
- **Non-Lethal Passage**: When not using lethal force against apes (Optional Objective ①), the ape horde will not mark the Reincarnators as a threat.
- **Vehicles Available**: Abandoned vehicles line the streets. A Mechanics check (Intellect (INT) + Drive (DRV) DC2) can start one.

### Main God Restrictions (主神限制條件)
- Do not reveal the existence of the Main God Space (主神空間) to plot characters
- Tech weapons beyond year-2020 level are forbidden in this world
- Reincarnators are issued protective masks (ALZ-113 is spread by droplets)

## VII. Key Scenes (Mixed Format)

### S01 – Financial District Awakening
> **GM Read-Aloud**: "Sirens echo in the distance. In the office-building lobby, overturned chairs and scattered documents show this place was evacuated in haste. Outside the window, a few apes are in the convenience store across the street — using their hands, like humans, to rummage for food."

**Interactable**: Gen-Sys Building (visible but access-locked), Abandoned Police Car (may still have a radio), Convenience Store (occupied by apes)
**Encounter**: 2 scout apes (won't attack unless provoked)

### S02 – Zoo Passage
> **GM Read-Aloud**: "The zoo's iron fences are torn from the inside out. Caesar's troop has established a temporary stronghold here. Apes stand watch on the railings but do not attack passing civilians — Caesar's order is clear: 'We are not beasts.'"

**Branch**: ① Detour (safe but costs 2 hours) ② Pass through the zoo (must persuade the ape guards, Control (CON) + Social (SOC) DC3)

### S03 – Fisherman's Wharf Breakthrough
> **GM Read-Aloud**: "Fog blankets the wharf. In the alleys between warehouses, wild apes that never followed Caesar have claimed the territory — far more dangerous than Caesar's troop."

**Gene Lock (基因鎖) Hint**: Recommended Gene Lock stage — Unlocked. This scene can be completed without opening the Gene Lock. Use sound lure (thrown object DC2) or stealth detour (Agility (AGI) + Stealth (STL) DC2).

**Encounter**: 3–5 wild apes (★★)

### S04 – Golden Gate Bridge Final Defense
> **GM Read-Aloud**: "The Golden Gate Bridge's red cables burn in the sunset. At the south end, soldiers behind police sandbag lines are loading rubber bullets. On the north shore, helicopters wait for the last evacuees. But on the bridge — hundreds of apes are charging the defensive line."

**Gene Lock Hint**: Recommended Gene Lock stage — Unlocked.
**Branch**: ① Assist the police defensive line (Side Quest S-03) ② Slip past the ape horde via the maintenance passage beneath the bridge (stealth route)
**Encounter**: Ape horde (charge formation; does not attack a passive route)

## X. Appendix

### GM Memo
- Caesar's troop should not be a pure enemy — they are intelligent, organized, and motivated. Let players feel "they aren't villains, just different from us."
- Wild apes (those not led by Caesar) are the real threat.
- The virus mechanic gives players time pressure without despair — the vaccine exists in the lab and is an achievable goal.
