## II. Story Overview (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> An unknown virus outbreaks, and Fujimi Academy turns into hell within minutes. The Reincarnators must organize survivors amid the Dead (Zombie) tide, break through the blockade, and reach the evacuation point within 72 hours.

### Full Story Background

The world is set in modern-day Japan. An unknown virus spreads through the air; infected die within minutes, then revive as "Dead." The Dead react only to sound, have no vision, move slowly but in staggering numbers. The bitten turn within half an hour.

The Reincarnators enter **10 minutes** after the outbreak. By then Fujimi Academy has partially fallen — the school gate is sealed, some students and staff are infected, and communications are down. The source-film protagonists (Takashi Komuro's group) are gathering inside the school building, preparing to break out.

GM Note: In the source world, the "Dead" threat lies not in individual strength but in **numbers and resource pressure**. The script's true challenge is resource management (food, water, weapons, ammo) and team cohesion — not pure combat.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid:** the first 12 hours are free exploration (inside the school building), then it shifts to act-by-act progression (evacuation route).

### World Special Rules

- **Infection Mechanism:** After being bitten by a Dead, make an END check (DC 3). Failure turns you into a Dead after 1d6×10 minutes. Success means only bite damage (1d6 piercing), no transformation.
- **Sound Attraction:** The Dead have no vision, only reacting to sound. Gunshots (attract within 200 ft), shouting (within 50 ft), normal conversation (within 10 ft). Thrown objects can create noise to lure Dead away (AGI + Athletics check, DC 2).
- **Fatigue System:** Every 24 hours without rest, make an END check (DC 2); on failure all action checks lose 2 DP until 4 hours of rest.

### Lord God Restrictions

- "Forbidden to reveal the existence of the Lord God Space to story characters."
- "This world forbids tech weapons beyond year-2006 level" (world time is 2006).
- "Displaying supernatural abilities (outside this world's system) before any story character costs 500."

## X. Appendix

- **Maps:** See `maps/` directory: `HOTD_WM-01.svg`, `HOTD_EM-01.svg`, `HOTD_BM-01.svg`, `HOTD_BM-02.svg`
- **Special Rules Supplement:** Infection mechanism, sound-attraction rules, fatigue system (see "V. World Setting → World Special Rules")
- **GM Memo:**
 - **Common Stuck Points:** Players may be too cautious to leave the starting classroom. Solution — show other survivor activity on campus through the window, or have source characters knock and seek cooperation.
 - **Difficulty Up:** Multiply Dead count ×1.5, add "Runner Dead" variant (AGI 6, base DEF 8, move speed ×2).
 - **Difficulty Down:** Lower infection END check DC to 2, reduce each Dead's HP to 8.
 - **Unconventional Player Actions:** If players try to "occupy a supermarket and hold long-term," remind them of the movement-range limit — only the parking-lot coordinates count as the Lord God evacuation point.
