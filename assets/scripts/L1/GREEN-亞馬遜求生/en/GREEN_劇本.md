## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> Deep in the Amazon rainforest, a helicopter crashes in an uncharted primal jungle. The Reincarnators must face threats from jaguars, crocodiles, and native warriors — and above all the rainforest's own lethal trials: wound infection, parasites, starvation, dehydration, venomous snakes, and tropical disease. A rescue helicopter will arrive at the crash site at a set time, but first they must survive until then.

### Full Plot Background

The Amazon is one of Earth's most hostile environments. High heat and humidity, rampant disease, nearly every creature venomous or deadly. The native Yabahaca tribe sees the crash survivors as invaders; their poison darts and spears are no less dangerous than the beasts. Worse, parasites — once they burrow inside, you must perform crude self-surgery before the infection spreads. The Reincarnators must build camps, manage resources, and find the rescue helicopter's landing point within 16 days.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: free exploration + day–night cycle. By day explore the jungle, build camps, gather resources; by night reinforce camps against beast raids. Every 4 days the camp must move closer to the final extraction point.

### World Special Rules

- **Four Survival Metrics**: Hunger (1 food/8h, shortage → all DP-1), Dehydration (1 water/4h, shortage → all DP-2), Infection (untreated wound needs Endurance DC2 every 6h or 2d4 damage), Parasite (contact with unclean water/raw meat needs Endurance DC3; failure burrows inside — requires Intellect + Medicine DC3 surgery to remove).
- **Camp System**: a camp needs 4 elements: fire source (prevents night raids + cooking), water source (drinking), shelter (rest/recovery), traps (prevent raids + food). Each missing element raises night encounter rate +20%.
- **Tropical Disease**: malaria (mosquitoes — 30% infection chance in swamp, Endurance DC3 every 12h or DP-2), yellow fever, typhoid. The native shaman's herbs cure all tropical diseases.

### Lord God Restrictions

- "GPS or electronic navigation devices are forbidden — they fail in the rainforest."
- "Maliciously attacking non-hostile native civilians deducts 800."
- "Do not leave the designated rainforest range — beyond the boundary is a radiation zone."
