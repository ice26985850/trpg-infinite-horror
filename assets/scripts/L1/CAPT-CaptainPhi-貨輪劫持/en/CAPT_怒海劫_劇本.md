## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> Somali pirates hijack the US cargo ship Maersk Alabama. The Reincarnators must defuse the hijacking without being killed.

### Full Plot Background

The world is 2009, the Somali waters of the Indian Ocean. Four heavily armed Somali pirates, led by boss Abduwali Muse, board the US-flagged container ship Maersk Alabama, take the crew hostage, and demand ransom.

The Reincarnators are extra crew members — contractors, interns, or stowaways. When the hijacking happens they are at different positions in the ship (engine room, galley, below deck) and luckily not spotted by the pirates at first.

The real threat is not the pirates' numbers (only 4), but their AK-47s and the ever-present risk of international naval intervention (US Navy SEALs already en route). Any side's extreme action could cause casualties across the whole ship. The Reincarnators must:
1. Avoid being discovered and killed by the pirates
2. Defuse the hijacking (via negotiation, disarmament, or waiting for naval rescue)
3. Protect the crew (especially Captain Phillips)

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: free exploration up front, then scene-by-scene progression in the middle and late game.

### World Special Rules

- Pirates are not professional soldiers — an Intimidation check (DC2-3) can make them back down or surrender
- The US Navy arrives at scene hour 8. Before then, if unresolved, the Navy will take armed action (increases whole-ship casualty risk)
- Gunshots draw all pirates' attention — silent methods give a stealth bonus
- Crew are all civilians, no combat ability — if made to fight, 50% casualty rate

### Lord God Restrictions

- Do not reveal the existence of the Lord God space to story characters
- This world's weapons limited to modern conventional arms (2009 level)
- If Reincarnators kill an already-surrendered pirate, deduct 300 each
