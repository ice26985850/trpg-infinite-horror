## II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> An unknown virus breaks out aboard the KTX high-speed train from Seoul to Busan; the infected frenziedly chase and bite the living. The Reincarnator must organize survivors in the sealed cars, break through the infected, and survive until the train reaches Busan.

### Full Plot Background

The world is modern Korea, 2016. An unknown virus leaks from a Seoul bio-lab; the infected convert within seconds into frenzied, sprinting "Infected". The Infected are extremely sensitive to sound and light, act violently without reason, and cannot open doors. A bitten person inevitably converts within ~30 seconds, with no known immunity.

The Reincarnator enters **30 minutes** after the train departs Seoul Station. The outbreak has not yet happened in the cars, but a passenger in Car 10 is about to turn — the Reincarnator is in Car 11. The train travels south at 300km/h; next station Daejeon is ~40 minutes away.

GM Note: The core pressure of this script comes from **spatial confinement** (a high-speed train you cannot flee), **infection spread speed** (convert 30s after bite), and **resource scarcity** (only passengers' personal items in cars). Combat is not the only way out — effectively organizing passengers, controlling car doors, and using the broadcast system to lure the Infected are key.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: The train interior is linear progression (car to car), but each car scene offers multiple passage methods (combat breakthrough / stealth bypass / sound-light lure).

### World Special Rules

- **Infection Mechanism**: No immunity after being bitten by an Infected — inevitably converts to a normal Infected after 30 seconds (1 round). The converted Reincarnator character immediately falls under GM control as an enemy unit.
- **Sound-Light Sensitivity**: Infected have an abnormal sensitivity radius of **9m** (about one and a half standard cars). Gunshot / explosion (attract radius 30m), shout (15m), normal conversation (5m, doubled to 10m in sealed car). Throwables (water bottle, backpack) or the broadcast system can create remote noise to lure Infected (DEX+Athletics Check DC 1–3 by distance).
- **Car Door Mechanism**: Electric sliding doors between KTX cars normally work (button switch). Forced manual opening needs DEX+Athletics Check DC 1. While a door is open, Infected pour from that door into the current car. A car is considered "fully occupied" after ~20 Infected.
- **Train Broadcast System**: Operable from the cab or crew control panel. Plays a high-frequency alarm in a designated car, attracting Infected from that car and adjacent cars (max 2 uses, 3 rounds each).
- **Fatigue System**: Every 5 consecutive combat rounds in an infected car requires an Endurance (CON) Check DC 2; failure gives all action Checks DP−1 (cumulative, max −3). Resting 15 min in a safe car clears 1 fatigue layer.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Prohibited from revealing the existence of Main God Space to plot characters"
- "Supernatural abilities (magic, psionics, etc.) banned in this world"
- "Do not open exterior car doors when train speed exceeds 80km/h" (jumping equals suicide)

- **GM Memo**:
  - **Common Stuck Points**: Players may fear disembarking at Daejeon, causing extreme supply shortage. Solution — provide enough temporary weapons (fire extinguisher, safety hammer) during car exploration.
  - **Difficulty Up**: Increase Runner (★★) count (1–2 per car), double Daejeon platform Infected, add 1 Runner per round in final surge.
  - **Difficulty Down**: Infection conversion time extended from 30s to 1 min (2 rounds), add emergency lever inside car doors to lock directly (no Check), halve Daejeon platform Infected.
  - **Player Unconventional Actions**: If players try "all hide in restroom and wait", remind the Main God watch has a movement-range limit — must be near the front cab to be judged "arrived at Busan".
  - **Pacing Control**: This script is very fast-paced; GM should strictly control each car's combat time (suggest 2–3 quick rounds per car) to maintain panic pressure.
