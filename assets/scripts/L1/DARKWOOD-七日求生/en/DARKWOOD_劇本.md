## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> In a remote forest in 1980s Soviet-occupied Poland, an unknown "forest mutation" is spreading. Trees grow human faces, animals undergo grotesque mutations, and after nightfall the forest seeps a corrosive black mist. The Reincarnators must survive in the forest for 7 days, find the source of the mutation, and escape the woods.

### Full Plot Background

A remote Polish forest has been corrupted by an unknown force. Daytime is relatively safe — you can explore abandoned houses, gather supplies, and interact with other trapped souls. But at night you must hide inside a safe house: black mist wells up from the depths of the forest, and within it lurk unspeakable aberrations. Every night is a survival ordeal: reinforce doors and windows, manage fuel and light sources, and fight off creatures that come from the dark. On the 7th day the mutation reaches its peak, and the Forest Mother will fully awaken.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: a day–night cycle. By day, freely explore 5 regions to gather intel and supplies; by night, defend your safe house. The 7th day is the final boss battle.

### World Special Rules

- **Day–Night Cycle**: Daytime (06:00–18:00) you may freely explore, gather supplies, and move safe houses. Nighttime (18:00–06:00) you must stay inside a safe house. Safe houses can be upgraded (planks + nails = Base Defense +2). Night assaults come in 3 waves (2 hours apart), each wave 1d3 random enemies.
- **Light System**: Stove = basic light (radius 10ft). Kerosene lamp = mid light (radius 20ft, consumes kerosene). Generator = high light (radius 40ft, auto-defense 1 round). Enemies in darkness gain +3 DP on attacks.
- **Resource Management**: Food (1 ration/person/day; shortage → attribute DP-1), fuel (stove and kerosene lamp), medical supplies (herbs / first-aid kits), building materials (planks / nails / barbed wire). All must be gathered by going out in daytime.
- **Mutation Infection**: After being hit by an aberration, Endurance DC3; failure accumulates infection. At 5 points a permanent -1 to an attribute. At 10 points you transform into an aberration (character controlled by the GM).

### Lord God Restrictions

- "Leaving the forest boundary by more than 1 km is forbidden — the world beyond the boundary is barred by the Lord God."
- "Deliberately destroying the only radio or outside-communication device deducts 500 reward points."
- "The werewolf choice affects the ending — if you kill him, the Forest Mother's weakness info cannot be obtained."
