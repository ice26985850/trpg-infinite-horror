## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

> Hope County, Montana, is wholly controlled by the cult "Eden's Gate." The cult leader Joseph Seed calls himself the Prophet; his family of "Heralds" — Jacob (military), Faith (drug-induced hypnosis), and John (brainwashing judgment) — each controls a region. The Reincarnators, as civilians trapped in Hope County, must organize the local militia and rise up.

### One-Line Summary

> Organize a militia uprising in a cult-controlled town, topple the three Heralds, and overthrow the cult leader.

## V. World Setting

### Timeline

> Modern day, Hope County, Montana. Eden's Gate has severed all outside communication; the outside world believes this is just normal isolation for a remote town.

### Key Locations

- **Holland Valley (John's region)**: church, farmland, bunker brainwashing facility
- **Henbane River (Faith's region)**: Bliss flower fields, riverside suspension bridge, hallucination swamp
- **Whitetail Mountains (Jacob's region)**: militarized wolf-kennel prison, sniper towers, training camp
- **Joseph's Church**: the cult leader's main cathedral; entrance unlocked after defeating the three Heralds
- **Hope County Sheriff's Office**: militia home base, armory and medical station

### World Special Rules

- **Militia Recruitment**: after completing side quests or liberating outposts, you may recruit NPC militia (each provides a +1 DP assistance bonus).
- **Weapon Acquisition**: outposts contain random weapons (pistol / rifle / bow / explosives) that must be found through exploration.
- **Cult Bounty**: each time a Herald is defeated, patrol density in the remaining regions doubles.

### Lord God Restrictions

- Do not reveal the existence of the Lord God's space to plot characters.
- Tech weapons beyond 2020 levels are disabled in this world.
