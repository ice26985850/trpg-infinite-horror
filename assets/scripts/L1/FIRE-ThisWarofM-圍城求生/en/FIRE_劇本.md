## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The fictional Eastern European city of Pogoren is trapped in a civil-war siege. Supplies are scarce, snipers hold the high ground, rebels loot at will, and hunger and disease are deadlier than bullets. The Reincarnators are trapped in an abandoned apartment and must cooperate with other survivors to endure 30 days of siege until a ceasefire is signed.

### Full Plot Background

A siege is another kind of hell — no mutant monsters, no aliens; the enemy is human nature and scarcity. Food, medicine, fuel, drinking water — every basic survival resource is desperately scarce. By day, snipers force you indoors or into tunnels. By night you may go out for supplies — but at the risk of being discovered by rebels, blown up by a mine, or even forced to steal or rob other survivors. Every moral choice has consequences.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: fully free — a 30-day day–night cycle. By day manage the camp (cooking, crafting medicine, building, interacting with survivors); by night go out to explore (each night pick a destination for resource gathering or a mission). After 30 days a ceasefire arrives; surviving is victory.

### World Special Rules

- **Day–Night Cycle**: Daytime (06:00–18:00) you must stay indoors — snipers outside (going out requires Agility + Stealth DC4 or be sniped for 3d10). Nighttime (18:00–06:00) you may go out, but you have only one night and must return before dawn.
- **Resource System**: Food (1 ration/person/day; shortage → cumulative DP-1), Medicine (untreated wound infects after 24h, DC3), Fuel (warmth + cooking; 2 rations/day in winter), Building Materials (repair house + build workbench). All resources gathered at night or traded with black-market merchants.
- **Morality System**: Each choice accumulates a morality value. Stealing from survivors → -10 morality (at cumulative -30 all suffer Resolve DC3 or mental trauma). Helping others → +10 morality (at +50 gain "Hope" bonus: all DP+1 for 3 days).
- **Weather**: Days 1–10 autumn (normal), Days 11–20 winter (fuel demand ×2, disease risk +30%), Days 21–30 spring (back to normal but rebels' final assault).

### Lord God Restrictions

- "Use of tech weapons beyond the 1990s is forbidden" — civil-war era setting.
- "Killing other civilian survivors in self-defense deducts 500/person (self-defense exempt with just cause)."
- "Do not go out by day — a forced rule. Violators suffer sniper fire 3d10 (no save)."
