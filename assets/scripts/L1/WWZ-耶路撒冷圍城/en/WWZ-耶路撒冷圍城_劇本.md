## II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> In the global zombie apocalypse, the wall defense of Jerusalem is humanity's last fortress. Zombies run at stunning speed, stack into human walls to climb obstacles, and devour everything like a flood. The Reincarnator must lead civilians through the city to the helicopter extraction point before the wall collapses.

### Full Plot Background

The Reincarnator's task is not to repel zombies on the wall, but to lead at least 10 civilians from a designated zone safely to the helicopter extraction point before the defense collapses. Only 48 hours.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: First half organizes civilians and gathers supplies in the city (free exploration); second half is scene-by-scene escort of the evacuation convoy (linear progression).

### World Special Rules

- **Zombie Stacking**: Zombies can stack into towers to climb obstacles. Cumulative 2d6 per round; 20 can climb <10m obstacles.
- **Noise Attraction**: Gunshot (radius 300m), explosion (500m), helicopter (1km). Can throw sound devices to lure away (INT+Tech DC2).
- **Civilian Morale**: Each civilian death adds +20% panic chance to the rest. Panicked civilians run wildly, need Grace (CHA)+Persuasion DC2 to calm.
- **Z-Virus**: Bite requires Endurance (CON) DC4; failure converts to Zombie within 12 seconds.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Prohibited from revealing the existence of Main God Space to plot characters"
- "Without IDF permission, do not use heavy weapons against wall structures"
- "Causing more than 5 non-essential civilian deaths deducts 300"
