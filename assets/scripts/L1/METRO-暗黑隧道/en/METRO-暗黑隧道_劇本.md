## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> Twenty years after the nuclear war, the Moscow Metro is humanity's last refuge. Radiation-mutated creatures lurk in the tunnels, and each station has formed its own micro-society. The Reincarnators (輪迴者) must depart from VDNKh Station and cross tunnels filled with mutants and hostile factions to reach the legendary safe harbor — Polis Station.

### Full Story Background

In the post-nuclear world, the surface is choked with lethal radiation and even deadlier mutants — Demons (惡魔, flying predators) wheel overhead, and Dark Ones (暗黑者, psychically gifted mutant humans) watch from the shadows. The Metro tunnels are the only passage, yet the tunnels themselves are unsafe: cave-ins, mutant attacks, bandit raids, and patrols of the Nazis and the Red Line.

### Story Structure Type

**Mixed**: Station-by-station progression — each station is a scene requiring completion of its challenge to advance. Station types: Friendly Supply Station, Combat Defense Station, Puzzle/Exploration Station.

### World Special Rules

- **Filter System**: Gas masks require filters. Each filter lasts 1 hour (surface) or 3 hours (tunnel). Each character starts with 3. With insufficient filters, Endurance (END) DC3 causes 2d6 radiation damage every 10 minutes.
- **Bullet Currency**: Military-grade 5.45×39mm rounds (MGR) are the Metro currency. Usable for: trading (1 MGR ≈ 5 RP in supplies value), as "+1 damage reinforced ammo", or simply as normal ammunition. During trade, Presence (PRE) + Trade DC2 yields better prices.
- **Radiation Zones**: Some areas exceed radiation limits. Without protection, radiation accumulates per minute (beyond 5 points → -1 attribute; beyond 10 points → -2 HP per round). Anti-radiation drugs ("Radroot") reduce accumulation by 4 points (side effect: Perception (PER)-1 for 2 hours).

### Main God Restrictions (主神限制條件)

- "No use of supernatural abilities in front of Metro residents" — may be mistaken for a Dark One
- "Do not deliberately destroy a station's survival resources" — mushroom farms, generators, water purifiers
- "Killing innocent Metro residents deducts 500 per person"
