## 二、劇情概要（GM 限定）

### One-Line Summary

> The mist-shrouded valley of Barovia is a cursed land. Reincarnators (輪迴者) are trapped in the domain of the vampire Count Strahd von Zarovich and must rally a resistance before sunset, defeat the Dark Lord, and escape.

### Full Story Background

Barovia is a valley kingdom sealed off by mist and ruled by the vampire Count Strahd.
Hundreds of years ago Strahd struck a pact with dark powers, gaining immortality
at the price of being forever trapped in this cursed land.

The Reincarnators (輪迴者) are flung into the mists of Barovia by the Lord God (主神) and awaken near a Vistani camp.
Castle Ravenloft looms atop the cliffs, overlooking the entire valley.
Strahd has already noticed the "outsiders" and sent his minions to watch them.

The Reincarnators (輪迴者) must cross Barovia within 72 hours, gathering clues and weapons against Strahd—
the fabled Sunsword and the Holy Symbol—before entering Castle Ravenloft to challenge the Dark Lord.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Open exploration at the start, shifting to act-by-act progression in the mid-to-late game.

### World Special Rules

- **Mist Barrier**: Barovia is surrounded by magical mist. Attempting to cross it requires an Endurance + Resolve check (DC 5); failure deals 2d6 poison damage and the mist sends you back.
- **Daylight Limit**: Barovia's sunlight is greatly weakened by the mist. Outdoor radiant damage during the day is halved; indoors is always treated as a dark environment. Holy water and holy items are unaffected.
- **Vampire Rules**: Strahd and vampire spawns take 2d6 acid damage per round in running water. They cannot enter a dwelling uninvited. Silver weapons deal +50% extra damage. Sunlight deals continuous damage (2d6 radiant per round).
- **Telepathy**: Strahd can monitor the Reincarnators' (輪迴者) actions via telepathy. A Resolve DC4 is needed to resist detection.

### Lord God (主神) Restrictions

- Modern technology (beyond 1800s level) is forbidden in Barovia
- Strahd cannot be permanently killed by non-holy weapons—specific items (Sunsword / Holy Symbol) must be found
- Revealing the Lord God (主神) space to Barovia's residents is forbidden
