## 二、Story Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> Halloween night, October 31, 2018. Shibuya is sealed beneath a Veil; tens of thousands of civilians are trapped inside. A coalition of Special-Grade Cursed Spirits launches an all-out assault with one goal — seal away the strongest sorcerer, Satoru Gojo. Reincarnators (轮回者) are thrown in as Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College's temporary reinforcements and must survive the gaps between Domain Expansions and turn the tide.

### Full Story Background

The year is 2018. In this world, humanity's negative emotions coalesce into "Cursed Spirits" (咒靈), and those who can wield their own Cursed Energy to fight them are called "Jujutsu Sorcerers" (咒術師).

The Shibuya Incident is the largest terror attack in jujutsu history, planned by the Special-Grade Cursed Spirit Mahito, Jogo, Hanami, and a mysterious sorcerer (pseudo-Suguru Geto):
1. Release massive numbers of cursed spirits across Shibuya to spread panic.
2. Use tens of thousands of civilians as hostages to force Gojo alone into Shibuya.
3. Exploit Gojo's personality weakness — "he will not harm humans" — to encircle him.
4. Spring the trap — the Prison Realm (獄門疆) seals Gojo away.

The Reincarnators enter 10 minutes after the Veil is raised. Communication and transit around Shibuya Station are cut. Gojo has just charged alone into the Shibuya underground. College reinforcements (Itadori, Fushiguro, Kugisaki) are assembling to enter in waves.

The core challenges:
- **Domain Expansion**: Multiple enemies wield Domain Expansion; attacks inside a domain are "guaranteed hit" — the deadliest mechanic of this script.
- **Cursed Energy Management**: Techniques drain the Cursed Energy pool; running dry means you cannot fight.
- **Civilian Protection**: Many civilians are trapped in the subway; protecting or abandoning them directly affects rewards and moral consequences.
- **Gojo's Fate**: Whether you help Gojo break the encirclement decides the story's direction.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: The first half (0–3 hr game time) is linear (assemble → descend underground); after the underground mall it becomes free exploration (multiple routes), culminating in the station concourse Domain battle.

## 五、World Settings

### Cursed Energy System (World Special Rule I: Cursed Energy)

On entering this world, the Lord God grants "Cursed Energy Perception" and "Cursed Energy Manipulation." All Reincarnators may become "sorcerers" — able to perceive and wield Cursed Energy against curses.

#### Cursed Energy Pool

| Field | Content |
|------|------|
| Maximum | Resolve × 2 + Control × 2 + 10 |
| Recovery | Recover Control-attribute points at scene end; "Cursed Energy Meditation" (Resolve + Composure check, DC 2, 10 min) recovers Resolve × 2 points |
| Cost | Technique use: by skill (see below); Cursed Energy reinforcement: 1 CE per +2 DP; Cursed Energy barrier: 1 point/round |

#### Four Basic Cursed-Energy Operations

| Operation | Effect | Cost |
|---------|------|------|
| CE Reinforcement | Infuse body with CE for physical boost. Attack check DP+2, damage +Resolve | 1 CE/round |
| CE Barrier | Form a CE defense layer around the body. Gain defense bonus successes equal to Control | 1 CE/round (reaction) |
| CE Perception | Sense CE sources in range (incl. hidden curses). Perception + Composure check DP+3 | 0 (passive) |
| Technique Use | Use your personal innate technique (see design rules) | By technique |

#### Innate Technique (術式)

Each Reincarnator designs 1 technique — like "Hatsu" in Nen. It must have clear effect and CE cost. Examples:
- **Cursed Energy Bullet**: Condense CE into a ranged attack, 3d6 CE damage, mid-range 15m, cost 2 CE.
- **Cursed Energy Barrier (Zone)**: Create a 5m-radius protected zone; civilians inside gain +3 defense bonus, lasts 1 scene, cost 3 CE.
- **Flash Step**: Teleport up to 10m (line of sight), cost 2 CE, usable as reaction.

#### Black Flash (黑閃)

| Field | Content |
|------|------|
| Trigger | On a melee attack check, if a natural 10 is rolled (crit/bonus-success die) and the hit lands within 0.000001 sec of physical and CE impact overlapping |
| Effect | That attack's damage ×2.5, DP+2 allocatable to any combat check, lasts 1 round |
| Mechanic | In any melee attack check, rolling 10 with 2+ successes may be declared "Black Flash" — damage = (2d6 + Power + CE reinforcement) × 2.5. The user gains "Black Flash Awakening" next round: all checks DP+2, can trigger again |
| Notes | The strongest melee mechanic in Jujutsu Kaisen — Yuji Itadori once landed four consecutive Black Flashes (record holder) |

#### Reverse Technique (術式反轉)

Reverse Cursed Energy via "negative × negative = positive" to produce positive energy (healing). Requires Resolve + Control check (DC 3) to maintain focus:
- **Heal**: cost 3 CE, restore 1d8 + Control HP
- **Damage to curses**: positive energy deals 3d6 to curses (curses cannot be immune)

#### Domain Expansion (領域展開)

The strongest ability in Jujutsu Kaisen — materialize your inner world as a closed space where all attacks are guaranteed to hit.

| Field | Content |
|------|------|
| Mechanic | Expander makes a Resolve + Control contest vs all inside. If expander wins: all attacks inside are "guaranteed hit" (no dodge), CE checks DP+5. If loser: domain neutralized by "Domain Amplification," no bonus either side |
| Cost | 10 CE (one-time), domain lasts 1d6+2 rounds |
| Limit | Each person may expand at most 1 domain per script |
| Counter | (1) Expand your own domain for domain clash (same cost); (2) Use "Domain Amplification" (simple domain) — small CE-free space around self, lowers enemy domain checks DP-3; (3) Attack from outside — domains have boundaries, external attacks can break the wall |
| Notes | **Even if conditions are met, a Reincarnator may expand only 1 domain during the incident. Design your own domain effect — it must not exceed the "CE DP+5 + guaranteed hit" frame** |

### GM Memo

- **Common Stuck Point 1**: Players may not grasp CE and domain mechanics — fix: in S01 Yuji gives a short demo (CE reinforcement shatters stone / CE barrier blocks a curse). The Biscuit-style lecture doesn't fit Shibuya's tight pace; use "learn while fighting."
- **Common Stuck Point 2**: Domain Expansion is too complex — simplify to "enemy domain: attacks DP+5 and undodgeable, but breakable from outside." Players needn't fully understand Gojo's and enemies' domain details — just that "domain = guaranteed-hit zone."
- **Difficulty Up**: (1) Gojo sealed at round 15 (default 30); (2) Mahito modifies 1 civilian every 3 rounds (default 5); (3) all three Special-Grades successfully expand round 1 (default interfere); (4) civilians reduced to 30 (harder protection).
- **Difficulty Down**: (1) Gojo's seal delayed to 60 rounds (lots of prep); (2) Jogo and Hanami can't expand domains in civilian zones (need focus — civilian negativity destabilizes them); (3) college reinforcements arrive at 60 min (extra NPC power); (4) Mahito fixates on Yuji, not civilians.
- **Player Unconventional Actions**: (1) If players try to bring civilians out through the Veil with Gojo — remind them the Veil blocks ordinary people leaving (including civilians sorcerers carry out); (2) If players try to destroy the Prison Realm's physical form — remind them it's not physical, but the materialization of a seal technique — strikes useless, but CE Perception finds the technique core; (3) If players try to persuade pseudo-Geto to spare Gojo — not an option. Pseudo-Geto (actually Kenjaku) has a millennia-old goal, immovable by words. But players may try to expose he's not the real Geto — may shake the curse side's cooperation (Jogo and Hanami think they're working with Geto).
