## 二、Story Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> A VR death game you cannot log out of — Aincrad. Reincarnators (轮回者) and 10,000 players are trapped in the 100-floor floating castle; the death game begins. Clear to floor 50 and survive — that is the Lord God's mission.

### Full Story Background

November 6, 2022: the world's first VRMMORPG, Sword Art Online, launches. But its creator Kayaba Akihiko (avatar "Heathcliff," raid-group leader) announces to all players: the logout button is gone, clearing the game is the only way out — death in-game means the NERvGear's high-power microwave destroys your real brain.

10,000 players are trapped in the 100-floor steel floating castle "Aincrad." Each floor is guarded by a Floor Boss; defeating it opens the path upward. The clearing group pushes the front lines while non-combat players live daily life in rear towns.

The Reincarnators enter on day 3 of the game — shortly after SAO's opening. At the central plaza of the Town of Beginnings, players are slowly organizing from initial panic. But the floor 1 raid has not yet begun.

Core challenges:
- **Death Game**: HP zero = real death, no revive.
- **Floor Raiding**: Must clear 50 floors; each Boss is a trial.
- **Player Society**: red-guilds, info brokers, complex clearing-group interactions.
- **Resource Management**: limited potions, sword-skill recovery lag, equipment durability.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Floor 1 (S01–S03) advances scene by scene (unified raid start); then, by Reincarnator choice, parallel scenes (S04–S05) trigger at specific Boss floors; floor 50 (S06) is the forced finale.

## 五、World Settings

### SAO Game System (World Special Rule)

In this world all Reincarnator abilities convert to the SAO system. The Lord God seals all external items and enhancements, keeping only raw body attributes. All combat uses SAO's Sword Skill system.

#### Sword Skill System

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Activate | Standard action; pick an equipped sword skill for an attack check. On hit, skill enters cooldown (recovery lag) 1 round |
| Hit | Power / Agility + Melee (SAO) check vs enemy Agility + Defense check |
| Recovery Lag | Next round after a sword skill, the character cannot use active skills and Defense check DP-2 |
| Switch | Quick position-swap tactic with a teammate: Agility + Athletics check, DC 2; success swaps places with an adjacent teammate and transfers aggro. Failure = 1 round lag |
| Combo Sword Skill | Two+ characters' consecutive switch attacks: after first hits during lag, second replaces attack DC-1 (target's defense reaction too slow) |

#### HP and Death

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| HP Calc | (Endurance × 4) + (character level × 2) + equipment bonus |
| HP Zero | Instant real death — NERvGear releases high-power microwave, irreversible brain damage |
| Recovery Crystal | Max 2 per combat. Instantly restores 2d8 + Endurance HP |
| Safe Zone | Towns are SAO safe zones (no PK, HP slowly auto-recovers — Endurance-value HP per hour) |

#### Floor Transfer Gate

- Behind each floor Boss room is a staircase to the floor above.
- After passing, a "Transfer Gate" auto-activates — teleport from any floor's main-town plaza to the first safe zone of unlocked floors.
- No reversion — once teleported down from a floor's gate, must redo the labyrinth.

#### Equipment Tiers

| Tier | Condition | Drop Floor |
|------|---------|---------|
| Copper | D-tier — normal mobs | floors 1–10 |
| Iron | C-tier — mid mobs & side bosses | floors 10–25 |
| Steel | B-tier — elites & low-chance floor bosses | floors 25–40 |
| Silver | B–A tier — rare boss drops | floors 40–50 |
| Legendary | A-tier — unique boss only drop | specific floors (explore) |
