## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-line Summary

> Reincarnators (RC) arrive in the Lands Between as Tarnished, in the devastated world after the Shattering, to defeat Godrick the Grafted — ruler of Limgrave — and claim the first Great Rune.

### Full Story Background

After the Elden Ring shattered, the offspring of the goddess Marika — the demigods — waged a devastating war over the Ring's fragments (the Great Runes). This is "The Shattering." After the war, the Lands Between became a war-scorched wasteland; the demigods each hid in their domains, guarding the Great Rune fragments they seized.

Guided by "Grace," the Reincarnators come to the Lands Between — they are Tarnished, once exiled but now recalled, bearing the mission to repair the Elden Ring. Their journey begins in Limgrave — once beautiful land now ruled by the demigod "Godrick the Grafted."

Godrick's stronghold — Stormveil Castle — sits atop a cliff north of Limgrave. The road to the castle is guarded by Margit the Fell Omen. Godrick is a twisted tyrant — he pursues power by grafting others' limbs onto himself (hence "the Grafted"). He holds the first Great Rune — the key to repairing the Elden Ring.

Core conflict: cross Limgrave's dangerous wilds, defeat the gatekeeper Margit, breach Stormveil Castle, defeat Godrick in his lair, and seize the Great Rune.

### Story Structure Type

Act-by-act: depart from the Limgrave grace point → cross Stormhill to face Margit → storm Stormveil Castle → defeat Godrick the Grafted and seize the Great Rune.

### World Special Rules

- **Tarnished Mechanic**: After death in the Lands Between, Reincarnators revive at the nearest grace point — but each death consumes 1 revival (total 3). 4th death = permanent death (quest failure). After revival HP restores to full, but all unspent Runes (XP currency) are lost.
- **Grace Points**: Golden-light rest points. At a grace point you can: ① restore HP and FP (magic points) ② upgrade Attributes ③ travel to other discovered grace points. More discovered grace points = more exploration reward.
- **Spirit Horse Torrent**: A ghostly steed summonable at will (costs 1 action — summon/dismiss). While mounted move speed ×2 but combat DP-1 (mounted combat less precise than on foot). Cannot ride in Boss fights.
- **Runes**: Currency/XP gained from defeating enemies. Used at grace points to upgrade (each level needs progressively more Runes). On death lose all Runes; must return to the death location after revival to retrieve.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Revealing the existence of the Main God space to story characters is forbidden"
- "Any ability may be used in this world — but when cast it is treated as a unique Ash of War or Incantation"
- "Do not attack friendly NPCs (e.g. Melina) — violation loses all grace guidance"
- "If you surrender to Godrick and hand over Runes — treated as betraying the mission, quest failure"
