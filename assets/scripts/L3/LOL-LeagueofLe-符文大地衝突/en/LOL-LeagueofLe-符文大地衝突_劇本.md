## 二、Story Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> Runeterra faces invasion by the Noxus Empire. Reincarnators (轮回者) are dragged into the regional conflict between Demacia and Noxus, must choose amid the flames of war, and stop the Void rift from opening.

### Full Story Background

Runeterra is a world of magic and conflict. The Noxus Empire launches a massive invasion aimed at conquering the Kingdom of Demacia.

But a greater threat brews behind the war — a Void rift accidentally opens near the battlefield, and Void creatures begin pouring into the real world. The Noxian military tries to weaponize the Void's power but cannot control this destructive force.

Reincarnators intervene as a third party; the Lord God requires them to join the regional conflict and survive. The true challenge is surviving under the dual threat of the Noxian army and the Void creatures.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Open free exploration, turning to scene-by-scene progression in the mid-late game.

### World Special Rules

- **Mana Field**: Using magic in Demacia territory requires an extra Concentration check (DC 3). Noxus zones have no such limit.
- **Void Corruption**: Within 10 min of the rift, Endurance + Resolve check (DC 3); failure deals 1d4 mental damage and grants a stack of "Void Corruption."
- **Faction Reputation**: Actions affect standing with Demacia / Noxus. Too low may be treated as hostile.

### Lord God Restrictions

- Forbidden to directly attack civilians and non-combatants
- Forbidden to reveal the Lord God Space to story characters
- Leaving the border war zone for over 1 hr = mission failure
