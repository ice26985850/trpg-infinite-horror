## 二、劇情概要（GM 限定）

### One-Line Summary

> A deadly death curse is devouring the world—all who were ever resurrected begin to rot and perish. The Reincarnators (輪迴者) must delve into the Chult jungle, find the curse's source—Acererak's Soulmonger—and end the extinction.

### Full Story Background

The Chult jungle is a perilous tropical rainforest where giant snakes, dinosaurs, and worse things roam.
Deep in the jungle, the lich Acererak (阿瑟瑞克) built a gigantic dungeon called the "Tomb of the Nine Gods."
Within it he placed the Soulmonger—an artifact that devours souls, and is the cause of the "death curse."

Anyone who was ever resurrected is slowly rotting; souls can no longer be recalled to this world.
The Lord God (主神) casts the Reincarnators (輪迴者) into Port Nyanzaru, the port city of the Chult jungle,
tasking them with crossing the jungle within 10 days, finding the tomb's entrance, piercing the trap maze, defeating Acererak,
destroying the Soulmonger, and ending the death curse.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Opens with free exploration, shifts to act-by-act progression in the mid-to-late game.

### World Special Rules

- **Death Curse Effect**: Any character ever resurrected loses 1 max HP every 24 hours in the jungle. This effect cannot be removed by normal healing. Restored after destroying the Soulmonger.
- **Jungle Disease**: Traveling in the jungle each day requires an Endurance DC2 disease check. Failure infects jungle fever (all actions DP-1, until treated).
- **Insect Cloud**: Some areas are filled with venomous insect clouds. In an insect cloud, take 1d4 poison damage per round; requires Intellect DC3 to find insect-repelling plants.

### Lord God (主神) Restrictions

- Flying abilities beyond medieval level forbidden (jungle curse causes flight to fail)
- Direct teleport forbidden—jungle magic barrier blocks teleport spells
- Revealing Acererak's true name to locals forbidden (triggers surveillance magic)
