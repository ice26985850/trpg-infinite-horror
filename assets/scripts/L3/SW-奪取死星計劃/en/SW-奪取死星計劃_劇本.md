## 二、劇情概要（GM 限定）

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者) infiltrate the Empire's ultimate weapon—the Death Star—to help the Rebel Alliance seize the Death Star blueprints from the Imperial data center and escape under the pursuit of Darth Vader.

### Full Story Background

Under the rule of Emperor Palpatine, the Galactic Empire built a superweapon capable of destroying entire planets—the Death Star. Upon learning of this threat, the Rebel Alliance dispatched its finest agents to steal the Death Star plans and find its weakness.

The Reincarnators (輪迴者) enter the world as newly recruited special operatives of the Rebel Alliance. They are sent inside the Death Star—a spherical space station 160 km in diameter garrisoned by hundreds of thousands of Imperial Stormtroopers, AT-ST walkers, and the Empire's ultimate weapon: Darth Vader, a Jedi who fell to the dark side.

The timeline is set between *Rogue One* and *Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope*—the Death Star plans are being transmitted to the Rebels, but the Empire still holds a complete backup. The Reincarnators' (輪迴者) mission is to infiltrate the Imperial data center, destroy or seize the backup plans, and withdraw alive.

Core conflict: Sneak through the high-alert interior of the Death Star, avoid or defeat Stormtrooper patrols, obtain the Death Star plans from the data center, then face Darth Vader himself—who has already noticed the intruders.

### Story Structure Type

Hybrid: Opens with a mission briefing and equipment prep at the Rebel hidden base → mid-game infiltration of the Death Star interior with free choice of渗透 route → finale Boss fight: against Darth Vader in the Superlaser Reactor control room while ensuring successful data transmission.

### World Special Rules

- **Blaster Weapon System**: Blasters deal energy damage (not physical piercing). Hits on metal surfaces ricochet—armor-piercing (ignores 2 points of base defense). Blaster rifle (E-11): 1d8+1 energy damage, range 30/60m
- **Force Sense**: Darth Vader can sense the Reincarnators' (輪迴者) presence through the Force. Each time a Reincarnator (輪迴者) makes a check (DC3+) or uses Gene Lock (基因鎖), Vader's Force-sense check gains +1 DP (cumulative). At cumulative DP5, Vader pinpoints their location and comes directly
- **Imperial Alarm System**: Alarm level 0–5. Level 0 = normal patrol; triggering combat or an unsilenced kill → +1 level; loud explosion → +2 levels. Each level adds 1 extra patrol group. Level 5 = shipwide lockdown, all exits auto-close
- **Lightsaber Combat**: Lightsabers ignore all defenses except energy shields. Lightsaber-vs-lightsaber parry is an opposed check (D10 dice-pool opposed)
