## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Sentence Summary

> The Reincarnators (RC), as Jianghu (martial world) heroes, ascend Bright Peak. Amid the life-and-death siege of the Ming Cult by the Six Major Sects, they assist the Ming Cult in withstanding the assault and expose Cheng Kun's conspiracy.

### Full Plot Background

In the late Yuan Dynasty, the six major Jianghu sects (Shaolin, Wudang, Emei, Kunlun, Huashan, Kongtong) join forces to besiege the Ming Cult's headquarters at Bright Peak, vowing to eradicate this martial faction regarded as the "Demonic Cult." Yet behind this siege lies a earth-shaking conspiracy — Cheng Kun (Hunyuan Thunder Palm) , to avenge the former Ming Cult leader Yang Dingtian, has lurked in many places sowing discord between the Six Sects and the Ming Cult, single-handedly orchestrating this martial-world catastrophe.

The Reincarnators emerge from the Ming Cult's secret passage at the rear of Bright Peak — the Main God (MG) has arranged them as the Ming Cult's hidden allies. At this point the Six Sects are already attacking up the mountain from all directions; the Ming Cult's high command, led by the White-Browed Eagle King Yin Tianzheng, fights desperately but suffers heavy casualties. Zhang Wuji, having only recently learned the Qiankun Great Shift from the secret passage, is not yet able to fully join the battle.

Core Conflict: Hold the Ming Cult headquarters for at least 8 rounds under the combined siege of the Six Sects (waiting for Zhang Wuji to finish his secluded healing and arrive with reinforcements), while preventing Cheng Kun from assassinating the Ming Cult's high command, and ultimately helping Zhang Wuji resolve the Jianghu feud.

### Plot Structure Type

Scene-by-scene: Emerge from the Ming Cult secret passage → Observe the battlefield situation → Choose a defensive route → 8-round headquarters defense battle → Zhang Wuji's reinforcements arrive → Final BOSS battle (Cheng Kun).

### World Special Rules

- **Inner Energy (Qi) System**: All Jianghu fighters in this world possess an Inner Energy value (Qi Pool) — equivalent to a Willpower pool. Using martial arts techniques consumes Inner Energy. Inner Energy can be restored through meditation.
- **Martial Arts System**: Attack checks use a D10 dice pool. Each style has specific moves — the move's DP bonus depends on proficiency in that art.
- **Six Sects' Traits**: Shaolin (ferocious melee — Vajra Palm / staff technique), Wudang (soft counter-attacks — Taiji Sword / using softness to overcome hardness), Emei (agile assassination — Emei Swordplay / hidden weapons), Kunlun (two-person coordination — Liangyi Sword Formation), Huashan (sinister and poisonous — Xian Yutong's unique poison needles), Kongtong (heavy strikes — Seven Injuries Fist, hurts self before hurting others)
- **Ming Cult Five-Element Banners**: After round 8, the Five-Element Banners (Flame Banner, Flood Banner, etc.) join as reinforcements, each with a 5-person squad to assist defense

### Main God (MG) Restrictions

- "Prohibited from revealing the existence of the Main God Space to plot characters."
- "Any enhancement ability may be used in this world — but when deployed it is regarded as a strange martial art."
- "Do not kill Ming Cult high command — violators are deemed to be assisting the Six Sects, mission failure."
- "Do not voluntarily leave the Bright Peak area."
