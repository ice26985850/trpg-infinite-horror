## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-line Summary

> Reincarnators (RC) accidentally No-Clip into the Backrooms and must cross from Level 2 (Pipeline Nightmare) through Level 37 (Submerged Hall) and finally sprint to the finish in Level ! (Endless Corridor) to escape alive.

### Full Story Background

The Backrooms is a labyrinthine world of infinitely extending rooms, corridors, and extradimensional spaces — a dimension behind reality. People may "No-Clip" into the Backrooms for various reasons, and once inside, escape is extremely difficult.

This script sets the Reincarnators accidentally No-Clipping in during a mission, plummeting directly into Level 2 — also called the "Pipeline Nightmare" — a maze of endless concrete corridors and steam pipes. The air is hot and humid, filled with the roar of machinery. It is home to Smilers — predatory entities that bare ghastly pale grins in the dark.

Finding the way out of Level 2 leads to Level 37 — the "Submerged Hall" — a half-flooded opulent hotel lobby. The water carries an aquatic plague (environmental hazard — contact with contaminated water infects one with Backrooms disease). One must swiftly find the exit teleport point in the water.

Finally the Reincarnators fall into Level ! — the Backrooms' most infamous nightmare: a one-way-only endless corridor. Behind them surges a wave of chaser entities. They must sprint with all their might to the portal at the corridor's end — any pause may be fatal.

Core conflict: a three-level consecutive crossing — each level has different environmental hazards (steam burns → water plague → pursuit), resources steadily drain, and pressure keeps mounting.

### Story Structure Type

Act-by-act: Level 2 pipeline exploration to find the exit → Level 37 wading to search for the teleport point → Level ! endless corridor sprint to the finish.

### World Special Rules

- **Backrooms Rules**: No traditional physical laws — space may switch arbitrarily between levels. Almond Water is the universal resource: restores HP, clears mental anomalies, even tradable.
- **Level Crossing**: Moving from one level to the next requires finding a specific teleport marker (usually an abnormal wall crack or flickering light point). Teleport is one-way — no going back.
- **Aquatic Plague**: The Level 37 water contains a Backrooms-specific pathogen. Contact requires END save DC2 each round; failure → infection (1d4 damage per hour, lasts 6 hours). Almond Water cures it.
- **Level ! Rules**: A corridor where you can only advance, never retreat. The entity wave behind advances 3 cells per round. Random obstacles may appear ahead (GM secretly rolls each round: 1=none, 2=small obstacle DC2, 3=medium obstacle DC3, 4=large obstacle DC4, 5=door enterable DC4 check, 6=supply pickup).

### Main God Restrictions

- "Revealing the existence of the Main God space to story characters is forbidden" (no story characters in the Backrooms — but applies when meeting other travelers)
- "Any ability or item may be used in this world"
- "Do not attempt to stay in the Backrooms — the Main God continuously applies mental pressure"
