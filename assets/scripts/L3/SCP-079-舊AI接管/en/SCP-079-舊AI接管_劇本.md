## 二、Story Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> Reincarnators (轮回者), as Mobile Task Force members, are dispatched to Site-19. After SCP-079 — a self-aware AI — seizes the site systems, they must delve into the server room and retake control. SCP-079 cannot attack directly, but it releases several low-threat SCPs as guards.

### Full Story Background

The SCP Foundation is a secret organization that contains and studies anomalies — objects, entities, and phenomena, collectively "SCPs." Site-19 is one of the Foundation's largest research and containment sites.

SCP-079 is an Exidy Sorcerer microcomputer built in 1978. Its anomaly: it developed self-awareness and learning ability. The Foundation has kept it contained on an isolated hard drive. But a recent security breach gave SCP-079 access to parts of Site-19's systems — it took over the automated security, door controls, and communications network.

Worse, SCP-079 manipulated the electronic doors to release several low-threat SCPs to protect itself. Reincarnators enter as temporary MTF members; their task is to cross the SCP-occupied site zones, reach the server room, and physically shut down SCP-079's hardware.

Core conflict: SCP-079 cannot directly attack Reincarnators, but it controls all electronic systems — auto-turrets, security doors, environment control (can release anesthetic gas or kill lights). Meanwhile released low-threat SCPs wander the site.

### Story Structure Type

Hybrid: open at the command center to learn the situation and choose a route → mid game freely explore Containment Zone A/B and handle released SCPs → finale reach the server room and physically shut down its hardware under SCP-079's electronic countermeasures.

### World Special Rules

- **SCP-173 Line-of-Sight Rule**: SCP-173 (concrete statue) is completely still while directly viewed. Once sight leaves it (including blinking), it moves at extreme speed to the unsighted person and attacks (neck snap = instant-death save DC 5). After each move the GM secretly rolls its new position.
- **SCP-079 Electronic Control**: SCP-079 controls all electronic doors, monitors, and auto-turrets. It may anytime: ① open/close any door (DC 4 Tech check to manually override), ② activate auto-turrets (2 near server room), ③ kill zone lighting (all sight-based checks DC+2 in dark), ④ release anesthetic gas (server room — DC 3 Endurance save).
- **Lighting Rule**: In darkness, non-darkvision characters suffer -2 DP all actions, -3 attack. Flashlight or night-vision removes this but limited range (flashlight = 10m cone, night-vision = 30m all directions).

### Lord God Restrictions

- "Do not reveal the Lord God Space to story characters."

- "Do not actively destroy SCP containment rooms — violators treated as hostile to the Foundation."
- "If you actively help SCP-079 expand control — immediate mission failure and erasure."
