## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-line Summary

> Reincarnators (RC) enter the Dou Qi Continent as alchemists. Learning the location of the Strange Fire "Green Lotus Core Flame," they descend into the underground lava world to seize it, while evading the pursuit of Hall of Souls envoys.

### Full Story Background

The Dou Qi Continent is a world whose power system is "Dou Qi" — nine tiers from Dou Zhe to Dou Di. On this continent, "Strange Fires" are the rarest existence — flame spirits naturally born of heaven and earth, each with world-destroying power. If an alchemist refines a Strange Fire, their pill-craft leaps qualitatively.

This script is set on the desert border of the Gama Empire — the Alchemist Guild receives intel that the legendary Strange Fire "Green Lotus Core Flame" has been found in the underground lava world deep in the Tagor Desert. This fire ranks 19th on the Strange Fire list — not the strongest, but a priceless treasure to any alchemist.

However — the mysterious dark organization "Hall of Souls" is also tracking this fire. The Hall of Souls is one of the most feared powers on the continent, specializing in capturing powerful souls and collecting Strange Fires. They have dispatched envoys to the Tagor Desert, intending to seize the fire before the Alchemist Guild.

The Reincarnators join the expedition as mercenaries specially recruited by the Alchemist Guild — their task is to find and refine the Green Lotus Core Flame before the Hall of Souls, then escape the Hall's pursuit with the fire.

### Story Structure Type

Act-by-act: desert-border preparation → descend into the underground lava world → Strange Fire refining ritual → Hall of Souls interception Boss fight → escape pursuit.

### World Special Rules

- **Dou Qi System**: The power tiers of the Dou Qi Continent — Dou Zhe → Dou Shi → Da Dou Shi → Dou Ling → Dou Wang → Dou Huang → Dou Zong → Dou Zun → Dou Sheng → Dou Di. Reincarnators' Attributes roughly correspond to Dou Ling through Dou Huang level.
- **Strange Fire Refining**: Refining a Strange Fire requires 6 rounds of sustained checks (INT + Alchemy DC4). Each failed round triggers rampage risk — Rampage check DC3 (uses Will save). Perfect refine (no failures) → achieves optional objective ③. After refining, the fire can be used in combat (fire damage 3d6+5, ignores non-magical defense).
- **Hall of Souls Traits**: Hall of Souls envoys use dark Dou Qi — their attacks carry soul-corrosion (each hit requires Will save DC3 or take 1d4 soul damage — soul damage only recovers through rest). Hall members wear bone masks — their identity mark.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Revealing the existence of the Main God space to story characters is forbidden"
- "Any enhancement ability may be used in this world — but when cast it is treated as a special Dou technique"
- "If you actively hand the Strange Fire to the Hall of Souls — immediate quest failure and Erasure"
- "Do not attack Alchemist Guild members"
