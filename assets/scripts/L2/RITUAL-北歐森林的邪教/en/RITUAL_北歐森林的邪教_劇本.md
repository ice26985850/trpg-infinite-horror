## II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者) awaken in a Nordic primeval forest — hunted by cultists, they must find a way out.

### Full Plot Background

They awaken in an abandoned cabin in a Nordic primeval forest. Cultists worship the ancient forest deity "Jötunn," offering lost travelers as sacrifices. No map, no supplies, full of traps. Deep within lurks an undead Lich.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**

### World Special Rules

- **Forest Navigation**: Perception + Survival DC2. Three consecutive successes find the exit.
- **Cultist Tracking**: After each encounter, their numbers +1.
- **Weather**: A check every 2 hours; rain/fog reduces visibility; thunderstorms may start fires.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Do not reveal the Main God Space"
- "No GPS / modern technology"
