## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者) enter the Final Selection on Mt. Fujikasane — the Taisho-era Demon Slayer Corps entrance exam. Survive seven days among man-eating demons, slay enough of them, and reach the wisteria-safe summit to prove worthy of becoming a Demon Slayer.

### Full Plot Background

In the Taisho era, demons bred by Muzan Kibutsuji ravage Japan. The Demon Slayer Corps — swordsmen who fight demons with "Breathing Styles" — holds the Final Selection each year on Mt. Fujikasane: candidates must survive seven days in a mountain full of demons. The demons here are low-rank ones captured by the Corps and imprisoned, starved and savage.

The Reincarnators arrive at dusk on the first day — Tanjiro, Zenitsu, Inosuke and the other canon leads are among this batch of candidates. Brought to the mountain foot and told the rules, the candidates scatter into the woods.

The challenge: demons cannot be killed at night (without a sun blade or sunlight), and most Reincarnators lack Breathing Styles — they must rely on tactics, cooperation, and the canon characters' help to survive. Seven days of day-night cycles, each with different threats and chances.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: daytime free exploration (supplies, rest, meet candidates); nighttime act-by-act (demon encounters, hold the position). Seven day-night cycles form the spine.

### World Special Rules

- **Day-night cycle**: by day (06:00–18:00) all demons retreat to shadow and won't attack. By night (18:00–06:00) they prowl. Each night passed raises encounter chance and count (+20%/night, cap +100%).
- **Breathing Styles**: this world's unique combat art. Reincarnators may learn from NPCs (need enough favor, see NPC section) or redeem from the Lord God on return.
  - **Water Breathing**: Agility (DEX) + Melee check DP+2; attacks gain "flow" (bypass some defense).
  - **Thunder Breathing**: initiative +5; first hit adds 1d4 lightning damage.
  - **Beast Breathing**: Strength (STR) + Brawl or Melee check DP+2; attacks inflict "rend" (extra 1 bleed damage/round for 3 rounds).
  - Learning: favor ≥3 with the matching NPC, spend 4 daytime hours training. Intellect (INT) + Athletics or Melee check, DC3.
- **Sun Blade**: a special-metal sword, ×2 damage to demons. Ordinary weapons deal half (rounded down) to demons.
- **Sunlight**: demons die instantly in sunlight (no check). Sunlight is the only kill method needing no sun blade.
- **Wisteria**: demons' natural repellent. Carrying wisteria lowers a demon's action DP by 2 within 10 ft. Wisteria poison on a weapon (spends 1 wisteria) adds 1d4 toxin damage per hit on demons, 3 rounds.
- **Stamina management**: every 24 hours without rest requires a Stamina (STA) check, DC2; failure gives −2 DP to all actions until 4 hours' rest.

### Lord God's Restrictions

- Do not reveal the Lord God Space to candidates or Corps members.
- No firearms or modern high-tech weapons (Taisho-era tech level).
- Using non-native abilities (magic, tech gear) before in-world characters costs 500 each time.
- Sun blades cannot leave this world (unless redeemed from the Lord God).
- Reincarnators may not attack hiding demons outside wisteria zones by day (violates Corps rules).
