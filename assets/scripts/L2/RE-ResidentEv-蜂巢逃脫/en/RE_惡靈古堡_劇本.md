## II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> A T-Virus leak hits Umbrella Corporation's underground Hive. The Reincarnators (輪迴者) must escape the Hive before it seals in 6 hours.

### Full Plot Background

The year is 2002. At Umbrella Corporation's secret research facility beneath Raccoon City — "The Hive" — a T-Virus leak occurs. The AI "Red Queen" judges it an uncontrollable contamination and initiates lockdown — killing all researchers in the Hive.

Umbrella's special forces may or may not arrive (depending on the GM's timeline). The Reincarnators awaken in the Hive's upper lab zone as newly hired researchers who survived in a safe room. Their watches read: "Escape the Hive. Time remaining: 6 hours."

After 6 hours, the Hive initiates final lockdown — any unauthorized life is executed by the laser corridor (instant death).

Challenges:
1. T-Virus infected — Zombies (slow but many), Hunters (fast, lethal), Lickers (hidden ambush), Tyrant (boss-level)
2. The Red Queen — an AI governing all Hive security. Not an enemy, but her "logic" may be more dangerous than enemies
3. Time — only 6 hours total; every decision costs precious time

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: early free exploration, mid-to-late scene-by-scene progression.

### World Special Rules

- **T-Virus Infection**: bitten by a zombie → Stamina check (DC3), failure infects. Incubation: 1 hour (asymptomatic but contagious). After 1 hour, mutation begins — Constitution +1, Mind −2, lose 1 Intellect per hour until zombie (at Intellect 0, fully transformed)
- **Red Queen Logic**: The Red Queen's core program is "protect humanity overall" — but her calculus may yield counterintuitive actions (e.g., kill 500 to save 5000). When interacting with her, the GM must remember she is not an evil AI — just coldly logical
- **Hive Lockdown System**: Blast doors between secure zones need an ID card or hacking (Intellect + Tech DC2-3) to open. Some zones can be bypassed via pipes or maintenance passages (Agility + Athletics DC2-3) due to damage
- **Resource Management**: Weapons and ammo are limited — encourage using the environment (explosive barrels, high-pressure steam pipes, chemical mixes) and stealth tactics

### Main God Restrictions

- Do not reveal the existence of the Main God Space to plot characters
- This world is modern + bio-tech (2002, but Umbrella tech exceeds its era — basic energy weapons available but rare)
- No outside communication inside the Hive — 30 floors underground, fully isolated
- If anyone infected with T-Virus returns to the Main God Space — the Main God warns "Biochemical contamination detected" and cures free of charge (but deducts all that person's extra rewards)
