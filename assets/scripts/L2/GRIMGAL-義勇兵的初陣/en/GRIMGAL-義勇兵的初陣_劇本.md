## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者), as volunteer soldiers in Grimgar, complete three hunt missions — from goblins to orcs — surviving in a grim, grounded isekai.

### Full Plot Background

Grimgar — a world that makes the isekai "real." No flashy reincarnation cheats, no overpowered gifts. The Reincarnators serve as volunteer soldiers of Orthodar, surviving by their own strength like ordinary soldiers. Even weak goblins are a deadly threat to rookies — because here, death is death.

### Plot Structure Type

**Act-by-act**.

### World Special Rules

- **Grounded isekai**: no reincarnation perks — everything must be learned. Skills are learned from NPC trainers (500 + 1D1 each).
- **Death is permanent**: volunteer-soldier NPC allies truly die — affecting morale and later mission difficulty.
- **Food and rest**: after each hunt, return to town to resupply and rest. No rest → fatigue accumulates (−1 DP per unrested hunt).

### Lord God's Restrictions

- Do not reveal the Lord God Space.
- This world is medieval tech — no modern weapons.
- Volunteer corps reputation affects NPC interaction.
