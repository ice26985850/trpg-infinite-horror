## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> In 845, the Colossal Titan breaches the Wall Maria. The Reincarnators (輪迴者), dropped in as fresh Survey Corps recruits, fight the Battle of Trost District — surviving among the Titans, protecting civilians, and helping seal the breached wall.

### Full Plot Background

In 845, humanity had lived a century of peace behind three great walls (Maria, Rose, Sheena). That day, an unseen Colossal Titan — a 60-meter colossus — appeared outside Wall Maria and kicked the gate apart. Titans poured through the gap, and Wall Maria fell.

The Reincarnators arrive about two hours after the breach. The Lord God drops them as new recruits of the Survey Corps, inside Trost District — the forward salient of Wall Maria. The district is in chaos: Titans hunt everywhere, civilians flee in panic, soldier morale is collapsing.

The challenge here is that Titans are not "enemies" but an "environmental disaster." A single 3-meter Titan's raw strength far outmatches an ordinary mortal; a head-on brawl is suicide. The real path to survival is the Omni-Directional Mobility Gear (ODM Gear), tactical coordination, terrain use, and judgment at the crucial moment.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: opens with assembly on the wall and a mission briefing, then turns into free tactical street fighting, climaxing in the wall-breach sealing battle.

### World Special Rules

- **ODM Gear operation**: 3D movement with ODM Gear requires Agility (DEX) + Athletics check.
  - Basic movement: DC2 — success triples move (90 ft/round).
  - Sharp turn / hard stop: DC3.
  - Aerial nape strike: DC3 — success grants +5 DP (dive bonus) and the Titan cannot block with base defense.
  - Failure: cable misses, re-aim next round. Critical fail (a 1 with no successes): cable tangles, lose 1 round.
- **Gas limit**: Each ODM Gear has 10 uses (gas canister, ~10 combat rounds). Refill at supply point (round trip ~2 rounds, no encounter).
- **Titan weakness — nape**: The only fatal weak point is the nape (≈1m wide, ≈10cm deep). Nape attack: DP −3 (ground) or DP +5 (air). Hit the nape for 15+ damage = death.
- **Titan regeneration**: Non-nape damage heals 5 HP/round. Limb hits only briefly weaken, never kill.
- **Building destruction**: A Titan destroys a small building (wood) in 1 action; a mid-size stone building in 3; a large stone building (e.g. church) in 5.

### Lord God's Restrictions

- Do not reveal the Lord God Space to in-world characters.
- No flying devices or energy weapons above this world's tech level (ODM Gear is native tech and allowed).
- Using supernatural abilities (non-native system) in front of in-world characters costs 500 each time.
- Do not directly attack the Colossal Titan (★★★★★) — it is plot-scripted and appears only as background.

- **GM Memo**:
  - **Stall point 1**: Players don't know ODM Gear — have a veteran NPC demo nape-striking in the first encounter.
  - **Stall point 2**: Players fear Titans and won't sortie — pressure via Levi's authority ("want to live? then stop shaking"), or show civilian casualties to spark responsibility. Despair is the best fuel.
  - **Stall point 3**: Supply-point wipe — if the party is too weak, have Mikasa or Levi appear at the crux (lowers achievement); alt: divert Wave 3 Abnormal with a loud noise elsewhere.
  - **Stall point 4**: Players don't grasp nape strikes — Levi or a veteran diagrams it at the wall briefing; GM may stage a "lucky soldier hits the nape" demo.
  - **Difficulty up**: more Titans (×1.5 per encounter); add an Armored Titan cameo (hurling boulders); Abnormals become "runner type — initiative +8, 120 ft/round."
  - **Difficulty down**: fewer Titans (−1 per encounter); more gas and blades; more NPC soldiers give effective help.
  - **Unconventional actions**: if players try to "attack the Colossal Titan," warn — it radiates high-heat steam (2d6 fire/round within 100 ft) and the Lord God forbids it. If players try to "flee inland past the wall," warn — the Lord God mission requires sealing the breach; no seal, no return.
