## II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> A massive containment breach hits an SCP Foundation site. The Reincarnators (輪迴者) must escape the site amid the threats of SCP-173 and other anomalies.

### Full Plot Background

The present day. SCP Foundation Site-19 — the world's largest anomalous-item containment site — suffers a massive containment breach. The cause: an internal sabotage operation by the Chaos Insurgency. Multiple researchers killed, security systems fail, dozens of SCP items escape containment.

The most threatening is SCP-173 (the Sculpture - The Original) — a statue of concrete and rebar. When directly observed, it is perfectly still; but the instant eye contact breaks (including blinking), it moves at extreme speed to the nearest target and snaps its neck.

When the Reincarnators enter, the site's emergency lockdown has activated — all zones isolated by heavy blast doors. They awaken in a failed containment chamber, wearing orange D-Class jumpsuits (the Foundation thinks they are newly delivered D-Class).

Challenges:
1. SCP-173 — kills on blinking; requires coordinated team staring
2. Site lockdown — heavy blast doors block the way; need hacking or alternate routes
3. Other breaches — may encounter other SCP items (distraction, threat, or… usable resources?)

### Plot Structure Type

**Act-by-Act**: early free exploration, mid-to-late scene-by-scene progression.

### World Special Rules

- **SCP-173 Mechanism**: When a character directly observes it, SCP-173 is still. When all eye contact breaks (anyone blinks or looks away), it moves instantly (15 tiles/round) to the nearest target and attacks (1d20+10 hit, 4d10+5 blunt, targeting the neck — instant-death save DC20).
- **Blink Rule**: At the start of each round, every character staring at SCP-173 makes a Stamina check (DC2). Failure means an instinctive blink (cannot stare that round). Gene Lock open grants +2 DP against blinking.
- **Coordinated Staring**: Multiple characters stare in rotation (need at least 3 for unbroken staring — 2 is unreliable, 1 impossible). A well-coordinated team can use "Ready Action" to alternate staring the instant someone blinks.
- **Site Self-Destruct**: Can be manually activated in the security control room (60-min countdown), or auto-activates when the site detects full containment failure (happens 8 hours after scene start).
- **Other SCP Threats**: SCP-049 (Plague Doctor — non-hostile but dangerous, touch = instant death), SCP-096 (Shy Guy — seeing its face triggers a hunt), SCP-939 (Many-Voiced — mimics human voices to lure prey).

### Main God Restrictions

- Do not reveal the existence of the Main God Space to plot characters
- No unauthorized comms devices inside the site (triggers security alarm)
- After leaving the site, may only return to the Main God Space from an external evacuation point — not directly from inside the site
