## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者) accidentally Noclip into Level 0 of the Backrooms. They must find the Manila Room exit to Level 1, then locate the M.E.G. outpost in Level 1.

### Full Plot Background

The Backrooms is a hyperdimensional space of endless rooms, corridors, and staircases. Humans can fall into it through "Noclip" — a glitch in reality. Once inside, returning to the real world is nearly impossible.

Level 0 (the Lobby): the entry level. Endless yellow wallpaper, damp carpet, humming fluorescent lights. No physical threats here — but the most dangerous thing is "loneliness." Newcomers wander Level 0 alone, eventually going mad or starving. The Manila Room is the only exit — a normal room with brown wallpaper that leads to Level 1.

Level 1 (the Habitable Zone): slightly better, a vast warehouse-like space. Here the M.E.G. (Major Explorer Group) runs an outpost — food, water, basic protection. But threats exist: the Hound (a humanoid beast) and the Skin-Stealer (a disguised humanoid entity).

The Reincarnators are scattered across different spots in Level 0. They must first find each other (by describing surroundings, listening for sounds), then seek the Manila Room together, enter Level 1, and find the M.E.G. outpost.

### Plot Structure Type

**Free exploration**: open exploration early, scene-by-scene progression through the middle and final acts.

### World Special Rules

- **Sanity system**: the core mechanic. Each Reincarnator starts with Sanity = Resolve ×10. Each hour alone in Level 0 loses 1d4 Sanity. Sanity 0 = mental breakdown (character cannot act, falls into delirium — a teammate must spend 1 hour on a Composure check, Charisma DC3, to restore to 10 Sanity).
- **Almond Water**: restores 1d4 Sanity and 1 HP. In Level 0, a Perception + Investigation DC3 search each hour yields 1 bottle; in Level 1, 1 bottle per 2 hours but with higher encounter risk.
- **Noclip phenomenon**: Level 0's floor occasionally "glitches" — a character may suddenly fall to a deeper level (Level 2 pipe-nightmare — extremely dangerous, avoid). Perception + Intuition DC3 senses unstable areas.
- **Level 1 entity rules**: Hound (humanoid canine beast, Threat Level ★) — 1d2 per encounter, 40-ft sense range, aggressive but driven off by bright light. Skin-Stealer (Threat Level ★★) — disguises as human (Perception + Insight DC4 to see through), reveals true form when attacking.

### Lord God's Restrictions

- Do not reveal the Lord God Space to in-world characters.
- The Backrooms has no day or night — time is tracked only by watch and intuition.
- Any attempt to violently break Level 0 walls/floor only causes a Noclip to deeper levels (very dangerous).
- In Level 1, do not use overly powerful weapons — they attract more entities (gunfire draws within 200 ft).
