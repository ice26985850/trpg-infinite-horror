## II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者) investigate the terrifying truth of the Deep Ones in Innsmouth and try to escape this cursed town.

### Full Plot Background

In 1928, on the New England coast, lies Innsmouth — a forgotten fishing port. Its residents are visibly abnormal: bulging eyes, flattened noses, grey-green skin. The Reincarnators are dropped here by the Main God (主神) to investigate a series of disappearances — several travelers vanished near Innsmouth, the last being a young student named Robert Olmstead. The town hides an unspeakable secret: the Deep One cult, the Order of Dagon, and Cthulhu sleeping on the seabed.

**Hybrid**

### World Special Rules

- **Sanity (SAN)**: This script introduces the SAN concept. Initial SAN = Willpower (WIL) ×5. Each supernatural-terror encounter requires a SAN check (Will save); failure costs 1d4–1d10 SAN. SAN reaching zero → permanent madness.
- **Deep One Disguise**: Most Innsmouth residents carry Deep One blood — identifiable via Perception + Investigation, DC2.
- **Call of the Ocean**: Near the shore, those with low Willpower (WIL ≤2) hear the ocean's whisper — tempting them toward the sea.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Do not reveal the existence of the Main God Space"
- "1928 technology level restriction"
- "Using the supernatural before the cult → immediately triggers a town-wide assault"
