## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者) assist the Claymore warrior Clare to hunt an Awakened Being.

### Full Plot Background

A continent overrun by Yoma. The Organization assigns the Reincarnators to help the Claymore warrior Clare complete an extermination mission — the target is an Awakened Being: a fallen former Claymore.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**.

### World Special Rules

- **Yoma aura sense**: Claymores sense Yoma within 200 ft.
- **Yoma disguise**: betrayed by pupil color.
- **Yoma power release**: DP+4 but requires a Resolve check to avoid Awakening.

### Lord God's Restrictions

- Do not reveal the Lord God.
- No modern weapons.
- Supernatural abilities → Claymores treat you as a threat.
