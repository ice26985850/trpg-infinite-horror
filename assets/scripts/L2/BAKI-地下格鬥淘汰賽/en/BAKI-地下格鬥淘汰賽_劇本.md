## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者) enter Tokyo's underground fighting tournament and fight their way through pure hand-to-hand combat to the finals.

### Full Plot Background

The Tokyo underground arena — the ultimate showdown with no rules and no protective gear. The Reincarnators must defeat every opponent in the bracket to reach the finals.

### Plot Structure Type

**Act-by-act**: tournament rounds → finals.
