## II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者), as Imperial Demon Army soldiers, protect the Shibuya human stronghold in the vampire apocalypse.

### Full Plot Background

The year 2020, vampires rule the world. The Reincarnators are dropped into the Imperial Demon Army's Shibuya stronghold to protect 500 human survivors from attacks by the vampire noble Ferid.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid: stronghold briefing → ruins exploration → defense battle**
