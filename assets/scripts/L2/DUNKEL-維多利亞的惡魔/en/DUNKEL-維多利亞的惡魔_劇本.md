## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> Victorian London — the Reincarnators (輪迴者), as demon hunters, investigate the demon behind a string of serial killings.

### Full Plot Background

London, 1889, the Victorian era. A serial-killer case — bodies carved with symbols, drained of blood, hearts arranged in a pentagram. The secret order "the Demon Hunters' Society" knows the truth: a demon has awakened in London's shadows. From low sewer demons, to a possessed noble MP, up to a Demon Duke.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**.

## VIII. Key Scenes

### Scene Structure Notes

This scenario is **hybrid**: the Society briefing opens, then investigation across London's districts, culminating in the church showdown under the full moon.

### Scene Overview

| Scene | Location | Trigger | Core Content |
|------|------|------|----------|
| S01 | Society Stronghold | Scenario start | Abraham welcomes the party below the bookshop; receive the hunt brief. |
| S02 | Sewer Nest | Track the first scene | Brick tunnels, black slime; a demon swarm guards the first node. |
| S03 | The MP's Mansion | Investigate the noble circle | A lavish mansion; by day a tea party, by night the possessed MP. |
| S04 | Old St. Paul's Church | Full-moon night | A pentagram array, three nodes; the Demon Duke descends. |
