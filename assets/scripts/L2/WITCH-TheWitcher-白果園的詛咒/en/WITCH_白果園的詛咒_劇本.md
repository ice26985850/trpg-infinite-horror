## II. Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators (輪迴者), as witcher apprentices, complete three monster-hunt contracts in White Orchard — Drowners, a Striga, and a Griffin.

### Full Plot Background

The Reincarnators are dropped by the Main God into the world of The Witcher — White Orchard, a war-ravaged remote village. Nilfgaardian troops have just withdrawn, leaving devastation and corpses — which now draw scavenging monsters.

The village chief posts three witcher contracts — Drowners (水鬼) attack fishermen at the riverbank, a Striga (吸血妖鳥) nests in an abandoned tower, and a wounded Griffin (獅鷲獸) hunts travelers deep in the forest. As witcher apprentices, the Reincarnators must complete at least one contract to survive — but completing all three earns the true reward.

This script tests not raw combat power, but preparation. The witcher philosophy: "Know the monster's weakness and you've won half the battle." Intelligence gathering, potion-brewing, and tactical choice are the path to survival.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid: opening village briefing → free exploration (investigation, alchemy, hunting) → finale battle**

### World Special Rules

- **Witcher Potion Toxicity**: Each potion drunk accumulates 1 toxicity point. When toxicity exceeds Constitution (CON), each extra point deals 1 toxin damage per hour.
- **Blade Oil System**: Apply blade oil against specific monster types; gain DP bonus against that type (base +2). Requires Intellect (INT) + Alchemy check, DC1, to brew.
- **Silver Sword vs Steel Sword**: Silver sword deals 2× damage to magic/cursed creatures. Steel sword for humans/beasts.
- **Witcher Medallion Vibration**: Near magic creatures or magic traps, the medallion vibrates (Perception (PER) check identifies direction and intensity).
- **Monster Bestiary**: Successfully identifying a monster (Intellect (INT) + Lore check) reveals its weakness; whole party +1 DP.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Do not reveal the existence of the Main God Space to plot characters"
- "No weapons obviously beyond this world's tech (energy weapons, flying devices)"
- "Using supernatural abilities too overtly before plot characters deducts 500 each time"

## XI. Appendix

- **Maps**: See `maps/` for `WITCH_WM-01.svg`, `WITCH_EM-01.svg`, `WITCH_BM-01.svg`, `WITCH_BM-02.svg`
- **GM Memo**:
  - **Common Blockage 1**: Players unfamiliar with witcher preparation — have Vesemir proactively guide: "In the witcher's world, intel matters as much as the sword."
  - **Common Blockage 2**: Striga too hard — if the party is unprepared, have Geralt appear at a key moment to assist (trade Affinity for plot progress).
  - **Common Blockage 3**: Can't find the Griffin's nest — the curse-lifted girl gives detailed directions, or Vesemir provides a tracking potion (Perception +2 for 1 hour).
  - **Difficulty Up**: Add nighttime Nilfgaardian skirmishers (hostile, Threat Level ★~★★), increase monster count.
  - **Difficulty Down**: Provide +1 free blade oil + potion per monster type; have Geralt give a tactical hint before each battle (+1 DP).
  - **Unconventional Player Actions**: Trying to bypass monsters and skip contracts → more villager deaths, main quest fails. Trying to negotiate with monsters → Intellect (INT) + Socialize, DC4 (Drowners uncommunicative / Striga controllable / Griffin not).
