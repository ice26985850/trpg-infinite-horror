## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The hive world "Revelation's Gate" faces a massive summoning ritual by Chaos cultists. The Reincarnators (輪迴者), dispatched as Inquisition agents, must stop the Chaos ritual within 48 hours — or the entire hive will be dragged into the Warp and become a daemon's hunting ground.

### Full Plot Background

In the 41st millennium of Warhammer 40K, the Imperium of Man barely clings to existence amid countless threats. The hive world "Revelation's Gate" is a gigantic city of tens of billions; its Underhive is the ideal hiding place for Chaos cultists.

The Black Legion — Chaos Space Marines — has infiltrated a squad into the hive's lower depths to assist local cultists in a massive summoning ritual. If the ritual succeeds, a Greater Daemon will be summoned into realspace; the entire planet will be consumed by the Warp, and tens of billions of souls will become offerings to the Chaos Gods.

The Main God grants the Reincarnators the identity of Inquisition Inquisitors, tasked with finding and stopping the summoning ritual within 48 hours. Time is short, resources are limited, and the Underhive environment itself is a terrible enemy.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Free exploration at the open, shifting to act-by-act progression in the mid-to-late game.

### World Special Rules

- **Warp Corruption**: In the Underhive, every 2 hours requires a Resolve + Endurance check (DC 4). Failure gains one Corruption layer (all actions DP-1, stacks). At 5+ Corruption layers the character begins to mutate.
- **Chaos Runes**: Chaos runes in the Underhive curse those who approach. Touching or passing close requires Resolve DC 4; failure deals 2d4 mental damage.
- **Boltgun Rules**: The boltgun is a high-power weapon — damage 2d10 + Strength, +50% extra damage against Chaos units. Usable at most 3× per battle (ammo limit), refillable at the outpost.
- **Emperor's Blessing**: After receiving a clergy blessing at the outpost, saving throws against Chaos units gain +2 for the next 24 hours.

### Main God Restrictions

- Forbidden to trade or compromise with Chaos in any form (by the Emperor's decree)
- Forbidden to use Psyker (psychic) abilities (Warp activity in the hive is already unstable; using psyker powers may attract daemons)
- Forbidden to destroy the hive's main structural pillars (causes massive collapse)
