## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> In the Norse Nine Realms, defeat a fallen Valkyrie and help Kratos and his son find a crucial prophecy before Ragnarök.

### Full Plot Background

The Norse Nine Realms are in turmoil. Odin's corruption spreads to every realm, and the imprisoned Valkyries fall into madness one by one. The Reincarnators arrive shortly after Kratos defeats the first Valkyrie — eight remain, rampaging across the realms.

The Reincarnators must find the entrance to the Valkyrie Council Hall on the icy plains of Midgard, and track the fallen Valkyrie through the realm rifts. Along the way they must cross three realms (Midgard, Alfheim, Helheim), solve Norse rune mechanisms, and face the undead legion corrupted by chaos magic.

The final BOSS is a fallen Valkyrie — but a greater threat lurks in the shadows: Baldur, son of Odin, is hunting Kratos and may intervene in the battle at any time.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: freely explore Midgard and Alfheim to find the Valkyrie → final battle in Helheim.

### Key Locations

#### Location A: Midgard Ice Plain (Start)

The ruins of the Bridge of the Nine Realms tower at the center of the ice plain. The frozen lake reflects an eerie aurora. Kratos's cabin is nearby — a faint wisp of smoke rises from the chimney.

#### Location B: Valkyrie Council Hall

The sealed Council Hall entrance is hidden in the mountain. The massive stone door is carved with Norse runes — eight rune mechanisms must be solved to open it. Freya's enchantment protects the entrance from Odin's gaze.

#### Location C: Helheim Rift (Final)

The fetid air of the land of the dead pours from the fissure. Green ghost-fire lights the path to the Valkyrie's cage. Beneath your feet lie the bones of countless undead warriors — all who in life tried to challenge the mistress here.

### World Special Rules

- **Norse Runes**: Each mechanism requires an INT + Occult check to disable (DC 3–5). Failure triggers a rune trap (random elemental damage).
- **Nine Realms Rift**: The passage between realms is unstable — crossing requires STA DC3, failure deals 1d6 chaos damage.
- **Valkyrie's Curse**: The fallen Valkyrie's attacks carry Odin's curse — those struck must make RES DC4, failure gains "Stun" for 1 round.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Revealing the existence of the Main God Space to plot characters is forbidden."
- "Leaving the Nine Realms (falling into the realm void) is judged as death."
- "If Atreus dies, Kratos will attack all life indiscriminately — including the Reincarnators."
