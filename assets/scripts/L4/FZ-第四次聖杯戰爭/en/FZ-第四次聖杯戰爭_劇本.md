## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators enter the 4th Holy Grail War in Fuyuki City as the 8th Master, surviving the melee among seven original participants and uncovering the truth that the Grail is corrupted by black mud.

### Full Plot Background

The 4th Holy Grail War — seven magi (Masters) summon seven Heroic Spirits (Servants) to fight in Fuyuki City; the final victor gains the omnipotent wish machine — the Holy Grail. However, this war's Grail has been corrupted by Avenger (Angra Mainyu), who failed in the previous war; the Grail's contents have become "All the World's Evils" (the black mud).

The Reincarnators are thrown into this war by the Lord God as an extra 8th Master. This means:
- They must face the seven original participants simultaneously
- They must manage their own Servant (assigned by the Lord God or self-summoned)
- They must survive the melee until only the last 2 factions remain
- They may choose to ally or betray

### Plot Structure Type

**Multi-Team PvP Hybrid**: Multiple factions act simultaneously; the GM must manage several parallel timelines. Each faction has its own strategy and goals.

### World Special Rules

- **Servant Summoning & Control**:
  - The Reincarnators will receive a Servant assigned by the Lord God (recommended class decided by team traits)
  - Command Spells ×3: each can force the Servant to perform one action (no DC check, but permanently consumed on use)
  - Servant death = that faction eliminated (unless the remaining Master can find a new Servant)
- **Noble Phantasm**: The Servant's ultimate ace. Using it consumes large amounts of mana (Master must pass Endurance DC 4 or take 2d6 mana-drain damage). Cannot be used again within 24 hours after use (unless EX-rank).
- **Mana Supply**: Every 24 hours the Master supplies mana to the Servant. If the Master's Endurance drops below 1, the Servant's attributes −2. Can be replenished by spending (every 200 points = temporary mana +1, lasts 24 hours).
- **Spirit Form**: All Servants can become spirit-form (Stealth DC−3 / cannot use physical attacks but can move)

## 八、Multi-Team Competition Rules ⚠️

### This section is the core rules of the multi-team competition script

The essence of the Holy Grail War is multi-faction survival competition. The following rules are for both GM and players:

#### Faction Management

1. **Initial Factions**: Eight Master-Servant pairs (7 original + 1 Reincarnator team).
2. **Faction Status**: Each pair has an independent status — "Active / Allied / Neutral / Hostile / Eliminated".
3. **GM Tracking**: The GM must update faction status at the end of each act and reveal public info to players (e.g. "Caster eliminated"). Hidden actions (like Assassin's clone movements) are not made public.

#### Alliance & Betrayal

1. Any two pairs may ally (Manipulation + Social DC depends on the target).
2. Allies share scout info; Servants will not attack each other.
3. Betrayal (attacking an ally during alliance) permanently damages the betrayer's credibility — future Negotiation DC +2 with any Master.
4. Eventually only 2 pairs (or fewer) may remain — alliances must ultimately break.

#### Command Spell Conflicts

1. Each Master starts with 3 Command Spells.
2. May spend contribution at the Church for extra Command Spells (every 1000 contribution = 1 Command Spell, max 2 extra).
3. Defeating other Masters can seize their unused Command Spells (each Command Spell = value of C1 equivalent).

#### Information War

1. Each Master may scout (Intelligence (INT) + Investigation DC depends on target's precautions) to discover other Masters' bases.
2. After discovering an opponent, may choose: direct attack / ally / leak info to a third party (manipulation).
3. Info leak (revealing one Master's weakness to another) grants DP+1 bonus in the third party's battle.
