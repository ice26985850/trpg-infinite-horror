## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> Sigil — the center of the multiverse, where the possible and the impossible meet. The Reincarnators (輪迴者) awaken here with shattered memories. They must recover the lost memory fragments while fleeing the Lady of Pain — Sigil's guardian — whose sanction against any threat to the city's stability is merciless. She is designed to be unbeatable: escape is the only path to survival.

### Full Plot Background

Sigil is a ring-shaped city floating at the center of the multiverse, ruled by the Lady of Pain. The Lady of Pain is no god — she is older and more unspeakable than the deities. She speaks to no one and accepts no worship, yet anyone who threatens Sigil's stability is cut to pieces by her "Maze," or simply by her shadow.

The Reincarnators awaken in Sigil's "Hive" district with no memory of their quest. They only know they must recover the lost memory fragments — scattered among Sigil's factions and planar rifts.

The script's core is "memory resurgence" — each fragment recovered not only restores part of their memory but reveals that their true identity is far more complex than imagined. And the Lady of Pain is always watching — not interfering, but never tolerating acts that damage the city.

Finally, once all fragments are gathered, the Reincarnators face a painful truth: they themselves were once the entity that threatened Sigil's stability. The Lady's judgment is about to fall — and the only way to survive is to flee Sigil.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Free exploration at the open, shifting to act-by-act progression in the mid-to-late game.

### World Special Rules

- **Memory Fragments**: 7 fragments scattered across Sigil. Collecting a fragment requires an Intellect + Perception DC 3 check to understand its contents. Each fragment understood grants 1 attribute point (permanent, assignable) and part of the quest background.
- **The Laws of the Lady of Pain**: The Lady is not defined as an "enemy" — she will not chase the Reincarnators. But any of the following acts within Sigil trigger her judgment: (1) attack her, (2) attempt to become a god, (3) massively destroy the city's structure. Punishment: cut to pieces by shadow (instant death) or sent into the Maze (permanent imprisonment).
- **Planar Rifts**: Sigil has many rifts to other planes. Entering a rift may yield memory fragments, but each crossing requires a Resolve DC 4 check; failure means being lost in a strange plane (extra time to return).
- **Faction Reputation**: Interactions with factions affect reputation (hostile / neutral / friendly / respected). Faction reputation affects the difficulty of gaining intel and support.

### Main God Restrictions

- Absolutely forbidden to attack the Lady of Pain (instant death, no save)
- Forbidden to massively destroy Sigil's buildings
- Forbidden to attempt to seize the Lady of Pain's power
- Memory fragments are the only quest objective — do not lose yourself in Sigil's infinite diversity
