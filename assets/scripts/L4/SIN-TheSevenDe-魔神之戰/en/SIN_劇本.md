## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> Aid the Seven Deadly Sins against the Ten Commandments and stop the Demon King from fully reviving.

### Full Plot Background

The Kingdom of Britannia — a world of knights and magic. The Demon King — the ultimate being of the ancient era — is reviving in the human world through the Ten Commandments (his ten elite subordinates). The Seven Deadly Sins — the kingdom's seven strongest Holy Knights — are the only force that can match the Commandments.

The Reincarnators (輪迴者) enter at the moment the Ten Commandments begin their large-scale offensive. The Demon King is not yet fully revived — but each time a Commandment member is defeated, the Demon King absorbs their power. You must defeat enough Commandments before the Demon King revives, or gather enough strength to face the complete Demon King head-on.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Freely choose objectives — pick off the Ten Commandments one by one, or rally the allied army for a frontal assault.

## V. World Setting

### Timeline

| T+0 hrs | Awaken on the plains of Britannia — the Seven Deadly Sins are clashing with the Ten Commandments |
| T+3 days | Find the Boar Hat — regroup with Meliodas |
| T+7 days | Defeat at least 1 Commandment — the Demon King begins to wake |
| T+10 days | Rally all forces — final preparations |
| T+14 days | Demon King fully revives — final battle |

### Key Locations

#### Location A: The Boar Hat

Meliodas's mobile tavern — a giant pig-shaped building. This is the Seven Deadly Sins' rally point and the only safe rest stop. Hawk — a talking pig — is the proprietor.

#### Location B: The Ten Commandments' Lair

The Commandments have established strongholds across Britannia. Each is guarded by 1–2 Commandments. Approach requires Perception DC 4 for magic detection.

#### Location C: The Demon King's Sealing Ground

The site of the ancient seal. A gigantic magic array is carved into the earth — the seal is slowly breaking apart. The final battlefield.

### World Special Rules

- **Commandments**: Each of the Ten Commandments bears a "Commandment" — a passive curse aura (radius 50ft). Those who violate it suffer a specific penalty. Commandments stack.
- **The Demon King's Revival**: Each defeated Commandment raises the revival progress by 10%. Revival progress affects the final boss's starting HP.
- **Power of Noon**: Escanor gains +6 to all attributes at noon (11:00–13:00) — the best attack window against the Demon King.

### Main God Restrictions

- "Do not reveal the existence of the Main God Space to story characters."
- "The Ten Commandments' rules apply equally to the Reincarnators."
- "If all members of the Seven Deadly Sins die — the Demon King's revival cannot be stopped."
