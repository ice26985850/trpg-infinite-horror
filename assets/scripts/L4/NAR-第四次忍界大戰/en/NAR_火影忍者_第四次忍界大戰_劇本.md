## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The Fourth Great Ninja War erupts in full. The Reincarnators (輪迴者), fighting as members of the united shinobi army of the Five Great Nations, face Madara Uchiha, Obito, and the Ten-Tails under the banner of the Allied Shinobi Forces. This is a war to slay gods — no single person can stand against it, but what the Reincarnators can do is survive, and turn the tide at the critical moment.

### Full Plot Background

In the timeline of the Naruto world — the Fourth Great Ninja War. Obito Uchiha (posing as Madara Uchiha) declares war on the Five Kage and announces the beginning of the "Eye of the Moon Plan." One hundred thousand White Zetsu and the Edo Tensei reincarnations of history's greatest shinobi rise from the earth, igniting the largest war in the history of the shinobi world.

The Five Great Shinobi Villages (Konoha, Suna, Kumo, Iwa, Kiri) set aside a century of enmity and form the Allied Shinobi Forces — roughly 80,000 shinobi in all. Naruto and Killer Bee (the Eight-Tails Jinchūriki) are isolated on an island belonging to Kumo, the Hidden Cloud Village, out at sea — to keep the Nine-Tails and Eight-Tails out of enemy hands. Kakashi leads the Third Division on the front lines, while Gaara serves as commander of the Allied forces.

The Reincarnators enter at the eve of the war's outbreak — the Allied Shinobi Forces are still mustering troops across the lands of the Five Great Nations. The Main God's quest is: survive the war, and defeat at least 2 members of Akatsuki or 1 Edo Tensei Kage-level fighter.

The core challenges of this script:
- **Large-scale war**: This is not a one-on-one clash of Ninjutsu, but a battlefield of army-scale. Terrain, front lines, and friendly deployment matter just as much.
- **The Chakra system**: The Five-Element Counter (Fire > Wind > Lightning > Earth > Water > Fire) — choosing the right attribute combination is crucial.
- **Edo Tensei**: The summoned dead cannot be killed by conventional means — they must be sealed, which tests Intellect + Occultism.
- **Akatsuki and Madara**: Obito, Madara, the Ten-Tails — these enemies' stats exceed normal ranges. They demand tactics, not brute force.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: The early war (S01–S03) advances act-by-act (muster → coastal defense → Kage-level sealing). After that, depending on the front-line choice, the story switches to the Ten-Tails battlefield (S04–S05). The final decisive battle (S06) is a forced scene.

## V. World Setting

### The Chakra System (World Special Rules)

#### Five-Element Counter

```
Fire (火) ──克──→ Wind (風) ──克──→ Lightning (雷) ──克──→ Earth (土) ──克──→ Water (水) ──克──→ Fire
```

When your attack attribute counters the target's attribute, you gain DP+2 and +1d6 damage. When you are countered, you suffer DP-2 and -1d6 damage.

#### Chakra Pool

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Maximum | Resolve × 3 + 10 |
| Recovery | Recover 5 points at the end of each Scene; meditation (Resolve check, DC 2, requires 10 minutes) recovers a number of points equal to your Resolve attribute |
| Consumption | When casting Ninjutsu, declare the amount consumed — every 2 Chakra points spent raises the Ninjutsu's DP by +1 (max +DP 5, i.e. spending 10 Chakra per cast). Exceeding this limit requires an Endurance DC 4 check — failure deals 1d6 mental-exhaustion damage |

#### Ninjutsu System

| Rank | Base DP | Chakra Cost |
|------|---------|-----------|
| D-Rank (basic Ninjutsu) | DP 5 | 2 points |
| C-Rank (Chūnin-level) | DP 7 | 4 points |
| B-Rank (Jōnin-level) | DP 9 | 6 points |
| A-Rank (forbidden / advanced) | DP 11 | 8 points |
| S-Rank (secret technique) | DP 13 | 12 points |

#### Kekkei Genkai (Bloodline Limits)

Requires specific attribute combinations:
- Ice Release (Wind + Water): can freeze a target for 1 Round (Agility DC 3 to resist)
- Wood Release (Earth + Water): can suppress Tailed-Beast Chakra (DP-3 on checks against Tailed-Beast targets)
- Sharingan (Uchiha clan — unlearnable): DP+2 insight (Perception + Investigation), can copy non-bloodline Ninjutsu (Intellect DC 4)
- Lava Release (Fire + Earth) / Boil Release (Fire + Water) / Magnet Release (Wind + Earth) — resolved by attribute combination

#### Sage Mode

Perception + Resolve check, DC 4. On success, enter Sage Mode: all attribute checks gain DP+2, Chakra ceiling ×2, lasting 5 Rounds. On failure, suffer 2d6 natural-energy backlash and cannot retry for 2 Rounds.

#### Susanoo / Tailed-Beast Form (Enemy Special Ability)

All attribute checks DP×2, but consumes a massive amount of Chakra each Round (10 points / Round). Duration: after 5 Rounds an Endurance DC 4 check is required, or the state ends.

#### Edo Tensei — Can Only Be Sealed, Not Killed

A defeated Edo Tensei target whose Hit Points (HP) drop to 0 does not vanish, but enters a "pending seal" state (immune to all damage). It must be sealed within 2 Rounds:
- Intellect + Occultism check, DC 5, requiring 3 successes (multiple people may participate simultaneously; successes accumulate)
- If sealing is not completed within 2 Rounds, the target revives (HP restored to 50%)
- After a successful seal, the target vanishes permanently

## X. Appendix

### GM Memo

- **Common Stuck Point 1**: Edo Tensei sealing (DC 5 Intellect + Occultism) is extremely hard for characters without the relevant skill — solutions: offer multiple sealing routes: (a) use the ninja tools provided by the main Sealing Squad (DC-2), (b) exploit the Kage-level fighters' mutual attribute counters (against the Second Tsuchikage's Dust Release weakness — Wind Release can disperse the Dust particles), (c) if a Reincarnator has Perception, track down the caster Kabuto Yakushi — defeating the caster directly ends all Edo Tensei.
- **Common Stuck Point 2**: The Ten-Tails and Madara's scale creates a sense of despair — solution: emphasize this is not a battle any single unit can solve. The Reincarnators must rely on Naruto/Sasuke's power, the Allied Ninjutsu Battalion, and the Five Kage's cover — the descriptive focus should be on "your unique role in this battle" rather than "you must solo Madara."
- **Difficulty Up**: Three Kage-level fighters present simultaneously (rather than encountered separately); Ten-Tails Cannon charge time dropped from 1 Round to instant (interrupt DC raised to 80 damage); Six Paths Obito's Truth-Seeking Orbs changed from every 4 Rounds to every 2; all three of Madara's phases appear (Edo Tensei → revival → Six Paths).
- **Difficulty Down**: Kage-level fighters encountered separately (not all 3 at once); Sealing Technique DC lowered to 3; Naruto and Sasuke's combat power +50%; Obito does not use Truth-Seeking Orbs; Madara has no Infinite Tsukuyomi.
- **Players' Unconventional Actions**: If players try to "warn the Allies early that Madara will revive and thoroughly destroy the Outer Path Demon Statue altar" — ruling: the Reincarnators' interference with the plot can indeed affect the timeline, but Kabuto's Black Zetsu will protect the altar. Strength DC 5 or a specific Kekkei Genkai can destroy the core — on success Madara cannot revive (can only maintain Edo Tensei mode), greatly lowering difficulty. This is a creative, high-reward playstyle — encourage it.
- **Chakra Management**: The Reincarnators need to understand attribute counters and Chakra consumption — otherwise they may waste Chakra on wrong attributes. Before combat, offer one chance at a "Chakra attribute test paper" — let each Reincarnator learn their primary attribute.
