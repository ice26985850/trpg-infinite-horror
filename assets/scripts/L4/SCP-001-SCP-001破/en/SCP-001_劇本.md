## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The sunlight melts every creature exposed to it — the Reincarnators (輪迴者) must cross the city in total darkness to reach an underground shelter, evading both the sunlight and the pursuit of deformed flesh-masses.

### Full Plot Background

One morning the sun rises — but this time its light is no longer warm. Any creature exposed to the sunlight melts within minutes into formless masses of flesh (SCP-001-A). These masses, once human, actively hunt survivors to drag them into the sunlight and "assimilate" them. Civilization collapses within hours.

The Reincarnators awaken in the basement of an abandoned building. The Main God requires them to cross the city and reach the underground shelter — Site-19, the only confirmed facility that blocks the SCP-001 effect — within 48 hours. Along the way they must travel through dark tunnels, subways, and sewers, evading sunlight seepage points and the flesh-masses' pursuit.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Free exploration of escape routes + act-by-act triggered flesh-mass encounters.

### World Special Rules

- **Sunlight = Death**: Any exposure to sunlight for 1 Round = 1d10 radiance damage; from the 2nd Round onward, transformation begins (permanent loss of control, character becomes an NPC enemy).
- **Dark Survival**: Moving in total darkness requires Perception DC 3 to orient. Flashlights have a battery limit (consumes 1 cell per hour / 10 cells total).
- **Flesh-Mass Assimilation**: Touch from SCP-001-A stacks assimilation value — each layer of assimilation value = DP-1 + 1d4 mental damage per Round. Accumulating 5 layers = transformation.
