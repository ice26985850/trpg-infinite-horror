## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> Beneath Tingen's thick fog, Beyonder characteristics are spiraling out of control. A Sequence 5 Beyonder — a monster devoured by its own characteristics — is brewing a disaster in the city's sewers. The Reincarnators must find and stop this loss of control, or the entire city will be consumed by Beyonder characteristics.

### Full Plot Background

In the world of *Lord of the Mysteries*, Beyonders advance along specific "Sequence Pathways" by consuming potions. But the higher the Sequence, the greater the risk of losing control — when the Beyonder characteristics overwhelm one's humanity, the Beyonder loses self and becomes an uncontrollable monster.

Tingen is an industrial city in the Kingdom of Loen. Deep within its sewers, an out-of-control Sequence 5 Beyonder — once an Arbiter of the Arbiter Pathway — has been completely consumed by Beyonder characteristics after drinking a contaminated potion during advancement. The "Out-of-Control Beyonder" he has become is unconsciously spreading Beyonder-characteristic pollution, converting ordinary citizens into low-Sequence mad Beyonders.

The Reincarnators' task is to locate and destroy this Out-of-Control Beyonder (or seal his characteristics) with the aid of the "Night Watchers" of the "Goddess of the Night Church," while protecting Tingen's citizens along the way. But this is no ordinary battle — Beyonder-characteristic pollution distorts reality, warps the mind, and can even push the Reincarnators themselves to the brink of losing control.

### Story Structure Type

**Hybrid**: free exploration at the opening, shifting to act-by-act progression in the mid-to-late phase.

### World Special Rules

- **Beyonder-Characteristic Pollution**: During combat with the Out-of-Control Beyonder or its derivatives, each round make a RES+STA contested check (DC equals opponent's level). Failure grants pollution points. Reaching 5 → Minor Loss of Control (occasionally lose action control); 10 → Moderate Loss of Control (random attribute mutation); 15 → Complete Loss of Control (character becomes an enemy NPC).
- **Sanity System**: Witnessing horrific scenes (the Beyonder's transformation, mass civilian conversion, etc.) requires a RES+WIS check (DC 3–6). Failure loses Sanity — similar to pollution but primarily affects the mind. At zero Sanity the character suffers mental collapse.
- **Anti-Pollution Potion**: Obtainable at Tingen University (S-02) or from the Night Watchers. Grants immunity to pollution-point gain for 4 hours after consumption. Note: continuous use builds resistance (second dose's duration is halved).
- **Sealed Artifact**: A special item usable to seal specific abilities of the Out-of-Control Beyonder. Each has a limited number of uses (1–3). Using it requires INT+Occult DC4.

### Main God Restrictions

- Forbid displaying obvious Beyonder abilities before citizens (triggers mass panic)
- Forbid letting the Out-of-Control Beyonder's pollution spread to the surface (failure if over 100 citizens infected)
- Beyonder-characteristic pollution cannot be purified by ordinary means — the source must be destroyed
