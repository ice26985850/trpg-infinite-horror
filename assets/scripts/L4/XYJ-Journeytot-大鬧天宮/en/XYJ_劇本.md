## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The Monkey King Sun Wukong is wreaking Havoc in Heaven. The Reincarnators' (輪迴者) task is to aid the Monkey King against the Heavenly Court's suppression army — fighting from the Southern Heaven Gate all the way to the Hall of Miraculous Mist. But the Buddha's final intervention will change everything.

### Full Plot Background

The mythic age of Journey to the West. Sun Wukong — the Monkey King of Flower-Fruit Mountain, the Great Sage Equal to Heaven — has single-handedly thrown Heaven into uproar, outraged by the Heavenly Court's contempt for him.

The Main God drops the Reincarnators into this mythic moment, charged with helping Sun Wukong. The Heavenly Court responds swiftly and fiercely: heavenly soldiers, the Four Heavenly Kings, and Erlang Shen Yang Jian — each wave stronger than the last.

But the most fearsome opponent has not yet appeared — the Buddha. When the Heavenly Court cannot stop Sun Wukong, the Buddha will act personally. The Reincarnators must, in this unwinnable battle, help Wukong delay as long as possible, protect the monkey offspring of Flower-Fruit Mountain, and make a crucial choice when the Buddha appears.

The true theme of this script is not "winning the battle" — but "leaving a mark upon the myth."

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Free exploration at the open, shifting to act-by-act progression in the mid-to-late game.

### World Special Rules

- **Sun Wukong's Help**: Wukong is the strongest character in the script — but he is also facing a huge number of enemies. The Reincarnators may request Wukong's help in a specific scene (requires Charisma DC 4), but each time Wukong is summoned, the heavenly soldiers elsewhere advance faster.
- **Somersault Cloud**: Wukong can provide Somersault Cloud fragments for the Reincarnators to fly briefly. But the cloud requires practice (Agility DC 3 check) to control freely. Failure means falling (1d6 fall damage).
- **Merit Value**: Aiding Wukong, protecting the monkeys, and defeating heavenly soldiers earns Merit Value. Merit Value can be spent on Wukong's special teachings (temporary buffs) or summoning monkey-soldier support.

### Main God Restrictions

- Forbidden to harm the monkey offspring of Flower-Fruit Mountain
- Forbidden to steal Sun Wukong's Ruyi Jingu Bang (any attempt gets you slain by one strike)
- Forbidden to kill mortals (heavenly soldiers are not mortal and may be killed)
- The Buddha cannot be defeated — the battle against the Buddha has survival as its only objective
