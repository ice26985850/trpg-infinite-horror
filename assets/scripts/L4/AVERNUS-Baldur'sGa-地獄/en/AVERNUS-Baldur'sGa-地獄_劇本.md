## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> Baldur's Gate has been dragged into Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells. The Reincarnators must find their way back to the Material Plane while fleeing the relentless pursuit of the devil armies.

### Full Plot Background

Baldur's Gate — one of the greatest cities of Faerûn — was torn into Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells, during a great catastrophe. The city now floats beneath the blood-red skies of Hell, surrounded by the boiling River Styx and endless legions of devils. The culprit is the fallen angel Archduke Zariel, who seeks to transform the entire city into a forward base for the Blood War.

The Reincarnators awaken in the city's Temple District. The Lord God informs them: in 72 hours the city will be fully dragged into the depths of Hell, and all life will be transformed into devils by the infernal energy. They must reach the portal at the city's edge (the only exit to the Material Plane) and escape successfully before then, all while fleeing Zariel's pursuit.

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Phase One (exploration within the city) is free-form exploration — gathering resources and finding allies across the city's districts. Phase Two (escape from the city) is scene-by-scene progression as the devil armies launch their full assault.

### World Special Rules

- **Infernal Energy**: Except within the temple ward, all other city districts require an Endurance check DC3 every hour; failure grants 1 layer of Infernal Corruption (DP-1 per layer, max 5 layers)
- **Devil Soul Coins**: Killing devils yields soul coins (roll 1d4 for quantity), usable in S-02. 3 coins = 1 transaction
- **Holy Weapons**: The Temple District can provide holy water (vs devils +2d6 radiant damage, single use)

### Lord God's Restrictions

- "Do not reveal the existence of the Lord God's Space to plot characters"
- "Leaving the city boundary (falling into the abyss) is judged as death"
- "If the ward core is destroyed, all Reincarnators immediately gain 3 layers of Infernal Corruption"
