## II. Plot Summary (GM Spoilers)

### One-Line Summary

> The Nine Dragons Pull Coffin descends upon Earth and carries the Reincarnators (輪迴者) into the Beidou Star Region. In this cultivation world where the strong rule, they must survive the scramble among the great Holy Lands and seize the fragments of an Emperor Weapon — the only key back to Earth.

### Full Plot Background

The world of *Shrouding the Heavens* is a vast cultivation cosmos. The Nine Dragons Pull Coffin — a bronze ancient coffin dragged by nine giant dragons — falls from the heavens and brings the Reincarnators (輪迴者) to the Beidou Star Region. This is a world where cultivation civilization has reached its zenith; the great Holy Lands hold fragments of ancient Emperor Weapons (weapons of Great-Emperor rank).

Emperor Weapon fragments lie scattered across the Beidou Star Region, each containing the distilled essence of a Great-Emperor-level being's power. Once the Reincarnators (輪迴者) are dropped in by the Main God, they discover there is only one way home: gather three Emperor Weapon fragments and, when the Nine Dragons Coffin reappears, activate its spatial-transport function.

But the disciples of the Holy Lands are hunting these fragments too. The Taixu Holy Land, the Tianxuan Holy Land, the Ancient Families — peerless prodigies with attributes of 10+ treat the Reincarnators (輪迴者) as rival plunderers. Worse still, certain Great-Power-level existences (attributes 15+) take interest in these "visitors from beyond the heavens."

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: free exploration at the opening, shifting to scene-by-scene progression in the mid-to-late game.

### World Special Rules

- **Spirit-Energy Cultivation**: In the Beidou Star Region, you may make one cultivation check per day (Intellect or Resolve, DC3). On success, gain 1 temporary attribute point (freely allocated, lasts until the script ends, cap +3).
- **Emperor Weapon Fragment Resonance**: While holding a fragment, you can sense the rough direction of other fragments within a 50 km radius. Multiple fragments resonate with one another.
- **Holy Land Reputation**: Each Holy Land has an independent reputation value (Hostile – Neutral – Friendly – Revered). Reputation affects combat difficulty and how easily you obtain intel. Killing Holy Land disciples lowers that land's reputation.

### Main God Restrictions

- Forbidden to display combat power beyond the Golden Core stage before mortals (it draws heavenly retribution)
- Emperor Weapon fragments cannot be destroyed — only seized or traded
- Forbidden to damage the Nine Dragons Pull Coffin (the only means of return)
