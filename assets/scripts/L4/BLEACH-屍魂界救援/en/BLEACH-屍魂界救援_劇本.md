## 二、Plot Summary (GM Only)

### One-Line Summary

> The Reincarnators enter the Soul Society, assist Ichigo Kurosaki's group in infiltrating the Seireitei, rescue Rukia Kuchiki from the pursuit of the Gotei 13's captain-level shinigami, and stop the execution at the Sōkyoku Hill.

### Full Plot Background

The world's timeline is set during the Soul Society Arc. Rukia Kuchiki was arrested by the Gotei 13 for transferring Shinigami powers to a human (Ichigo Kurosaki), and has been sentenced to execution by the Sōkyoku. The Reincarnators enter at the moment when Kisuke Urahara is training Ichigo's group — only **72 hours** remain until Rukia's execution date.

The Reincarnators must depart from Urahara Shop, infiltrate the Soul Society through the Senkaimon, cross the Rukongai, break through the Seireitei's defenses, and finally reach Sōkyoku Hill. Along the way they will face layered resistance ranging from ordinary shinigami to captain-level opponents.

The Gotei 13 become an "institutional antagonistic force" in this script — most captains are merely following orders, not inherently evil. The GM must note: characters like Kenpachi Zaraki have the potential to turn "foe or friend."

### Plot Structure Type

**Hybrid**: Act I (training/preparation phase) is semi-free exploration; Act II (Seireitei infiltration) is scene-by-scene progression.

### World Special Rules

- **Spiritual Power System**: A Reincarnator's spiritual power = Resolve+Perception check DP. All Shinigami-related checks gain a spiritual-power bonus. Non-Shinigami Reincarnators start with lower spiritual power but can raise it via S-03 Zanpakutō training.
- **Zanpakutō Rules**:
  - **Shikai**: DP+3, with an elemental effect determined by character attribute (INT=Kido-type / STR=direct-attack-type / WIS=perception-type / AGI=speed-type)
  - **Bankai**: DP×2, but after 5 rounds requires an Endurance DC4 check, else suffer 1d6 fatigue damage per round. Captain-level NPCs release Bankai when HP < 30%.
- **Kido System**: INT + Occult check.
  - Hadō (attack): DP by number (1-10 = DP 5, 11-30 = DP 7, 31-60 = DP 9, 60+ = DP 11)
  - Bakudō (control): DC by number (same as above)
- **Shunpo**: AGI + Athletics DC2 check; success multiplies movement ×3 that round (Yoruichi-level is ×5–×10)
- **Reiatsu Sense**: Perception check DC2 (ordinary shinigami) to DC5 (captain-level); success detects surrounding reiatsu sources

### Lord God's Restrictions

- "Do not reveal the existence of the Lord God's Space to plot characters"
- "Zanpakutō abilities gained by Reincarnators are only valid in this world; after return they convert to equivalent or related enhancements"
- "If you kill a non-hostile irrelevant shinigami (not self-defense), deduct 1000 per person"
